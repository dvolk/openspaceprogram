# Proximity (flying regime): the active ship is in orbit (inertial-orbit)
# and a neighbour sits 100 m away in the same orbit-slot group. With the
# default fly radii (2 km in / 10 km out) the neighbour is well inside the
# engage radius, so it leaves the rails (ENGAGED, fly regime). Warping up
# (PERIOD) while it is engaged must be capped back to the encounter warp
# (default 1x) -- a close approach can't stay in warp.
NAME prox-fly
ARGS --fleet e2e/cases/fleet_fly.json --time-accel 1 --timeout 6 --prox-log
ARGS --sim-press 1500,0,PERIOD
EXPECT [prox]
EXPECT ENGAGED
EXPECT , fly)
EXPECT warp CAPPED
FORBID , ground)
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
