# "Resume Flight" from the Space Center hub: the menu's button and Esc both
# pop (hubKeyActions), so Esc drives the same transition headless. The hub is
# a live view of the running sim (the ship coasts beneath it) -- the pop must
# hand the parked camera back to the flight and let the ship keep coasting
# (orbit still advancing at the end).
NAME spacecenter-back
ARGS --ship res/ships/racer.json --scenario rot-orbit --time-accel 1 --timeout 10
ARGS --space-center 1000
ARGS --sim-press 2000,0,ESCAPE
EXPECT [scene] flight -> spacecenter (push)
EXPECT [scene] spacecenter -> flight (pop)
EXPECT Timeout reached (10.0 s)
FORBID GL_
