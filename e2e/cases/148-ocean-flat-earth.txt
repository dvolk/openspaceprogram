# Earth's sea renders flat ("surface.ocean": "flat" in the system JSON).
# The ocean shell is a 128-ring UV sphere, so its flat faces sit ~radius/13300
# below the sea-level sphere: ~45 m on Kerbin, invisible, but ~480 m on Earth,
# where the sea floor sticks out above the water. Earth opts out and paints its
# sea on a floor clamped to sea_level instead (terragen.h OceanMode).
#
# The dossier reports both the authored mode and the shell the loader actually
# built, so this pins the effect and not just the data: a BuildOcean guard that
# regressed and handed Earth a shell too would fail here. The two modes' math is
# pinned in test_terrain.cpp.
NAME ocean-flat-earth
ARGS --system res/systems/solar_system.json --timeout 6 --atlas-dump 4000
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
# Earth's own dossier block: the "seas" fact sits a dozen lines under the body
# header, so match from the header with a non-greedy span.
CHECK re.search(r"\[atlas\] Earth weight=.*?seas\s+yes, \+0 m, flat sea", out, re.S)
# Earth is the only sea body in the shipped Solar System, so a flat sea means
# the whole system renders without a single ocean shell.
CHECK "ocean shell" not in out
