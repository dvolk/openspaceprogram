NAME eva-spawn
CLAIM EVA spawns the roster's lowest-id kerbal at the capsule.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 9 --eva-log --orbit-interval 0.3 --sim-press 1000,0,V,2500,2000,W --frame-cap 30 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
# The kerbal is named from the shipped roster (res/data/kerbals.json): the
# lowest id is the first one a seat claims, so 'Ash' is id 1.
EXPECT Crew: 'Ash' aboard 'racer' part 0 (1/1)
EXPECT EVA: 'Ash' out of 'racer' part 0
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2: Ash
FORBID error:
CHECK len(eva) > 5
CHECK max((e["vel"][0]**2 + e["vel"][1]**2 + e["vel"][2]**2)**0.5 for e in eva) > 1.5
