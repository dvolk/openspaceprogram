# THE SHIP LIST'S x IS THE THIRD HOLE (#1162 review round). 1162-a closed the proximity
# pass and 1162-b closed the ship switch; the review found the verb nobody had looked at:
# Game::ui_remove_vehicle deleted a hull a kerbal was hanging on, and she dropped exactly as
# she did before the fix -- measured with the guard absent:
#
#   Removed 'heavy_asp' (active unchanged: Ash)
#   [evalog] t=4.0s mode=space alt=28.99 m ladder=0 vel=[0.00 -0.00 -9.70]
#   [evalog] t=5.0s mode=space alt=14.46 m ladder=0 vel=[0.00 -0.00 -19.29]
#   [evalog] t=6.0s mode=ground alt= 5.44 m
#
# With the guard:
#
#   [toast] Cannot remove heavy_asp -- a kerbal is hanging on its ladder
#
# That is the same policy the verb already runs on crew: a hull with anyone aboard is
# refused ("EVA its crew out first"), and a kerbal on a ladder is aboard in every way that
# matters -- her pose is that hull's pose. cullFarDebris gets the same question (it skips
# EVA hulls, but a hung-on booster is exactly the population it cuts loose).
#
# --ui-click rather than --sim-mouse: it hit-tests through imgui, so a renamed or hidden
# control fails the case instead of clicking empty space (src/uiinput.cpp).
NAME remove-a-hull-a-suit-hangs-on-refused
CLAIM Clicking remove on a hull that a kerbal is hanging on is refused and the kerbal keeps hanging (#1162).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --sim-steps 600 --startship heavy_asp,res/ships/heavy_asp.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --eva-log --prox-log --no-paint --sim-press 1000,0,V
ARGS --ui-click 1500,"Windows/Ship List" --ui-click 3000,"Ship List/x##rm_heavy_asp"
LIMIT 20
EXPECT [toast] Cannot remove heavy_asp -- a kerbal is hanging on its ladder
FORBID error:
FORBID Removed 'heavy_asp'
FORBID frozen on rails
CHECK len(eva) >= 6
CHECK all(e["mode"] == "ladder" and e["ladder"] != 0 for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0)
CHECK min(e["alt"] for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0) > 33.0
# The hull is still there after the refused press, at the same hold point through t=9.
CHECK max(e["t"] for e in eva) >= 9.0
CHECK all("heavy_asp" not in l or "LIVE" in l for l in out.splitlines() if l.startswith("[prox]  "))
