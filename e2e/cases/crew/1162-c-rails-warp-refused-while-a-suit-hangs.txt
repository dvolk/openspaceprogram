# RAILS WARP WHILE A SUIT HANGS (#1162, the player-facing half of Kerbal::canRail).
# Kerbal::canRail refuses the rails while ladderUid != 0, so a hanging suit vetoes
# rails warp for the whole fleet -- and the refusal has to say which of the two reasons
# it is. Measured on the pad, suit out at t=1.0, warp dial tapped at t=3.0 and 3.6:
#
#   [toast] Time accel: 10x                                  <- 10 is under kRailsWarp
#   Rails warp refused: 'Ash' is on a ladder (warp stays 10)
#   [toast] Rails warp refused: 'Ash' is on a ladder
#
# Before the split both sites said "is in the atmosphere" -- for a kerbal standing on a
# hatch ladder 34 m up, a reason that sends the player looking for a flight problem that
# is not there. main.cpp's start-of-run clamp (a --load at --time-accel 100) uses the same
# Game::railRefusalReason() door; only this one is pinned here.
#
# The veto is the cost of the fix and is ticketed as #1165. What this case pins is that
# the veto is honest and that the hang survives the long 10x stretch after it: the suit is
# still on part 9 at 33.88 m at t=63.2, the last row 600 steps buys at that rate.
NAME rails-warp-refused-while-a-suit-hangs
CLAIM Asking for rails warp while a kerbal hangs on a ladder is refused, and the refusal says "on a ladder" (#1162).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --sim-steps 600 --startship heavy_asp,res/ships/heavy_asp.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --eva-log --prox-log --no-paint --sim-press 1000,0,V
ARGS --sim-press 3000,0,PERIOD --sim-press 3600,0,PERIOD
LIMIT 20
EXPECT [toast] Time accel: 10x
EXPECT Rails warp refused: 'Ash' is on a ladder (warp stays 10)
EXPECT [toast] Rails warp refused: 'Ash' is on a ladder
FORBID error:
FORBID is in the atmosphere
FORBID frozen on rails
CHECK len(eva) >= 8
CHECK all(e["mode"] == "ladder" and e["ladder"] != 0 for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0)
CHECK min(e["alt"] for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0) > 33.0
# The run went on at 10x with the pair live (measured last row t=63.2 s), so this is not a
# refusal that quietly ended the flight.
CHECK max(e["t"] for e in eva) > 50.0
