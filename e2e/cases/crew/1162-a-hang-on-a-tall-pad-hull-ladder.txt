# HANGING ON A TALL HULL'S LADDER (#1162). heavy_asp on the pad is the one shipped hull
# where stepping out dropped the kerbal: it grabbed the capsule's hatch ladder at t=1.0 and
# by the next logged row (t=2.0) was in mode=space falling at 9.84 m/s, landing on one of
# its own stages 11.7 m below.
#
# Nothing about the ladder was wrong. The prox pass decided the hull was far away:
#
#   [prox] t=1.017 heavy_asp RELEASED at 20.2 m (> 20.0 m, ground)
#   @@@ heavy_asp frozen on rails (grounded around Kerbin)
#
# -- because Vehicle::gapTo is CENTRE-to-centre. heavy_asp is tall enough that a kerbal on
# its top hatch is 20.1 m from the stack's COM at all times, so the ground release radius
# (20 m) parks the hull out from under the suit, and resolveLadder() refuses a grip on an
# on-rails hull (by design, #1073: a railed ship's pose is a frozen park and the hang law
# reads its parts per substep). The grip dies, the suit falls. racer on the same pad measures
# 2.2 m and never trips it; heavy_asp in ORBIT uses the wide fly radii and never trips it
# either -- which is exactly the "works fine everywhere else" of the report.
#
# The fix is not a bigger radius (a stack can be arbitrarily tall) but the fact the pass was
# blind to: a hull with a suit hanging on it is not "away". Game::anySuitHangsOn() reads the
# grip through its DURABLE half -- the ladder part's uid -- because the cached part pointer
# is not durable: applyLadder and disengageLadder clear it whenever the suit stops ticking
# (a ship switch, #734) or the law re-aims, while the uid survives a park and only needs the
# save's uid map on the load path. select_vehicle() asks the same question, because switching
# ships used to park the hull the suit was left on and drop it the same way (eva.h's disarm
# note says a kerbal left on a ladder hangs there while the player flies elsewhere -- that
# promise needs a live hull to keep).
#
# --sim-steps rather than --timeout: a fall is the most timing-sensitive thing in this family
# (#138, #871), and under --sim-steps the --sim-press schedule runs on the SIM clock
# (Game::schedClockMs, #472), so V lands at t=1.0 exactly on any box. 2 s of wall time.
NAME hang-on-a-tall-pad-hull-ladder
CLAIM Stepping out onto the top-hatch ladder of a hull taller than the ground release radius keeps the kerbal hanging there: the proximity pass may not park a hull a suit is on (#1162).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --sim-steps 600 --startship heavy_asp,res/ships/heavy_asp.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --eva-log --prox-log --no-paint --sim-press 1000,0,V
LIMIT 20
EXPECT EVA: 'Ash' out of 'heavy_asp' part 0
EXPECT [prox]
FORBID error:
FORBID RELEASED
FORBID frozen on rails
CHECK len(eva) >= 5
# The grip is real, not a free-fall that happens to be pointed at the hull.
CHECK all(e["mode"] == "ladder" and e["ladder"] != 0 for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0)
# And it stays at the hatch. With the guard neutered this same command reads alt 28.90 m at
# t=2.0 and 22.19 m at t=3.0 (grounded, on its own stage); the hold sits at 33.88 m through
# the whole run, so a fall of even a metre reddens this. Measured alt values in a passing
# run: 33.92 at t=1.0 (the grab row) and 33.88 at every row after -- so the spread over the
# t > 1.0 rows this CHECK reads is 0.00 m, and 0.04 m over all rows.
CHECK min(e["alt"] for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0) > 33.0
CHECK max(e["alt"] for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0) - min(e["alt"] for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.0) < 0.25
# Velocity, because alt alone could be read by a suit that slid down and stopped: the hang is
# still (the servo holds it), not parked at the bottom of a slide.
CHECK all(max(abs(c) for c in e["vel"]) < 0.5 for e in eva if e["t"] > 1.5)
# The neighbour line is the pass's own record: heavy_asp sits at 20.1 m -- past the 20 m
# ground release radius -- and the pass left it LIVE. That is the fix, in the log the pass
# prints for itself.
CHECK any("heavy_asp" in l and "LIVE" in l for l in out.splitlines())
CHECK all("heavy_asp" not in l or "LIVE" in l for l in out.splitlines() if l.startswith("[prox]  "))
