# HANGING THROUGH A SHIP SWITCH (#1162, the other half of 1162-a). Stepping out of
# heavy_asp onto its hatch ladder and then flying the hull -- F6/']', Slot::NextShip --
# took the walking kerbal out of the world while she was still gripping part 9:
#
#   @@@ Ash frozen on rails (grounded around Kerbin)
#   [prox]   Ash dist=20.1 m rate=-0.0 m/s alt=289 m on-rails
#
# isGrounded() calls a hang 34 m up "grounded around Kerbin" (the analytic terrain band is
# generous), so the switch's own ship->goOnRails() (select_vehicle, game.cpp:1738) parks the
# suit it is leaving. Her pose then sits frozen in mid-air with no law running on it --
# railsTick has no ladder law -- and resolveLadder() (#1073) refuses a grip on a railed hull,
# so the hang only comes back if something wakes her. Switch away and straight back and she
# re-grips; leave her out there and the hull parks too, which is 1162-a's fall.
#
# The gate is Kerbal::canRail: a hanging suit has no park of its own -- its pose IS the
# hull it grips -- so it refuses the rails while ladderUid != 0. One gate covers every park
# site (the proximity pass, select_vehicle, the start-of-run warp clamp, enter_rails_warp,
# the save commit) instead of a guard at each. 1162-a covers the twin problem on the other
# side: a hull with a suit on it must not park either -- and it is the twin that bites here,
# because with ONLY the hull guard removed this same command drops her: the switch parks the
# hull, resolveLadder() takes the grip, and the evalog goes
#
#   [evalog] t=4.0s mode=space alt=34.12 m ladder=0 vel=[0.00 -0.00 -9.84]
#
# The park that freezes her is the switch itself -- game.cpp:1738 parks the ship you switch
# TO, whatever its neighbours are; the 2.2 m between racer and heavy_asp only matters later,
# as the reason the proximity pass wakes the hull again.
#
# --sim-steps makes the whole thing step-identical (#472): the key schedule runs on the
# SIM clock there (Game::schedClockMs), so the presses land at t=1.0/3.0/5.5 exactly, on
# any box, and the run is 2.6 s of wall time instead of 15.
NAME hang-through-a-ship-switch
CLAIM Flying the hull while a kerbal is left on its ladder keeps the kerbal hanging there: a gripping suit may not go on rails (#1162).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --sim-steps 900 --startship heavy_asp,res/ships/heavy_asp.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --eva-log --prox-log --no-paint --sim-press 1000,0,V
ARGS --sim-press 3000,0,RIGHTBRACKET --sim-press 5500,0,RIGHTBRACKET
LIMIT 25
EXPECT EVA: 'Ash' out of 'heavy_asp' part 0
# Both switches happened: out to the hull and back to the suit.
EXPECT Active ship 1 of 2: heavy_asp
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2: Ash
FORBID error:
FORBID frozen on rails
FORBID RELEASED
CHECK len(eva) >= 8
# The pass's own record: while the suit was the hull nobody was flying it stayed LIVE.
# The unfixed run prints the same field as "on-rails".
CHECK any("Ash" in l and "LIVE" in l for l in out.splitlines() if l.startswith("[prox]   "))
# Back on the suit it is still hanging on the same ladder part, at the hatch, not on the
# ground and not fallen. Measured rows: alt 33.88 m and ladder=9 at every t after the
# switch back.
CHECK all(e["mode"] == "ladder" and e["ladder"] != 0 for e in eva if e["t"] >= 6.0)
CHECK min(e["alt"] for e in eva if e["t"] >= 6.0) > 33.0
CHECK max(e["alt"] for e in eva) - min(e["alt"] for e in eva) < 0.25
# Still still after the round trip: the disarm a switched-away suit gets zeroes the climb
# demand but keeps the grip, so the hang holds rather than sliding.
CHECK all(max(abs(c) for c in e["vel"]) < 0.5 for e in eva if e["t"] >= 7.0)
