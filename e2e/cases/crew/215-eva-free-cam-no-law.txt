NAME eva-free-cam-no-law
CLAIM Free cam over an orbital kerbal leaves it coasting: the suit does not claim to be standing, and nothing brakes the orbit.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --eva-log --orbit-log --orbit-interval 0.5 --frame-cap 30 --free-cam-pos 0 0 700000 --startship kerbal,res/ships/kerbal.json,Kerbin,inertial-orbit
# #215. Free cam arms nothing ("a flier: no sticks"), but the suit's laws still run per
# substep, so before the fix this kerbal wore the constructor's default -- mode=ground,
# grounded=1 at 127 km -- and the walk law's brake pulled 10 m/s^2 out of a 2.2 km/s
# orbit: ecc 0.0033 -> 0.069 and sma 725,089 -> 680,473 m in 7.7 s, with nobody at the
# controls. disarm() gives a suit no arm ran a neutral tick and re-derives the regime.
EXPECT Spawn 'inertial-orbit' around Kerbin
EXPECT mode=space grounded=0
FORBID mode=ground
CHECK len(eva) > 5
# The two physical CHECKs walk `orbit`, and an empty all() is a note that cannot redden
# a case, so the channel itself needs a guard.
CHECK len(orbit) > 5
CHECK all(e["mode"] == "space" for e in eva)
CHECK all(o["ecc"] < 0.001 for o in orbit if o["t"] > 1.0)
CHECK all(o["v"] > 2200.0 for o in orbit if o["t"] > 1.0)
