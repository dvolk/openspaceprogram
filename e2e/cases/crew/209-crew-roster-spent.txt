# A fixed pool with more seats than people: the first capsule claims the only
# kerbal, the second seat stays EMPTY and the ship flies uncrewed. That is a
# legal state, not a failure -- the run must reach its timeout clean.
NAME crew-roster-spent
CLAIM A pool with more seats than people leaves the extra seats empty.
KIND invariant
ARGS --kerbals e2e/fixtures/tiny_kerbals.json --time-accel 1 --timeout 6
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --startship racer2,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
EXPECT Roster: 1 kerbal(s) from e2e/fixtures/tiny_kerbals.json
EXPECT Crew: 'Solo' aboard 'racer' part 0 (1/1)
EXPECT Crew: no kerbal left for 'racer2' part 0 (1 seats)
EXPECT Timeout reached
FORBID error:
LIMIT 60
