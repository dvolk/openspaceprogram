# The same rule case 225 pins at boot, on the path a player actually reaches it:
# the VAB lets you bolt parts onto a kerbal part, so LAUNCH has to refuse rather
# than place a two-part "person" (which the save writer would silently shrink to
# one part, and boarding would abort on). A toast, not a crash, and the editor
# stays open with the build intact.
NAME vab-launch-kerbal-multidef
CLAIM The VAB refuses to launch a multi-part kerbal with a toast.
KIND invariant
ARGS --vab e2e/fixtures/kerbal_rover.json --ui-click 3000,"VAB TopBar/LAUNCH" --timeout 6
EXPECT [vab] entered the editor (sim keeps coasting)
EXPECT [vab] launch refused: 'kerbalrover' is kerbal-rooted but has 2 parts
EXPECT [toast] Launch refused: a staff member must be a single part
FORBID [vab] launched
FORBID error:
LIMIT 60
