NAME reload-refused-science
# A refused reload must not clobber the running career science (issue #133).
# Boot loads a save carrying score 1 / 1 recovered, then a refused reload of a
# score-0 save (a part no longer in the catalog) must leave that science
# untouched -- the fleet rolls back, and so must the career score + log.
ARGS --load e2e/fixtures/save_science --time-accel 1 --timeout 8
ARGS --reload e2e/fixtures/save_corrupt --reload-at 3000
EXPECT Loaded game from e2e/fixtures/save_science (active: racer)
EXPECT [load] refused e2e/fixtures/save_corrupt
EXPECT no_such_part_in_the_catalog
EXPECT [load] science score=1 recovered=1 (unchanged)
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
