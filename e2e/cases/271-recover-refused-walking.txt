# Recovering while a crew member walks: the hub now offers the button and
# recoverActive refuses -- the same shape as 106 (in orbit) and 108 (wrong body).
#
# It used to hide the button for a walker while SHOWING it in those refuse-only
# states, so this case had to drive Game::recoverActive through --recover to
# reach the refusal at all (#316). One gate now: the button is drawn whenever
# there is an active ship, and the refusal lives where the rule lives.
NAME recover-refused-walking
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 9 --width 1920 --height 1080
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --ui-focus 1000,racer/capsule_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 1100,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-click 1500,"racer > Capsule (2m)##0/EVA##eva0"
ARGS --sim-press 2500,0,ESCAPE
ARGS --ui-click 3500,"Game Menu/Recover Vessel"
EXPECT [scene] flight -> spacecenter (push)
EXPECT [toast] Cannot recover
EXPECT a crew member can't be recovered
# The hub is open and the walker is active: the button is drawn, and pressing
# it is the whole subject. The CHECK pins THAT rather than a claim in this
# comment: --ui-click's success line carries label="Recover Vessel", and a
# missing button fails the click without printing it.
CHECK 'label="Recover Vessel"' in out
# The aim and the EVA click both have to land for the walker to exist at all;
# without this, a missed click shows up as the FORBID below rather than as the
# click that missed.
FORBID FAILED
# The refusal is the whole point: no flight ends here.
FORBID [recover] t=
FORBID [science] recovered
EXPECT Timeout reached (9.
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
