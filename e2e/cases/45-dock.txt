# Docking is intent-driven: Game::updateDocking mates the active ship only
# when it has BOTH an armed port of its own (Vehicle::dockArmPort, in-game
# "Arm for docking") AND a targeted port on the other ship (Vehicle::dockTarget*,
# in-game "Target for docking"). --dock-test near builds a station and a probe
# co-moving with their port faces already inside the 1.5 m capture window, and
# the builder sets both halves on the probe -- the test equivalent of the two
# right-clicks. On the first live tick the probe (active) absorbs the station:
# one [dock] line naming both ships.
NAME dock
ARGS --dock-test near --time-accel 1 --timeout 3
EXPECT Dock-test (near)
EXPECT [dock] t=
EXPECT a="dockprobe" b="dockstation"
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
