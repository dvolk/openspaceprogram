# Rails warp: from time-accel 10, one PERIOD enters rails warp (->100,
# ships coast) and one COMMA exits (->10, physics resumes). Both the entry
# and exit print lines must appear. Catches a regression in the warp
# enter/leave state machine.
#
# The assertions are the state machine's own prints (goOnRails / leaveRails in
# vehicle.cpp) rather than the warp toasts: they prove the ship actually parked
# on its conic and came back into the physics world, which is the transition
# this case is about.
NAME rails-warp
ARGS --scenario rot-orbit --ship res/ships/racer.json --time-accel 10
ARGS --timeout 10
ARGS --sim-press 1500,50,PERIOD
ARGS --sim-press 2500,50,COMMA
EXPECT @@@ racer parked on rails around Kerbin
EXPECT @@@ racer left the rails around Kerbin
EXPECT [toast] Time accel: 100x (rails)
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
