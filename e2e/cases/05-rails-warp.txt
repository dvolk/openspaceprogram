# Rails warp: from time-accel 10, one PERIOD enters rails warp (->100,
# ships coast) and one COMMA exits (->10, physics resumes). Both the entry
# and exit print lines must appear. Catches a regression in the warp
# enter/leave state machine.
NAME rails-warp
ARGS --scenario rot-orbit --ship res/ships/racer.json --time-accel 10
ARGS --timeout 10
ARGS --sim-press 1500,50,PERIOD
ARGS --sim-press 2500,50,COMMA
EXPECT Rails warp: time accel 100
EXPECT Rails warp: exited
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
