# Esc at the Tracking Station is the station's own main menu
# (trackingKeyActions, Slot::Menu like flight's pause menu), not a pop to
# the Space Center hub (the old hubKeyActions behavior). The two presses
# open then close the menu (each logs); the hub pop must never come. (The
# "Back to Space Center" path itself is case 69.)
NAME tracking-esc-menu
ARGS --ship res/ships/racer.json --scenario rot-orbit --time-accel 1 --timeout 10
ARGS --space-center 1000 --tracking 2000 --sim-press 3000,0,ESCAPE --sim-press 4000,0,ESCAPE
EXPECT [scene] flight -> spacecenter (push)
EXPECT [scene] spacecenter -> tracking (push)
EXPECT Tracking menu: open
EXPECT Tracking menu: closed
EXPECT Timeout reached (10.0 s)
FORBID [scene] tracking -> spacecenter (pop)
FORBID GL_
