# Pocket fuel: a free kerbal whose SUIT tank is empty but whose pocket holds a
# full mono tank of hydrazine holds W. The suit cannot draw (empty), so the
# ONLY way the RCS thrusts is the phase-4.5 pocket draw -- the quality pass
# found consumeResourceMass could never reach the pocket tanks (they are not in
# the kerbal's fuel groups), leaving a kerbal with an empty suit and a full
# pocket permanently unable to thrust. Here the mass must fall as the pocket
# tank is burned (the pocket draw sheds its mass).
#
# The time warp comes from the fixture (save_inventory/save.json time_accel 10;
# a --load keeps the save's warp, so the CLI --time-accel would be ignored).
# W is held 1 s..15 s (wall) = ~140 s of sim-time at 10x, burning ~12 kg
# (0.09 kg/s) of the pocket's 78.54 kg. The kerbal's mass starts at the dry
# suit (~87 kg) + the full pocket tank (~89 kg) = ~176 kg and ends near ~164 kg.
#
# --prox-warp 10 is the crux: the fixture's racer is ~500 m from the kerbal,
# inside the fly engage radius (2000 m), so it is LIVE/engaged and the warp
# would be capped to prox_warp (default 1.0) -- at 1x the burn is only ~1 kg
# and the mass never crosses 170. Lifting the cap to 10 lets the fixture's
# 10x warp run, so the 14 s hold burns ~12 kg. (The pocket draw itself works
# at any warp; this is purely a test-speed matter.)
#
# (No velocity assertion: in orbit gravity rotates the velocity vector by far
# more than the RCS adds over the burn, so the mass drop is the signal.)
NAME inventory-pocket
ARGS --load e2e/fixtures/save_inventory --timeout 18
ARGS --eva-log --orbit-interval 0.5 --sim-press 1000,14000,W --frame-cap 30 --prox-warp 10
EXPECT Loaded 4 inventory item(s) with the fleet
CHECK len(eva) > 5
CHECK all(e["mode"] == "space" for e in eva)
CHECK first(eva)["mass"] > 174.0
CHECK last(eva)["mass"] < 170.0
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
