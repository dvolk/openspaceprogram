# The camera park/restore round trip, driven by the button that causes it: the
# top bar's "Back to game". --vab-close called vabClose directly, which is the
# same function the button calls, so the hook only ever skipped the click.
#
# The subject is the pair of prints, not the pop: the camera must be parked to
# the editor's framing and restored to EXACTLY the distance it had in flight, so
# the two CHECKs require two lines with one distinct distance.
NAME vab-close
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --vab res/ships/tanker.json --timeout 10
ARGS --ui-click 2000,"VAB TopBar/Back to game##vab"
EXPECT [vab] entered the editor (sim keeps coasting)
EXPECT [vab] back to flight (sim keeps coasting)
EXPECT [scene] flight -> vab (push)
EXPECT [scene] vab -> flight (pop)
EXPECT [cam] parked: orbit focus=ship dist=
EXPECT [cam] restored: orbit focus=ship dist=
CHECK len(re.findall(r"\[cam\] (?:parked|restored): orbit focus=ship dist=([\d.]+) m", out)) == 2
CHECK len(set(re.findall(r"\[cam\] (?:parked|restored): orbit focus=ship dist=([\d.]+) m", out))) == 1
FORBID FAILED
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
