# The occupancy check is per pad, not per body: a ship parked on Kerbin's
# polar pad must not refuse a launch from the main space port. Guards the
# check from over-tightening into "any ship anywhere on this home body",
# which the COM proximity reject makes tempting.
NAME vab-pad-neighbour-clear
CLAIM Occupancy is per pad: a ship on the polar pad does not block the main port.
KIND invariant
ARGS --vab res/ships/tanker.json --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad,polar_pad
ARGS --ui-click 2000,"VAB TopBar/LAUNCH" --timeout 12
EXPECT [vab] launched 'tanker' (7 parts)
FORBID launch refused
FORBID error:
