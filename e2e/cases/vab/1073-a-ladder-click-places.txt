# The ladder part (#1073 stage 1): palette-arm "Ladder (2m)", click the racer's
# tank side in the 3D view, and the surface attach places it -- LAUNCH finds
# the racer's four parts plus the ladder. The click is at screen centre, which
# the loaded build's framing puts on the tank (the stack's middle); no stack
# node is near enough to snap, so this is the surface path, the one a kerbal
# will climb. Behaviour (climbing) is stage 2; this pins the part's existence,
# its palette row, and that the existing attach machinery takes it.
NAME ladder-click-places
CLAIM A ladder armed from the palette surface-places on a tank click and launches with the ship.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 12 --frame-cap 30
# In orbit, not on the pad: the picker lists it either way, and the LAUNCH
# pad must be free (a pad-occupied startship refuses the launch).
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --sim-press 1000,0,4
ARGS --sim-press 1500,0,F1
ARGS --ui-click 2000,"Game Debug/Dev vehicles in the VAB picker"
ARGS --sim-press 2500,0,F1
ARGS --ui-type 3000,"VAB TopBar/##vehfilter",racer
ARGS --ui-click 3600,"VAB TopBar/##vabload"
ARGS --ui-click 4200,"racer"
ARGS --ui-click 4800,"VAB TopBar/Load"
# The ladder is near the catalog's end, below the palette's fold: scroll the
# list (a visible row names its child window) to the bottom first.
ARGS --ui-scroll 5400,"Palette/Capsule (2m)##capsule",-30
ARGS --ui-click 6000,"Palette/Ladder (2m)##ladder"
ARGS --sim-mouse 6800,0,960,540,LMB
ARGS --ui-click 7600,"VAB TopBar/LAUNCH"
EXPECT [vab] launched
CHECK re.search(r"\[vab\] launched '[^']+' \((\d+) parts\)", out).group(1) == "5"
