# Nothing named, so the pad fills the ship -- EVERY seat, not one per capsule
# part. A 3-berth capsule used to launch with a single person while two stood
# watching from the pad (issue #275); the pad has three, so all three go up.
NAME vab-seat-autofill
CLAIM With no names, every seat is auto-filled from the pad.
KIND invariant
ARGS --vab-empty --kerbals e2e/fixtures/three_kerbals.json
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 12 --frame-cap 30
ARGS --ui-click 1000,"Capsule (3m)##capsule_r1.5h3"
ARGS --sim-mouse 2000,0,960,540,LMB --ui-click 4000,"VAB TopBar/LAUNCH"
CHECK re.search(r"Crew: 'Ace' aboard '.*' part 0 \(1/3\)", out)
CHECK re.search(r"Crew: 'Bram' aboard '.*' part 0 \(2/3\)", out)
CHECK re.search(r"Crew: 'Cyd' aboard '.*' part 0 \(3/3\)", out)
EXPECT [vab] launched 'Ship' (1 parts)
FORBID no kerbal left
FORBID error:
LIMIT 90
