# RMB picks a part in flight. The case NAMES the part it wants under the cursor
# (--ui-focus resolves it to a pixel) rather than writing a pixel down: since
# #876's pad camera turns with the ground, the screen centre is the hull's COM,
# and for `racer` that sits inside its fuel tank -- it used to be the top of the
# mast, because the turntable was held by the hull's own attitude. A pixel here
# would be a claim about framing, and framing belongs to #876/#919 to change.
NAME part-pick
CLAIM RMB in flight opens the part window for the part under the cursor.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 0 --timeout 15 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --ui-focus 1000,"racer/capsule_1"
ARGS --sim-mouse 1200,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
EXPECT Main loop starting
EXPECT [uifocus] 'racer/capsule_1' ->
EXPECT ship=racer part=0 (capsule)
FORBID miss px=
FORBID uifocus] 'racer/capsule_1' FAILED
FORBID error:
