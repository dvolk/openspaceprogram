# alt= is one height over an air bed (build_start_vehicles, src/vehicle.cpp), so it
# has no meaning on the two beds that don't have one. A pad's height is the ground
# under its site; an ellipse authors two heights at once (peri_alt/apo_alt) and a
# circular speed that goes with them. Accepting alt= there would either quietly
# discard the scenario or invent a third bed, and the player would read a bed that
# never happened.
# Same shape as the pad-id-on-an-orbit-bed refusal (#191): name the field, name the
# scenario, exit nonzero, never launch.
NAME startship-alt-on-a-pad-bed
CLAIM alt= on a bed that has no altitude of its own is refused at boot, by name, instead of being ignored.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 4 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad,alt=84000
EXPECT error: start ships: entry 0: alt places an air bed, but 'pad' stands on a pad -- its height is the ground under its site
EXIT 1
FORBID [vab] launched
FORBID alt 84000 m asked
LIMIT 60
