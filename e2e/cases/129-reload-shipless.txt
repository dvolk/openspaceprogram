NAME reload-shipless
# In-game reload of a shipless save (Game::loadFrom) must land on the Space
# Center hub, not the title. The bare boot is the title front door; the
# shipless reload moves it to the hub.
ARGS --system res/systems/ksp_system.json --timeout 10
ARGS --reload e2e/fixtures/save_shipless --reload-at 3000
EXPECT [boot] no vessel: title screen
EXPECT Loaded game from e2e/fixtures/save_shipless (active: (none))
EXPECT [scene] title -> spacecenter (enterSpaceCenter)
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
LIMIT 120
