# tracking the camera. What this pins is the tree ADVANCING -- at 685 km no
# leaf is near enough to carry a Bullet body, so every line reads collision=0
# and the grounded-ship claim is not what this case proves. The station's old
# row refreshed g.view only, so the tree froze and no [terrain] line appeared.
