# The hours-aboard row (#896) has two branches, and the charging one had no live coverage:
# every PRESET ship draws more than it generates (power_test's 298 W RTG sits under its
# 600 W of air), but the parts catalog ships a bigger one -- rtg_r1.5h0.375 at 1007.3 W --
# so a hull built from catalog parts pays for its air and still charges. surplus_test is
# power_test with that RTG swapped in, which is the cheapest way to stand in that regime
# without deciding what a real probe should carry (that call is #899's).
#
# A charging hull has no hours aboard, so the row prints the dash the other readouts use
# (`Prg (air): -`, `Air temp: -`) rather than 0.0 h or inf h. ui/601 pins the numeric
# branch's identity; this pins the absence, which no CHECK there can reach -- its identity
# CHECK raises by design on a hull that is not draining.
NAME charging-hull-dashes-its-hours
CLAIM A hull that generates more than it draws prints Aboard: - instead of an hours figure.
KIND invariant
ARGS --width 1600 --height 900 --sim-steps 900 --startship surplus_test,res/ships/surplus_test.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --ui-click 2000,"Windows/Systems"
ARGS --ui-list 5000
LIMIT 60
EXPECT [uilist] window="Systems" label="Aboard:     -   (this balance)"
# The dash must be the CHARGING branch, not a missing pool or a broken row: this dump has
# a full store and a positive Net. Not golden figures -- the sign and the presence are what
# the row claims, and a parts rebalance moves the watts without moving this.
CHECK 'label="Net:        +' in out
CHECK 'label="Charge:     12000.0 / 12000.0 Wh' in out
FORBID Aboard:     0.0 h
FORBID Aboard:     inf
