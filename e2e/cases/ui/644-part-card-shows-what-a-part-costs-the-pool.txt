# What a part costs the pool is printed on the part's own card (#644). The watts were
# readable before on dev surfaces only -- the Game Debug overlay's Power block and
# --power-log -- so mass, fuel, thrust and drag were on the card a part is chosen with
# while the watts that decide whether a hull can keep its instruments and its steering
# were not. #596 moved a pod's draw from 100 W to 225 W and #593 gave every wheel block a
# package figure, and nothing on any part card changed, because nothing there printed a watt.
#
# The rows live in drawPartPhysique, which is where part specs already live for BOTH
# surfaces: the flight part window and the VAB's part card. They read PartDef's own terms
# (part.h), the same accessors powerTick() and wheelDemandWatts() sum, so the card and the
# flight balance print one set of terms rather than two sums (#575's rule). uiText, not
# ImGui::Text, so the harness reads what the player is shown (#601).
#
# power_test is the electrical fixture flight/377 and ui/601 already use. Two of its cards
# are clickable from a flight orbit, and between them they print all three terms: the pod
# (225 W commanding -- it has internal drums, 450 N m x3 -- plus 600 W of life support) and
# the RTG (+298 W generated). The wheel block's card, whose row is the package figure #593
# introduced, is pinned in the VAB instead (ui/644-vab-prints-what-the-assembly-costs-the-pool)
# because its housing sits between pod and tank and the orbit raycast hits its occluder.
# The figures are pinned numerically in test_shipload; the balance they add up to is pinned
# on screen by ui/601.
NAME part-card-shows-what-a-part-costs-the-pool
CLAIM A part's own flight window prints its electrical terms: the pod's 225 W commanding and 600 W constant, the RTG's +298 W generated -- three rows, no others.
KIND golden
ARGS --width 1920 --height 1080 --timeout 9 --frame-cap 30
ARGS --startship power_test,res/ships/power_test.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --ui-focus 2000,power_test/capsule_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 2030,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-focus 2600,power_test/rtg_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 2630,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-list 4000
EXPECT [uifocus] 'power_test/capsule_1' ->
EXPECT [uifocus] 'power_test/rtg_1' ->
# The ## suffix on a flight part window is the part's INDEX in ship->parts, not its id
# (gameui.cpp names it from `part`), so reordering res/ships/power_test.json moves these
# literals -- a file flight/377, flight/647 and ui/601 also lean on.
EXPECT [uilist] window="power_test > Capsule (4.5m)##0" label="Power: 225 W while commanding (one drum at full demand)"
EXPECT [uilist] window="power_test > Capsule (4.5m)##0" label="Power: 600 W constant (life support)"
EXPECT [uilist] window="power_test > RTG (2m)##2" label="Power: +298 W generated"
FORBID error:
FORBID FAILED
# Exactly three power rows in the whole dump: one per term, on the card that term belongs
# to. A row printed for the wrong term, a generator given a draw, a pod that pays nothing
# because the rule keyed on part type instead of torque (#596), or a package figure
# tripled per drum (#593) all break this count as well as the EXPECTs above.
CHECK len([l for l in out.split("\n") if 'label="Power:' in l]) == 3
