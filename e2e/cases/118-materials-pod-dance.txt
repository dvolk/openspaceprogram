# The take/store dance (KSP): the labpod's Materials Pod runs a "materials
# study" (run -> the pod's active slot), an aboard kerbal TAKES it off the
# pod onto their suit (the courier), then STORES it into the capsule (the
# container). The finding ends up in the capsule, not the pod -- but
# recoverActive harvests every part's experiments, so it banks identically
# whether it sits on the instrument, the courier, or the container. First-
# time high-orbit materials study on the home body = 25 x 1.5 x 1.0 = 38.
# The [science] moved lines (take: pod->suit, store: suit->capsule) are the
# new anchors; the capacity ceiling + role rules are unit-tested in
# test_science (canHoldFinding) and test_shipload (the role pins).
NAME materials-pod-dance
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --startship labpod,res/ships/labpod.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --pod-experiment 1500
ARGS --take 2000
ARGS --store 2500
ARGS --space-center 3000 --recover 4000 --recover-anywhere
EXPECT [science] t=
EXPECT recorded
EXPECT materials study
EXPECT moved
EXPECT [science] recovered +38
EXPECT [recover] t=
EXPECT [spacecenter] entered
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
