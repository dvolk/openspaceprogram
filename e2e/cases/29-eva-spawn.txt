# EVA from the crewed capsule: the racer ships with a kerbal aboard its
# capsule (1 per capsule at build); V hands that crew member out onto EVA,
# control passes to the kerbal, and it walks (W) on the same pad floor.
NAME eva-spawn
ARGS --ship res/ships/racer.json --time-accel 1 --timeout 9 --eva-log --orbit-interval 0.3 --sim-press 1000,0,V,2500,2000,W --frame-cap 30
EXPECT Crew: 'kerbal' aboard 'racer' part 0 (1/1)
EXPECT EVA: 'kerbal' out of 'racer' part 0
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2: kerbal
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
CHECK len(eva) > 5
CHECK max((e["vel"][0]**2 + e["vel"][1]**2 + e["vel"][2]**2)**0.5 for e in eva) > 1.5
