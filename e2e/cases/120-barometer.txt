# Barometer: a basic science instrument (base 10) that works from anywhere
# but is biome-specific only when landed or flying-low. In LOW orbit (the
# rot-orbit scenario sits at 85km, below the 110km orbit cut) it yields a
# GLOBAL result -- "Low orbit barometer on Kerbin" (no biome) -- the
# "atmospheric pressure scan" rule. The active ship (baropod) carries a
# Barometer + an aboard kerbal (spawn_crew seats one in the capsule); the
# --pod-experiment hook runs it, and Recover Vessel banks it (recoverActive
# harvests every part's experiments). First-time low-orbit on the home body
# = 10 x 1.25 x 1.0 = 12.5 -> 13. The [science] recorded / recovered lines
# are the e2e anchors; the biome rule + name are unit-tested in test_science.
NAME barometer
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --startship baropod,res/ships/baropod.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --pod-experiment 1500
ARGS --space-center 3000 --recover 4000 --recover-anywhere
EXPECT [science] t=
EXPECT recorded
EXPECT Low orbit barometer on Kerbin
EXPECT [science] recovered +13
EXPECT [recover] t=
EXPECT [spacecenter] entered
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
