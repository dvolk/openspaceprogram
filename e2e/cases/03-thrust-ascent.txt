# Thrust ascent: on Moho (racer TWR ~2.3, the only body where the racer can
# lift off), ramp throttle (R) then fire (T). The ship must gain altitude.
# Direction-independent: only checks the altitude rose, not the trajectory.
# Kerbin is too heavy for every current ship (all TWR < 1), hence Moho.
NAME thrust-ascent
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Moho,pad --time-accel 1
ARGS --timeout 10 --dbg-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 3000,2000,T
EXPECT [dbg]
CHECK len(dbg) >= 2 and last(dbg)["alt"] - first(dbg)["alt"] > 10
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
