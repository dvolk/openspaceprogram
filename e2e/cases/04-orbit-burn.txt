# Orbit burn: in a circular orbit, ramp throttle (R) and fire (I). A non-zero
# burn changes specific orbital energy E (a constant between burns), so assert
# |ΔE| is significant. Deliberately direction-independent -- the spawn attitude
# isn't prograde, so a prograde/retrograde assertion would be flaky.
NAME orbit-burn
ARGS --scenario rot-orbit --ship res/ships/racer.json --time-accel 10
ARGS --timeout 12 --orbit-log --orbit-interval 0.5
ARGS --sim-press 500,500,R
ARGS --sim-press 1500,3000,I
EXPECT [orbitlog]
CHECK len(orbit) >= 2 and abs(last(orbit)["E"] - first(orbit)["E"]) > 1e5
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
