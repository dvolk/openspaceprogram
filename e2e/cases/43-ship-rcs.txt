# Ship RCS translation (KSP-style, ship-relative): a ship on an inertial
# orbit holds N (RcsForward) -- the ship translates along its own nose,
# burning hydrazine mono (the same propellant the EVA suit uses). RCS is a fixed thrust gated on the ship's propellant
# (consume-then-arm, like the ship's engines), so the burn both changes the
# velocity AND sheds fuel mass -- and the osculating orbit's specific energy
# E (a constant between burns) moves. The N press exercises the
# RcsForward binding (H/N back-forward, I/K up-down, J/L left-right; see
# src/keys.cpp) -- the ship's analogue of the EVA kerbal's WASD translation.
NAME ship-rcs
ARGS --ship res/ships/rcs_test.json --scenario inertial-orbit --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --dbg-log --orbit-log --orbit-interval 0.5 --sim-press 2000,2000,N --frame-cap 30
EXPECT Building ship 'rcs_test' (2 parts)
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
CHECK len(dbg) >= 2
# the burn changed the osculating orbit's specific energy (RCS actually
# thrust; E is direction-independent, unlike a single velocity component)
CHECK abs(last(orbit)["E"] - first(orbit)["E"]) > 100.0
