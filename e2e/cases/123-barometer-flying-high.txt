# Barometer in FLYING-HIGH: the global half of the flying rule. A ship
# airborne in the upper atmosphere (the flying-high scenario, ~50km on Kerbin
# -- above the 20%-of-atmo split) records a GLOBAL reading -- "Flying high
# barometer on Kerbin" (no biome) -- unlike flying-low, which is "of <Biome>".
# First-time flying-high on the home body = 10 x 1.2 x 1.0 = 12. Mirrors
# 122-barometer-flying-low (biome-specific) so the two pin the barometer's
# flying biome rule; the landed half is 121-barometer-landed.
NAME barometer-flying-high
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --startship baropod,res/ships/baropod.json,Kerbin,flying-high
ARGS --pod-experiment 1200
ARGS --space-center 2500 --recover 3500 --recover-anywhere
EXPECT [science] t=
EXPECT recorded
EXPECT Flying high barometer on Kerbin
EXPECT [science] recovered +12
EXPECT [recover] t=
EXPECT [spacecenter] entered
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
