# is NOT the home body -- the other half of the 107/106 guard
# (isGrounded() && m_parent == home). The racer parks on Moho's pad, so
# it IS grounded (rotating frame, near-static, low altitude) but m_parent
# is Moho, not Kerbin: recoverActive toasts and bails. Companion to 106
# (refused in Kerbin orbit) and 107 (allowed on the Kerbin pad).

ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Moho,pad
ARGS --space-center 1000 --recover 2000
EXPECT [scene] flight -> spacecenter (push)
EXPECT [toast] Cannot recover
EXPECT grounded on the home body
EXPECT Timeout reached (8.
