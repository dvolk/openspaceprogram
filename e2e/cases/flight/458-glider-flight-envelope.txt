NAME glider-flight-envelope
CLAIM The shipped glider pays air for the union of its parts AS MOUNTED (4.3 m2 nose-on, 26 m2 broadside, not a 30 m2 balloon), its lift owes induced drag, and with only yaw surfaces it can be steered past vertical but never pulled out.
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship glider,res/ships/glider.json,Kerbin,flying-slow
ARGS --timeout 14 --drag-log --mass-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 1500,6000,T
ARGS --sim-press 3000,3000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [ctrl]
CHECK len(drag) >= 10
CHECK all(d.get("aoa") is not None and abs(d["aoa"]) < 1.0 for d in drag[:3])
# Same reading as the jet case, on the nose-on rows: 4.3 m2 = the pod disc plus
# the two plates seen edge-on. RE-BASELINED 2026_10_28 twice -- #463's union, then
# the mount offsets it first dropped (which had it at 3.4, charging one part
# instead of a union).
CHECK 4.0 < min(d.get("a", 0.0) for d in drag[:3]) < 5.0
# Turned over, its planforms come into the wind: 26 m2, a 6.0x swing (the jet
# shows 8.0x). Reproduces to 0.01 across runs (#472 leaves shape alone) -- the
# balloon law showed 1.2x here, since a bubble does not change shape.
CHECK max(d.get("a", 0.0) for d in drag) > 4.0 * min(d.get("a", 0.0) for d in drag)
# On the clean rows the force printed IS the law printed: |F| / (q * Cd * A) =
# --drag-cd's 1.2 default. The knob-sensitive pin: --drag-cd 0.2 or 8 fails it
# while the shape pins above still pass.
CHECK 1.15 < max(d["F"] / (0.5 * d["rho"] * d["v"] * d["v"] * d["cd"] * d["a"]) for d in drag[:3]) < 1.25
# |L| / q, lift in area units: 2 x 8 m2 at CLmax 2.1 = 33 m2-equivalent ceiling,
# reached (25..32 depending on the run). It used to sample 14.4 because the
# balloon's drag bled the airframe before it could fly at a useful AoA.
CHECK 10.0 < max(d["L"] / (0.5 * d["rho"] * d["v"] * d["v"]) for d in drag if d.get("L") and d["v"] > 1.0) < 60.0
# #462's coupling: the lifting substeps pay induced drag and the post-stall rows
# (|L| = 0, the droop) pay none. Charge it unconditionally, or drop it, and one of
# these fails.
CHECK any(d.get("induced", 0.0) > 0.0 for d in drag)
CHECK all(d.get("induced", 0.0) > 0.0 for d in drag if d.get("L", 0.0) / max(0.5 * d["rho"] * d["v"] * d["v"], 1.0) > 1.0)
CHECK all(d.get("induced", 0.0) == 0.0 for d in drag if d.get("L", 0.0) == 0.0)
# PINNING A BUG: |AoA| reaches 169 deg and stays past 90 -- A only yaws it, so
# nothing pulls it out. Before #463 the balloon's drag at the centre of pressure
# hid the missing static margin; here it is the whole story. Expected to FAIL when
# #454 lands, and re-baselined in that commit.
CHECK max(abs(d["aoa"]) for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None) > 90.0
# And the flow spends its time in the back hemisphere (Beta to 165 deg), which is
# the half the silhouette cache cannot key directly: past +-90 in one angle the
# angles must wrap by 180 together, because AeroFrame's two share their atan2
# denominator. When that wrap was missing, every row past 90 read the area of a
# quartering flow -- 42-82% wrong on this airframe.
CHECK max(abs(d["beta"]) for d in drag if d.get("beta") is not None) > 90.0
CHECK any(d.get("L", 0.0) == 0.0 for d in drag)
# DROPPED on purpose (was 0.85..0.95 max L/F, and before that 0.25..0.5 g of
# weight carried): both peak inside the tumble, and two runs give 0.74 and 0.79 --
# #472, not physics. This airframe has no engine and no pitch surface, so there is
# no steady state to measure until #454 lands.
# What the airframe is built from: A is held for 3 s and both rudders reach their
# travel limit, and the shipped glider has NO pitch surface at all -- the stick
# cannot ask for nose-up, which is half of why nothing about trim can be tuned on
# it (#454). If a tailplane ever ships, that last line is the one to change.
CHECK all(d["t"] is not None for d in ctrl)
CHECK any(d["axis"] == "yaw" and abs(d["deg"]) > 15.0 for d in ctrl)
CHECK all(d["axis"] == "yaw" for d in ctrl)
