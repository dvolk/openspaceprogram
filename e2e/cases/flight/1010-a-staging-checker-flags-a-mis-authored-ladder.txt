# The staging checker (#1010), end to end. `e2e/vehicles/misstaged.json` is the shape a
# player produces when they number stages by feel rather than by presses: a wide gap
# (label 5 for the booster over a label-1 core) and a radial decoupler parked on the
# LOWEST label. Both are legal -- nothing refuses them -- and both cost a press, so the
# checker says so in the same witness the tree dump uses.
#
# No shipped hull trips these rules (checked against res/ships separately), which is why
# the fixture exists: without it this case would only ever prove the checker stays silent.
NAME staging-checker-flags-a-mis-authored-ladder
CLAIM The staging checker counts the presses a bad ladder wastes and names the floor decoupler.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship misstaged,e2e/vehicles/misstaged.json,Kerbin,pad --stage-dump 1 --sim-steps 5
ARGS --width 640 --height 360 --no-paint
LIMIT 60
EXPECT [stagetree] src=hull hull=misstaged parts=8 stage=5/5
EXPECT [stagetree] labels=1..5 presses=4 silent=4/8
# The gap: presses at 4, 3 and 2 have no part on the label that stands there. One line,
# the run compressed -- a ladder numbered 2..300 must not print 299 lines.
EXPECT [stagetree] warn: 3 of 4 presses do nothing -- no part carries 2-4
# The floor decoupler: the counter stops at label 1, so dec_pod needs one press more than
# stagesLeft() counts, and the ascent law will never make it.
EXPECT [stagetree] warn: label 1 is the lowest, and its decoupler/chute needs a press past the last one the counter counts
# The label-5 decoupler is NOT a fault: it separates on the first press like any other
# (#1018's point that a high label is first in order, not late). Its row says so.
EXPECT [stagetree] 4 dec_boost def=decoupler_r1 parent=eng_up stage=5 responds=decoupler
FORBID error:
FORBID FAILED
