NAME flying-slow-starts-in-air
CLAIM The flying-slow bed starts wings-level with its nose along the air at a fraction of the AIR-relative circular speed, so a plane enters its altitude band moving rather than dropping.
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship jet,res/ships/jet.json,Kerbin,flying-slow
ARGS --timeout 12 --drag-log --orbit-interval 0.2
EXPECT Spawn 'flying-slow' around Kerbin
EXPECT frame 'Kerbin (rotational)'
CHECK len(drag) >= 3
# speed_frac only means what it says in the frame the air sits still in, so name
# that frame: a bed that slipped into the inertial one would fail here rather
# than as a wrong number further down.
# Kerbin's flying-low bed runs CIRCULAR at 2412 m/s, which is what lets it hold
# 7 km with no lift at all (#99). 0.08 of that speed, read off the frame the bed
# resolves into, is 180 m/s of air: the start a wing can carry.
CHECK 150.0 < float(re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-slow' around Kerbin:.*?\|v\| = ([\d.]+) m/s", out).group(1)) < 188.0
# The upper bound is the bug this bed was written around. The atmosphere
# co-rotates, so scaling the INERTIAL circular speed prints 193 m/s while
# handing the ship 16 m/s of air -- Kerbin's ground and its air already move
# 177 m/s prograde. --orbit-interval 0.2 puts the first row at t=0, holding the
# speed the bed promised (179 m/s) instead of a wall-clock-gated sample of
# something already decaying at 45 m/s^2.
CHECK drag[0]["v"] > 0.9 * float(re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-slow' around Kerbin:.*?\|v\| = ([\d.]+) m/s", out).group(1))
# The same line now also prints the speed the AIR feels (#459), which is the one
# the aero pass uses. On a speed_frac bed the two agree -- that IS the bed's
# promise -- whereas the circular flying-low bed prints |v| = 2412 m/s next to
# |v_air| = 2112 m/s, because the atmosphere co-rotates. A missing field fails
# here (the regex raises) instead of quietly reading as one fewer number.
CHECK abs(float(re.search(r"\|v_air\| = ([\d.]+) m/s", out).group(1)) - drag[0]["v"]) < 20.0
# Nose along the flow, wings level. Up is the airframe's own +Y against the local
# vertical; the air-relative velocity here sits 1.5 deg out of the orbit plane
# (the co-rotation term), and faceAlong picks its roll axis from three candidates,
# so that tilt used to be answered with a 90 deg roll -- lift sideways, and W/S
# yawing the ship instead of pitching it.
CHECK abs(drag[0]["aoa"]) < 2.0
CHECK drag[0]["up"] > 0.9
FORBID error:
