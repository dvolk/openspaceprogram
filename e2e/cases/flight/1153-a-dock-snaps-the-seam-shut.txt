NAME dock snaps the seam shut
CLAIM A pair that captures tilted welds straight: the donor is moved by about the air and the tilt, and the mated seam is left with neither.
KIND invariant
# --dock-test tilt is --dock-test approach with the probe's bore 4 deg off the joining line
# -- inside the 5 deg cone (cos 4 deg = 0.9976 > kDockAlign = 0.9962), so the pair still
# mates, and it is the only fixture that gives #1153's snap an angle to take up. `approach`
# arrives with nothing to straighten and `misaligned` is refused at 40 deg, so a snap that
# never ran at all would leave both of those untouched. If kDockAlign is ever retuned below
# 4 deg, the FIXTURE has to move with it (as with misaligned's 40 deg) and this row stops
# printing until someone does -- a dock that refuses is not a dock that snapped.
# The two pins that matter are the post- ones: a fresh dockRead of the two ports INSIDE the
# merged hull, i.e. the parts the ship was actually built from.
#   postgap=  the air left between the presented faces -- the pair's collision standoff
#             (dockStandoff: 0.000 m at the shipped hull margins), not a geometric ideal
#   postkink= each bore's deviation from the seam line, summed; 0.00 deg when both bores sit
#             on it
# They are not an independent verdict on the mate's quality -- a correct snap makes them zero
# by construction -- they are the check that the merge DELIVERED the motion it computed, and
# each one sees a defect the other misses: a translation with no rotation leaves the two face
# CENTRES together (postgap at the standoff) with the station still bent, and a rotation with
# no translation straightens the bores and keeps the air. Mutation-checked one at a time --
# logs in tmp/night7: M1 no port pair -> postgap=0.099 postkink=3.75 (red); M2 rotate-only ->
# postgap=0.099 (red); M3 translate-only -> postgap=0.000 but postkink=3.75 (red).
# snap=/turn= are what the DONOR's mated port moved, so the correction is a number rather
# than something to squint at on screen. They are pinned loosely and nonzero: their exact
# values belong to the fixture (approach prints snap=0.099 m turn=0.00 deg for the same
# 0.099 m of air, because an aligned dock has only the gap to take up).
# HOW BIG IT CAN GET, for a pair the gates accepted: the mated FACE slides at most
# captureRange (0.10 m for the shipped r1 pair) and the pair TURNS at most 10 deg (2 x
# acos(kDockAlign)). That is NOT how far the ship appears to move -- the donor is rigid, so
# its far end swings turn x its distance from the seam: 0.23 m for this pair's 3.6 m stack at
# 3.75 deg, and a measured 1.45 m on a 16.6 m donor at the cone's edge. kDockAlign, not
# anything in the snap, is the knob that holds that down.
ARGS --dock-test tilt --time-accel 1 --timeout 8
EXPECT Dock-test (tilt)
EXPECT [dock] t=
EXPECT a="dockprobe" b="dockstation"
CHECK (lambda x: len(x) == 1 and 0.05 < float(x[0][0]) < 0.12 and abs(float(x[0][1]) - 0.10) < 0.02 and 0.05 < float(x[0][3]) < 0.20 and 0.5 < float(x[0][4]) < 5.0 and abs(float(x[0][5])) < 0.001 and abs(float(x[0][6])) < 0.05)(re.findall(r'\[dock\] t=\S+ a="dockprobe" b="dockstation" d=([-\d.]+) m cap=([-\d.]+) m v=([-\d.]+) m/s snap=([-\d.]+) m turn=([-\d.]+) deg postgap=([-\d.]+) m postkink=([-\d.]+) deg', out))
