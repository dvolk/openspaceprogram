NAME dock snaps the seam shut
CLAIM A pair that captures tilted welds straight: the donor is moved by about the air and the tilt, and the mated seam is left with neither.
KIND invariant
# --dock-test tilt is --dock-test approach with the probe's bore 4 deg off the joining line
# -- inside the 5 deg cone (cos 4 deg = 0.9976 > kDockAlign = 0.9962), so the pair still
# mates, and it is the only fixture that gives #1153's snap an angle to take up. `approach`
# arrives with nothing to straighten and `misaligned` is refused at 40 deg, so a snap that
# never ran at all would leave both of those untouched. If kDockAlign is ever retuned below
# 4 deg, the FIXTURE has to move with it (as with misaligned's 40 deg) and this row stops
# printing until someone does -- a dock that refuses is not a dock that snapped.
# The two pins that matter are the post- ones, and they are a fresh dockRead of the two
# ports INSIDE the merged hull, not the snap's own arithmetic echoed back:
#   postgap=  the air left between the presented faces, 0.000 m when the seam is shut
#   postkink= the bores' deviation from the joining line summed, 0.00 deg when it is straight
# Both are needed and neither is enough: a translation with no rotation leaves the two face
# CENTRES coincident (postgap=0.000) with the station still bent, and a rotation with no
# translation straightens the bores and keeps the air. Mutation-checked both ways -- see the
# notes in tmp/night7/comment_1153.md.
# snap=/turn= are what the DONOR moved, so the size of the correction is a number rather
# than something to squint at on screen. They are pinned loosely and nonzero: their exact
# values belong to the fixture (approach prints snap=0.099 m turn=0.00 deg for the same
# 0.099 m of air, because an aligned dock has only the gap to take up).
# Bound on the player's side: the snap cannot be bigger than the gates that let the mate
# happen -- 0.10 m of face for the shipped r1 pair, 10 deg between the two bores -- so a
# retune of kDockCaptureFrac or kDockAlign retunes the visible jump with it.
ARGS --dock-test tilt --time-accel 1 --timeout 8
EXPECT Dock-test (tilt)
EXPECT [dock] t=
EXPECT a="dockprobe" b="dockstation"
CHECK (lambda x: len(x) == 1 and 0.05 < float(x[0][3]) < 0.20 and 0.5 < float(x[0][4]) < 5.0 and abs(float(x[0][5])) < 0.001 and abs(float(x[0][6])) < 0.05)(re.findall(r'\[dock\] t=\S+ a="dockprobe" b="dockstation" d=([-\d.]+) m cap=([-\d.]+) m v=([-\d.]+) m/s snap=([-\d.]+) m turn=([-\d.]+) deg postgap=([-\d.]+) m postkink=([-\d.]+) deg', out))
