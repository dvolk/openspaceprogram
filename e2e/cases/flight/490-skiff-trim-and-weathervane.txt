NAME skiff-trim-and-weathervane
CLAIM A 552 kg airframe built to the #490 arithmetic -- 21.6 m2 of AR-3.33 wing 0.34 m behind the CG, a 4 m2 tailplane set to -5 deg incidence 2.0 m back, a fixed fin 2.2 m back -- holds 7000 m at 79 m/s inside 16 m with the stick released, keeps +2.6 deg of angle of attack through throttle-off AND a 1 s rudder kick, weathervanes that kick from 7.7 deg back to zero, and keeps its centre of pressure 0.69-0.81 m behind the CG the whole time.
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R,500,20000,T,25000,1000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [mass]
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
# The airframe, from the [mass] row: 11 parts, 552.3 kg. That is the #490 audit's
# prescription measured against the shipped glider's 1417 kg and 4.14 m2 of hull CdA:
# this hull is 0.75 m2 (the [drag] line's Cd 0.42 x A 1.75 m2 at AoA 0) for a quarter
# of the mass, so the two lifting surfaces carry the load instead of the fuselage.
CHECK mass[0]["parts"] == 11
CHECK 545.0 < mass[0]["mass"] < 560.0
# The bed. flying-slow starts a ship at 0.08 of air-relative circular speed, 180 m/s
# on Kerbin, which suits hulls that fly at several hundred m/s; a light wing trims near
# 80, so on that bed every run opens by trading the excess for 650 m of height and
# coming back through a phugoid. flying-cruise is the same altitude band at 80 m/s, so
# what the case measures is the airframe rather than the launch.
# LEVEL FLIGHT, hands off (no W/S/A/D at any point; R is the throttle and T only latches
# the engine, which releaseControl() starts shut). Throttle up at 0.5 s, latched from
# 0.5 to 20.5 s: altitude stays inside 16 m of the 7000 m it spawned at, and speed
# inside 0.6 m/s of 79. Lift 5.15-5.56 kN against a 5.42 kN weight, i.e. the wing is
# holding the aeroplane up, not the engine.
CHECK all(6960.0 < d["alt"] < 7040.0 for d in drag if 5 <= d["t"] <= 20)
CHECK all(78.0 < d["v"] < 80.0 for d in drag if 5 <= d["t"] <= 20)
CHECK all(0.90 * mass[0]["mass"] * 9.81 < d["L_up"] < 1.10 * mass[0]["mass"] * 9.81 for d in drag if 5 <= d["t"] <= 20)
# TRIM. AoA reads 2.6 deg on every row from t=1 to t=50 -- powered, gliding, and
# through the sideslip transient, where it reaches 2.8 at worst. There is no `trim`
# field in the ship def, and this law has no surface pitching moment and no alpha term
# for control surfaces, so with everything symmetric alpha = 0 is the ONLY pitch
# equilibrium: the first skiff, hands off, sat at AoA exactly 0.0 and sank. What trims
# it is mount incidence -- shipdef "roll" on a surface mount, since a plate's chord is
# its Z axis -- so the zero-moment angle sits at +2.6 deg with the wing at 0 (#487's
# design note that trim is a standing angle, not a control input, made physical).
CHECK all(2.3 < d["aoa"] < 3.2 for d in drag if d["t"] >= 5)
# GLIDE. The throttle latch releases at 20.5 s and nothing else is pressed. The ship
# slows to 62 m/s, dips, trades the height back to 79 m/s, and settles into a steady
# ~22 m/s sink at 78-79 m/s -- 3.5:1, against the shipped pair's 1.2:1 -- still at the
# same angle of attack, still upright, never below 55 m/s in the exchange.
CHECK min(d["v"] for d in drag if d["t"] >= 22) > 55.0
CHECK all(d["alt"] > 5800.0 for d in drag)
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.85 for d in drag if d["t"] >= 5)
# WEATHERVANE. One 1 s rudder press at 25 s, nothing held: Beta 7.7, -4.9, ... 1.4,
# -2.2, 0.3, 0.1, 0.0 -- back inside 1 deg by t=40, and AoA never left the band above
# while it happened. The shipped jet with a rudder HELD sits at 100 deg of sideslip
# (#482), with a tenth the area here. The difference is not the rudder: this hull's
# 1.75 m2 stands 0.69-0.81 m BEHIND the CG, so its own form drag pulls the nose back
# into the flow, and CpL (#486's lever) grows to -0.81 m at the worst row, which makes
# the restoring strongest exactly when it is needed.
CHECK max(abs(d["beta"]) for d in drag if d["t"] >= 24) > 5.0
CHECK max(abs(d["beta"]) for d in drag if d["t"] >= 40) < 1.0
CHECK all(d["cpl"] < -0.5 for d in drag if d["cpl"] is not None)
CHECK max(abs(d["cpl"]) for d in drag if d["cpl"] is not None) > 0.75
# #462's coupling still holds on a real airplane: every substep that lifts pays induced
# drag, and it pays the WING'S OWN aspect ratio (2 x 3.0 m half-span per part = 6 m over
# a 1.8 m chord = AR 3.33), not the whole ship's. It also jumps with the sideslip
# (0.29 -> 1.35 m2 on the kick row) because the fins present their edges -- the term
# reads the surface's own flow, #483.
CHECK all(d["induced"] > 0.0 for d in drag if d["L"] > 1.0)
CHECK max(d["induced"] for d in drag) > 1.0
