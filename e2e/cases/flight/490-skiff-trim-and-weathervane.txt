NAME skiff-trim-and-weathervane
CLAIM The skiff is the airframe the design point was written for: 569 kg on 24 m2 of AR-10.7 wing, a command pod stacked on a jet fuel tank, spawned at 80 m/s and 8.5 km and holding +2.4..+2.8 deg of angle of attack through its phugoid with 0.55 m of static margin. A one-second rudder kick swings Beta 6.2 deg and settles; the hull bill is Cd 0.57-0.60 on 0.80-0.88 m2, where the smooth spindle this replaced paid 0.11 on 0.87.
KIND golden
# #568 put the pod back on a tank: capsule_r0.5h1.5 at the root, jet_tank_r0.4h2 1.75 m
# aft of it, the engine 3.25 m aft, wings on the TANK at 1.2 m aft of the pod's centre,
# tail 2.85 m, elevators 3.65 m. #570 hung the rudder off the fin's trailing edge at
# 3.95 m and the ailerons off the wings' at 2.45 m -- clear plates, not plates buried
# in their parents. #567 then grew that wing to 16 x 1.5 (its chord now spans 1.95..0.45
# m, the ailerons hang off its trailing edge at 2.30 m aft of the origin, tips 8.4 m
# out; the model prices the pair as one 16.00 m system)
# and the hull to 569 kg (472 of parts, tanks full, plus one 97 kg kerbal -- the pod
# seats one, where the one-piece fuselage seated two), still paying 5x the body bill the
# smooth spindle did. The band below on A is the silhouette union of pod+tank+engine;
# FormCp is where that bill pushes -- -0.44..-0.50 m here, against the -0.68 m the
# spindle's bill reported.
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
# --no-paint: this case reads [drag] rows, never pixels, and a scripted frame
# without the 3D pass costs 0.12 ms rather than 3.08 (#666). The rows are the
# same either way; the run is 5x cheaper.
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log --no-paint
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 500,20000,T
ARGS --sim-press 25000,1000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [mass]
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
# Cruise, held: the trim is a wing station and a wing-loading, not a stick. This hull's
# zero-moment AoA is 2.5 deg and the phugoid and the rudder kick carry it 0.3 deg
# nose-high at their extremes. The ceiling is 0.1 deg over this run's peak; #567's bigger
# wing moved that peak 0.1 deg on its own, so re-measure before tightening it. Kerbin's
# wind column moved it once already: the bed sits at 8.5 km, where the authored column is
# worth ~31 m/s, and a weathervaning hull's null attitude in moving air is not the same
# attitude in still air -- the run went 2.5..2.8 unwinded and reads 2.5..3.2 with it.
CHECK all(2.4 < d["aoa"] < 3.3 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
# The first, uncoupled exchange: the bed gives 80 m/s of air and this hull trims near
# 41 m/s in #686's air (47..50 in the air this case was written against, because the
# bed's rho went 0.348 -> 0.502 kg/m3 and trim speed goes as 1/sqrt(rho)). So the arc
# bottoms at 40.7 m/s at t=19 s. The old floor of 60 sat above the OLD bottom and says
# nothing about this one; 35 keeps the claim that the hull trades speed for height and
# gets it back, with 5 m/s of slack, and still reddens if the exchange runs away.
CHECK min(d["v"] for d in drag if d["t"] <= 20.0) > 35.0
# The bed, not the airframe: flying-cruise spawns at 0.1 x the body's air top, and #682
# moved that top 70 -> 85 km, so the spawn row is 8502 m ASL where it used to read 7005.
# This floor is the spawn altitude less the 50 m the hull may fall before its wings
# answer -- it tracks the bed, so it moves whenever the air top does. It is not a sink
# measurement: the lowest row of the first 20 s IS the spawn row (8502 m), because this
# bed spawns above trim and climbs rather than falling. What it catches is the bed moving
# (a 70 km top puts the spawn at 7002 and reddens this) and a hull that falls 50 m before
# its wings answer.
CHECK min(d["alt_asl"] for d in drag if d["t"] <= 20.0) > 8450.0
# Static margin positive and steady (the VAB number, in flight): 0.550 on every row,
# +0.552 from the parts.
CHECK all(0.52 < d["sm"] < 0.58 for d in drag if d.get("sm") is not None)
# The kick: 6.2 deg of sideslip and back inside 0.1 deg by t=40 s. The same 1 s press
# bought 9.7 deg before #567 and 8.8 deg before that: the wing now carries 96 kg out to
# 8 m of tip, so the hull's yaw inertia is larger and one second of rudder swings less
# air around. #686 takes it a step further for the opposite reason -- the rudder's own
# torque is q x area, but the press now lands just past the bottom of the phugoid, where
# this hull is doing 45 m/s rather than the 58 m/s the old bed's phase put it. The
# ceiling is 12 so the case guards "it swings and weathervanes back" without betting on
# where one press lands.
CHECK 3.0 < max(abs(d["beta"]) for d in drag if d.get("beta") is not None) < 12.0
CHECK all(abs(d["beta"]) < 2.5 for d in drag if d["t"] >= 40.0)
# The bill: pod+tank+engine union 0.80..0.88 m2 at an area-weighted Cd of 0.57-0.60
# (nose-on: capsule drag_forward 0.35, tank 1.0, engine 0.15), wings at 0.010 profile,
# induced 0.10..0.51 as the throttle pushes it through its phugoid -- the top of that
# range is where a hangar-sized induced bill would start showing up as a wall. #543 A5
# took the top from 0.56 to 0.59 in the old air and #686 shrank the correction that
# priced it: at the fast end of the arc (79.8 m/s) the printed CLa is 173.82 against the
# 168.0 the parts author -- 3.5% of slope where it used to be 4.9% -- because that
# 79.8 m/s is Mach 0.260 in Kerbin's new 306.7 m/s air instead of Mach 0.305 in the old
# 275.0. The air got warmer, not thinner: 230..234 K at this bed, against the 188.0 K
# one scale height used to imply everywhere.
CHECK all(0.78 < d["a"] < 0.90 for d in drag if d.get("a"))
# The CLAIM's Cd pair is pinned beside the area it weights: 0.565..0.601 across the whole
# run, measured. A bill is Cd x A, so pinning one without the other leaves half the claim
# untestable -- which is how this row stayed green through a 1.44x change in the air.
CHECK all(0.55 < d["cd"] < 0.62 for d in drag)
CHECK all(0.35 < d["profile"] < 0.65 for d in drag if d.get("profile") is not None)
# None-guarded because #560 made Induced= dashable: every row of this run has a usable
# flow, so all 50 print a number, but max() over a dict comprehension that can yield None
# is a TypeError waiting for the first row that does not. The floor check below was
# already guarded for the same reason.
# The ceiling comes down with the run: 0.90 defended the OLD air's top, where #543 A5's
# compressibility correction priced 0.59. #686's arc reaches Mach 0.26 rather than 0.305,
# that correction is smaller, and the top of this run is 0.513 -- so a ceiling 1.75x the
# maximum it guards stopped being a wall. 0.60 is 0.09 of slack over the measured row.
CHECK max(d["induced"] for d in drag if d.get("induced") is not None) < 0.60
# ... and it is billed AT ALL. Smallest bill of this run: 0.100 m2, on the fast row
# where the wing is nearly unloaded. A ceiling with no floor stays green if the law stops
# charging -- an AR of zero short-circuits inducedCd in drag.h, and a pane that falls out
# of its lifting system is skipped by the bill -- while every other band here (aoa, sm,
# profile, a) still passes.
CHECK all(d["induced"] > 0.05 for d in drag if d.get("induced") is not None)
# -0.65 rather than -0.60: this run reads -0.44..-0.50, and the number worth keeping
# apart from it is the spindle's -0.68 -- a band that swallows that is no band. #570
# moved this floor 0.03 m on its own, so the margin against -0.68 is 0.03 m: re-measure
# rather than widen it again.
CHECK all(-0.65 < d["formcp"] < -0.40 for d in drag if d.get("formcp") is not None)
