# A load-keyed bed (#693): one airframe, three airs, three gravities.
# `flying-trim` does not author a speed and does not author a fraction of the body's
# circular speed (which is a GRAVITY number and has nothing to do with where a wing
# flies). It authors a LIFT-AREA LOAD -- q/g = 50.9 kg/m^2, which is skiff_trim's
# measured trim dynamic pressure 487 Pa divided by the 9.56 m/s^2 Kerbin pulls with at
# the bed -- and the speed falls out of each body's own air and gravity:
#   q = load x g(alt),  v = sqrt(2 q / rho(alt))
#   Kerbin  rho 0.502, g 9.54  -> 44.0 m/s   (the hull's own trim: 43)
#   Eve     rho 1.492, g 16.53 -> 33.6 m/s
#   Duna    rho 0.0110, g 2.85 -> 162.4 m/s  (its own trim: 150..230)
# Eve's row moved with #625: its air used to be 0.478 kg/m3 at 9 km and is now 1.49 at
# its 3.1 km bed, because a 292 K CO2 column on a 1.7 g world is compressed into a 3.3 km
# scale height, and its top came from 90 km to 31 km. Duna's moved too: its top is now
# the wiki's own 50 km (in band: 140 Pa of q), so the bed sits at 5 km, not 6.5.
# Is the bed's speed also the hull's TRIM speed there? Measured directly, one run each
# with --drag-log at the bed: L_up / (m g) is 0.88 on Kerbin (4757 N against 5424 N of
# weight) and 0.85 on Eve (8002 against 9397) -- the same hull, the same near-trim lift
# deficit, in airs 3x apart. Duna is the odd one: 2.0x (3254 N against 1622 N), because
# the bed there is Mach 0.73 and compressibility is lifting the wing. The old trim RANGES
# (43 Kerbin, 56..60 Eve, 150..230 Duna) were glide measurements in the airs before these
# tickets; Eve's scales as 1/sqrt(rho) to ~32..34, which is where the bed put it.
# Those are the three numbers this case pins, and they are GOLDEN, not invariant: they
# are readouts of rho and g at three altitudes, so they move if the air is retuned
# (#682 moved the bed heights once and #686 re-weighed the columns). What does NOT move
# is the authored load -- which is the point. A gravity fraction cannot say that at all:
# on Duna the same flying-cruise constant spawns the hull at 33 m/s, a fifth of the speed
# that hull needs to make lift there, and on Kerbin it spawns it ~1.9x OVER its trim.
# Authoring a q rather than q/g is the trap this nearly fell into: trim q = W/(S CL)
# carries the PLANET's g, so one q would be off by g_body/9.81 -- 2x under the lift
# needed on Eve, 1.8x over on Duna (both measured).
NAME trim-bed-speeds-follow-the-air
CLAIM A load-keyed spawn bed's speed comes from the body's air and gravity rather than from its circular speed: one authored 50.9 kg/m^2 lift-area load gives 44 m/s in Kerbin's 8.5 km air, 34 in Eve's 3.1 km air and 162 in Duna's 5 km at the same 0.1-of-air-top band -- within a few percent of the same hull's lift balance on the two subsonic worlds -- where the gravity-keyed cruise bed gives 33 m/s on Duna, a fifth of the speed that hull needs to make lift there.
KIND golden
ARGS --timeout 2 --startship k,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Kerbin,flying-trim --startship v,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Eve,flying-trim --startship d,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Duna,flying-trim --startship c,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Duna,flying-cruise
FORBID error:
FORBID needs air
# The three speeds, in order of the air they sit in. Kerbin is the bed the load was
# measured on; Eve's air is 3x denser at that band and its gravity 1.7x, and the extra
# weight it must carry is paid for in denser air, not in speed; Duna's air is 135x
# thinner and its gravity a third, and those do NOT cancel -- sqrt(g/rho) still lands 4x up.
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Kerbin.*\|v\| = 4[34]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 4[34]\.[0-9]", out)
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Eve.*\|v\| = 3[34]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 3[34]\.[0-9]", out)
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Duna.*\|v\| = 16[12]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 16[12]\.[0-9]", out)
# Same band on all three: Duna's air top is 50 km (its wiki row, adopted by #625 because
# it lands in band), so 0.1 of it is 5 km off its datum, r = 325000 m. Eve's top is 31 km,
# so its bed is at 3.1 km -- r = 703100 m, of a 700 km world. Without these pins, "it just
# spawned somewhere else" would explain the 4x spread away -- the wrong explanation.
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Duna.*r = 325000 m", out)
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Eve.*r = 703100 m", out)
# The contrast, same air, same band, gravity-keyed: 33 m/s where that hull needs 175 to
# carry its own weight. Over 40 here means the load keying stopped being air-keyed.
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-cruise' around Duna.*\|v\| = 3[23]\.[0-9] m/s", out)
# |v| == |v_air| on all three because no shipped body authors a wind. The bed authors an
# AIRspeed either way -- on the #688 windy fixture the same bed reads |v| 53.9 against
# |v_air| 44.0 -- which is what e2e flight/688-wind-bends-airspeed pins.
