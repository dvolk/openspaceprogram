NAME dock-approach
CLAIM A closing rate inside the pair's own gate mates it, and the weld keeps the gap inside that gate.
KIND invariant
# --dock-test approach puts the two ports 0.55 m apart on the station's port AXIS -- face
# gap 0.30 m -- and gives the probe 0.09 m/s along it. The r1 pair captures inside 0.10 m of
# face gap at 0.15 m/s or slower (#1152), so the mate lands about 2.2 s in, measured.
# The fixture used to fire the 50 kN engine for 30 ms instead. That arrived at 0.51 m/s --
# over three times the cap -- and the case was at the mercy of how many physics substeps a
# wall-clock press happened to catch (the flake this used to document). No keys are pressed
# here, so the mate has exactly one cause: the approach.
# The d=/cap= pair is the ticket. absorbShip is a rigid rebase, so whatever face gap the
# capture tick measures is the air that stays between the ports forever; 1.5 m of gate used
# to mean a docked pair holding each other at arm's length.
# cap= is the gate's own distance for this pair, printed since #1152, and it is pinned
# ABSOLUTELY at 0.10 m -- two r1 ports are 0.25 m deep, and the law is 0.4 x the shallower.
# That is the sentence which catches arm's length: with OSP_HULL_MARGIN=0.5 the pair mates at
# d=0.642 m under cap=1.250 m, and a bare `d < cap` reads GREEN on that (it is a restatement
# of the gate, which is exactly why the first version of this pin was wrong). The absolute
# figure is what says "not at arm's length".
# d is then pinned inside it with one print step of slack: d prints %.3f, and at
# --physics-hz 90 and above a coarser tick lets the pair cross in bigger steps, so d prints
# 0.100 -- equal to cap, not under it. Measured: 0.099 at 15/30/60/75 Hz, 0.100 at
# 90/120/200 Hz. The 0.05 floor says the pair flew most of the 0.30 m gap rather than
# starting already welded.
ARGS --dock-test approach --time-accel 1 --timeout 8
EXPECT Dock-test (approach)
EXPECT [dock] t=
EXPECT a="dockprobe" b="dockstation"
CHECK (lambda x: len(x) == 1 and 0.05 < float(x[0][0]) < 0.12 and abs(float(x[0][1]) - 0.10) < 0.02 and float(x[0][0]) <= float(x[0][1]) + 0.001)(re.findall(r'\[dock\] t=\S+ a="dockprobe" b="dockstation" d=([-\d.]+) m cap=([-\d.]+) m v=', out))
