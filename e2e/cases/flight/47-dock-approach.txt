NAME dock-approach
CLAIM A closing rate inside the pair's own gate mates it, and the weld keeps the gap inside that gate.

# --dock-test approach puts the two ports 0.55 m apart on the station's port AXIS -- face
# gap 0.30 m -- and gives the probe 0.09 m/s along it. The r1 pair captures inside 0.10 m of
# face gap at 0.15 m/s or slower (#1152), so the mate lands about 2.2 s in, measured.
# The fixture used to fire the 50 kN engine for 30 ms instead. That arrived at 0.51 m/s --
# over three times the cap -- and the case was at the mercy of how many physics substeps a
# wall-clock press happened to catch (the flake this used to document). No keys are pressed
# here, so the mate has exactly one cause: the approach.
# The d= pin is the ticket. absorbShip is a rigid rebase, so whatever face gap the capture
# tick measures is the air that stays between the ports forever; 1.5 m of gate used to mean
# a docked pair holding each other at arm's length. Anything under 0.1 m means the gate did
# what it now says.
