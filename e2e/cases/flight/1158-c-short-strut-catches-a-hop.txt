# THE OTHER HALF OF SHORTENING THE STRUT (#1158). 1158-a and 1158-b buy a smaller visible
# float by cutting leg_travel from 0.4 to 0.15, and the same number is the whole landing
# stroke: compression is clamped to travel, so a hard arrival that used to have 0.4 m of strut
# to spend now has 0.15. This case lands on it hard enough to find out, because "it stands
# lower" is worthless if the hull then bottoms out and tips.
#
# Stage after 0.5 s, throttle for 600 ms (T; the keys are SIM-clocked under --sim-steps,
# #472): the racer_legs hull reaches alt 11.5 m and comes back at 6.04 m/s (t=2.4). It does not
# tip -- worst nose.z 0.9927 through the rebound -- and it settles (|w| under 0.032 after t=5,
# 0.0000 by the end, |v| 0.01). The honest cost, which this case does NOT paper over: peak
# compression is 0.145 of the 0.15 stroke, and near-stop rows keep appearing between t=3.7 and
# t=5.4 as the hull rocks. At travel 0.2 the same arrival peaks at 0.186 of 0.20, so the strut
# is nearly spent either way; the real difference is the load ceiling (see 1158-a).
#
# And stroke length is NOT what saves a bad landing: measured arrivals of 15.7 m/s tip the hull
# (nose.z goes negative, |w| peaks ~2 rad/s) at travel 0.20, 0.15 and 0.13 alike. What a
# landing needs is a strut that stays spring-borne, not a longer one -- hence the telescoping
# strut issue, which would take the float out of the picture entirely.
NAME short-strut-catches-a-hop
CLAIM A legged hull that hops off its pads and comes down at 6 m/s does not reach its bump stops, tip, or keep bouncing.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --sim-steps 1200 --startship rl,res/ships/racer_legs.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --leg-log --dbg-log --att-log --no-paint --orbit-interval 0.2
ARGS --sim-press 500,0,SPACE,700,600,T
LIMIT 40
EXPECT [leglog]
EXPECT [attlog]
FORBID error:
# It really flew: 2.6 m of climb over the run (spawn rest 8.9 m), and a fast arrival.
CHECK max(d["alt"] for d in dbg) > 11.0
CHECK max(d["v"] for d in dbg) > 5.0
# The legs left the ground during the hop: the probe's reach is mesh+travel+0.2 = 0.4 m, so a
# gap of metres means the footpad is hanging in the air under a flying hull.
CHECK max(r["gap"] for r in leg if r["gap"] is not None) > 1.0
# The clamp never engaged: compression stayed under travel (peak 0.145 of 0.15), so the spring
# was holding the hull rather than the leg sitting solid on its stop.
CHECK all(r["comp"] < r["travel"] for r in leg)
CHECK all(abs(r["gap"] + r["comp"] - r["travel"]) < 0.01 for r in leg if r["comp"] > 0)
# Upright the whole way, including the rebound row (worst 0.9927).
CHECK all(a["nose"][2] > 0.98 for a in att)
# And it settles: no residual rock after the bounce, and standing still at the end.
CHECK all(a["wnorm"] < 0.05 for a in att if a["t"] > 5.0)
CHECK all(a["wnorm"] < 0.01 for a in att[-5:])
CHECK abs(dbg[-1]["v"]) < 0.5
