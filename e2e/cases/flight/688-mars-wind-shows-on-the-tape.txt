# Mars authors atmosphere.wind (#688) off the only fact sheet in this corpus that states
# a wind AT A LEVEL: marsfact.html's "Wind speeds: 2-7 m/s (summer), 5-10 m/s (fall),
# 17-30 m/s (dust storm) (Viking Lander sites)". Those are lander readings, so this is a
# datum row, and 10 m/s is the top of the two non-storm ranges -- the sheet's 30 is a
# dust storm, which is weather rather than a steady profile. See
# utils/make_solar_system.py beside the row; check_fact_wind refuses anything above 30.
# One row, so windAt() holds 10 m/s all the way up to the 60 km ceiling; flying-trim
# spawns at 0.1 x that ceiling = 6 km, reading the datum row's 10 m/s.
# What that does NOT do is push. Mars resolves 0.0155 kg/m3 at its own 636 Pa, so 10 m/s
# of air is 0.8 Pa of dynamic pressure -- a fortieth of what this hull pays at its own
# trim speed. The row still earns its place, because a wind is a VELOCITY and not a
# force (#688): the hull trims at 196.3 m/s of AIRSPEED and tracks 205.7 m/s over the
# ground, and it is the ground number that an approach, a landing march and a surface map
# are reading. The 9.4 m/s gap is the 10 m/s row projected onto the track (heading
# 110.6 deg, so 10 x cos 20.6 deg).
# The windless control (same ship and bed, this file with the row dropped -- tmp, not
# shipped) prints |v| = 196.3 |v_air| = 196.3. A flipped sign reads 186.9 with a
# westbound track; a table that refused to hold the datum row upward would read
# |v_air| = |v| here at 6 km.
NAME mars-wind-shows-on-the-tape
CLAIM Mars's datum wind row is carried all the way to the bed at 6 km: a hull trimmed at 196.3 m/s of airspeed in 0.8 Pa of moving air tracks 205.7 m/s over the ground and runs east off lon 0, even though the wind barely pushes.
KIND golden
ARGS --timeout 2 --info-log --system res/systems/solar_system.json --startship v,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Mars,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
FORBID error:
FORBID atmosphere.wind
# The launch state: 196.3 m/s through the air against 205.7 over the ground. A windless
# Mars reads 196.3 twice; a flipped sign reads 186.9.
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Mars.*\|v\| = 20[4-6]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 19[5-7]\.[0-9] m/s", out)
# Downwind, east off lon 0 (westbound would unwrap to ~359.99). The sign pin.
CHECK all(s["lon"] > 0.0 and s["lon"] < 1.0 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
# On the axis the table keys on (alt_asl, not alt_agl), and inside the band where the
# single datum row is what windAt() holds -- so 6 km reading 10 m/s is the row's
# documented reach, not a coincidence of the spawn height.
CHECK all(abs(s["alt_asl"] - 6000.0) < 400.0 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
# Ground speed stays between the hull's own airspeed and that plus the whole 10 m/s row
# (measured 205.0 at 0.8 s, 204.3 at 1.8 s).
CHECK all(s["hs"] > 196.0 and s["hs"] < 206.3 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
# #717: the readout states the split rather than leaving the player to infer it.
# wind_east= is the single datum row held up to 6 km (windAt()'s documented rule), and
# tas= is the air-relative speed the aero law bills -- 196 m/s, against 205 over the
# ground. On a body that authors no wind these two fields print "-" and become None
# here, which is what makes the dash a fact rather than a cosmetic gap.
CHECK all(abs(s["wind_east"] - 10.0) < 0.01 and s["wind_north"] == 0.0 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
CHECK all(s["tas"] is not None and s["tas"] > 194.0 and s["tas"] < 197.0 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
