# Venus is the first body in the catalogue to author atmosphere.wind (#688).
# Two rows: 1 m/s at the datum (venusfact.html's surface figure) and 100 m/s at
# 65 km (the cloud-top superrotation -- see utils/make_solar_system.py for what each
# one is sourced to, and for the sign). Interpolated, that is 25.4 m/s of air at
# 16 km, which is where flying-trim spawns:
#   v(16 km) = 1 + (16000/65000) x (100 - 1) = 25.4 m/s
# The hull (skiff_trim, whose lift-area load the bed is keyed to) asks for 6.1 m/s of
# AIRSPEED -- 50.9 kg/m^2 in Venus's 23.9 kg/m^3 of air at 16 km -- and the air is
# moving 4x faster than that. So it cannot make headway: it spawns going 19.2 m/s OVER
# THE GROUND (25.4 of air less its own 6.1) and drifts DOWNWIND, east off lon 0, while
# its nose points into the flow. A flipped sign in the rows reads 31.5 m/s and a
# WESTBOUND track instead.
# The windless control (same ship, same bed, the system as it was before this commit)
# prints |v| = 6.1 |v_air| = 6.1, so all of the 19.2 is the air's doing -- and the 6.1
# never moved, because a wind is an air velocity and not a force (#688).
NAME venus-wind-outruns-the-hull
CLAIM Venus authors a wind, and it moves the air rather than the hull: at 16 km the table interpolates to 25.4 m/s of air, so a hull that asked for 6.1 m/s of airspeed spawns at 19.2 m/s over the ground and drifts downwind (east, off lon 0) while pointing into the flow.
KIND golden
ARGS --timeout 2 --info-log --system res/systems/solar_system.json --startship v,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Venus,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
FORBID error:
FORBID atmosphere.wind
# The launch state: 6.1 m/s through the air (what the bed authored) against 19.2 over
# the ground (what the air is doing to it). Wrong sign in the rows reads 31.5/6.1; no
# wind reads 6.1/6.1; a wind sampled at the datum (1 m/s) instead of at the hull reads
# 5.1; a table that skipped interpolation and took the 100 m/s row reads 93.9.
CHECK re.search(r"Spawn 'flying-trim' around Venus.*\|v\| = 19\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 6\.[0-9] m/s", out)
# Downwind, not upwind: the ground track runs east off lon 0 (westbound would unwrap
# to ~359.99). This is the sign pin. The rows are ground-relative and +east is the
# sense the body's own spin carries the ground, which is what superrotation IS; the
# obliquity (177.36 deg) decides which orbital direction that turns out to be, not what
# east means. So the air runs WITH the ground and AGAINST a prograde hull.
CHECK all(s["lon"] > 0.0 and s["lon"] < 1.0 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
# Still at the bed, and on the axis the table keys on -- alt_asl off the sea-level
# datum, not alt_agl, which is terrain's business -- so the 25.4 m/s above is the row
# value where the hull actually is rather than a number that drifted with the hull.
CHECK all(abs(s["alt_asl"] - 16000.0) < 400.0 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
# Carried, not flying free: the ground speed stays inside the air's own 25.4 m/s all
# run (measured 19.7 at 0.8 s, 18.9 by 3.8 s). A wind that entered the readout but not
# the forces would let a 6.1 m/s hull climb away from the air's speed instead.
CHECK all(s["hs"] > 15.0 and s["hs"] < 25.4 for s in surf if s["t"] > 0.5)
