NAME elevator-trim
CLAIM The same 559 kg skiff, with 12 deg of elevator trim authored on the parts and nothing else changed, cruises at +5.0..+5.7 deg of angle of attack with the stick released: the download that trims this airframe comes from deflection, and the standing bill is real (0.47-1.02 m2 of induced CdA at the hold).
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff_trim,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 500,20000,T
ARGS --sim-press 25000,1000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [mass]
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
# The trim holds pitch up: every row is nose-high. Measured 5.0..5.3 deg while the
# phugoid is fast, 5.7 at its bottom (t=15 s: 29.7 m/s at 7378 m), and 5.1 from t=27 s
# on, once the throttle has damped the exchange. #568 moved this hold 2 deg up and
# 20 m/s down: the pod-on-tank stack carries its tailplane pair 1.65 m behind the wing
# rather than the spindle's 0.85 m, so the same 12 deg of tab buys more download than
# this hull wants.
CHECK all(d["aoa"] > 4.5 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
# The phugoid is real but bounded: it never decelerates past 30 m/s and it never
# trades more than 377 m of height in 50 s.
CHECK min(d["v"] for d in drag) > 26.0
CHECK max(d["alt"] for d in drag) - min(d["alt"] for d in drag) < 450.0
# 12 deg of standing deflection, and the bill follows the command: the hands-off
# rows bill 0.47-0.57 m2 of induced, the kicked rows up to 1.02. Pinned on the PITCH
# axis, because this run also kicks the rudder (+20.1 deg of yaw at t=3 s) and an
# unpinned "any surface is well off neutral" would pass on that alone.
CHECK any(c["axis"] == "pitch" for c in ctrl)
CHECK all(11.5 < abs(c["deg"]) < 12.5 for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch")
# The tail is one lifting system with its elevators (#556): its span is 4 m, so
# the standing bill is priced at AR 3.1, not at one 1.6 m tab.
