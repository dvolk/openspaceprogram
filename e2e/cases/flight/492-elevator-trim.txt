NAME elevator-trim
CLAIM The same 569 kg skiff, with 12 deg of elevator trim authored on the parts and nothing else changed, cruises at +4.8..+6.7 deg of angle of attack with the stick released: the download that trims this airframe comes from deflection, and the standing bill is real (0.34-0.54 m2 of induced CdA while the hull is pointing into the flow, 0.94 the instant the rudder is kicked).
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff_trim,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
# --no-paint: every assertion here parses [drag]/[mass] rows, so the 3D pass buys
# nothing and costs 95% of a scripted frame (#666).
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log --no-paint
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 500,20000,T
ARGS --sim-press 25000,1000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [mass]
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
# The trim holds pitch up: every row is nose-high. Measured 4.8..5.0 deg while the
# phugoid is fast, 6.7 at its bottom (t=14 s at 21.7 m/s, which is also the slowest row
# of the run: 7397 m), and 4.8..5.0 from t=27 s on, once the throttle
# has damped the exchange. #568 moved this hold 2 deg up and 20 m/s down: the
# pod-on-tank stack carries its tailplane pair 1.65 m behind the wing rather than the
# spindle's 0.85 m, so the same 12 deg of tab buys more download than this hull wants.
# #567 widened the exchange instead of shifting it: the floor of the hold is 0.2 deg
# lower and its top 0.3 deg higher, and the bottom of the exchange is 5.4 m/s slower
# (28.8 -> 23.4 m/s), because the trim download now has 24 m2 of AR-10.7 wing to answer
# it.
# #543 A5 (compressibility) widened it again rather than shifting the hold. The slope
# the hull flies is now the AIR'S: at the fast end of this phugoid (78.1 m/s, Mach 0.284
# in Kerbin's 275.0 m/s air) the printed CLa reads 174.54 against the 168.0 the parts
# author, and at the top of the arc (21.7 m/s, Mach 0.079) it reads 167.51 -- a 4.2%
# difference the old law could not make. The hold at speed is where it was (4.8..5.0),
# because a wing and a tail standing at the same Mach gain slope TOGETHER and the trim
# is a ratio. What moved is the bottom of the exchange: 6.2 -> 6.7 deg and 23.4 ->
# 21.7 m/s, because the stiffer fast end pushes harder at the bottom of the dive, so
# the arc swings deeper before the same download answers it.
CHECK all(d["aoa"] > 4.5 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
# The phugoid is real but bounded: it never decelerates past 21.7 m/s and it never
# trades more than 398 m of height in 50 s. This floor is NOT a stall floor any more,
# and that is worth saying out loud: res/ships/README.md's 23.1 m/s is arithmetic at
# low Mach (5.45 kN of weight over the authored slope at the wing's 20.1 deg stall
# angle, in 7 km air at rho 0.346), and the top of this arc lives at Mach 0.079, where
# A5's correction is 1.003x -- so the stall line has not moved and this run crosses
# under it. It may, because the slow rows are not equilibrium flight: at 21.7 m/s and
# 6.7 deg the law bills |L| = 1230 N against a 5578 N weight, which is a hull trading
# speed for height rather than one holding it. What the floor guards is the arc
# punching clean through the bottom of the exchange -- a wing whose slope DROPPED would
# fly a shallower one, and this row would rise -- so it sits 0.7 m/s under the slowest
# measured row rather than being tuned to it.
CHECK min(d["v"] for d in drag) > 21.0
CHECK max(d["alt_asl"] for d in drag) - min(d["alt_asl"] for d in drag) < 450.0
# 12 deg of standing deflection, and the bill follows the command: the rows that point
# into the flow bill 0.34-0.54 m2 of induced, and the single row in the rudder kick
# (t=26 s, Beta -8.5, Mach 0.248) bills 0.936 -- a steered surface pays induced drag at its own
# mount (#494). #567 took 0.13 m2 off the hold and 0.26 off that kick: inducedCd charges
# a lifting system (cl x A)^2 / (pi x 0.8 x span^2), and this went 12 m -> 16 m of span.
# #543 A5 put 0.02-0.07 m2 back on the hold and 0.08 on the kick: the bill is priced
# off the CL the law just made, so it SQUARES the slope correction. That accounts for
# most of it, not all: the kick row (Mach 0.248) prints CLa 172.74 where the parts author
# 168.0, so the slope is 1.028x and the bill 1.057x of what an uncorrected slope would
# price -- +5.7%, against the +9.9% the kick actually moved (0.852 -> 0.936). The rest is
# the arc, not the law: A5 also left this hull standing at a different AoA and q at that
# instant, and inducedCd prices those too.
# Pinned on the PITCH axis, because this run also kicks the rudder (Beta swings 8.5 deg)
# and an unpinned "any surface is well off neutral" would pass on that alone.
CHECK all(0.30 < d["induced"] < 0.60 for d in drag if d.get("induced") is not None and d.get("beta") is not None and abs(d["beta"]) < 4.0)
CHECK max(d["induced"] for d in drag if d.get("induced") is not None) > 0.75
CHECK any(c["axis"] == "pitch" for c in ctrl)
CHECK all(11.5 < abs(c["deg"]) < 12.5 for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch")
# The tail is one lifting system with its elevators (#556): its span is 4 m, so
# the standing bill is priced at AR 3.1, not at one 1.6 m tab.
