NAME elevator-trim
CLAIM The same 626 kg skiff, with the tailplane's mount incidence removed and 12 deg of elevator trim authored on the parts instead, cruises at +3.4..+11 deg of angle of attack with the stick released: the download that trims this airframe comes from deflection, and the standing bill is real (1-2 m2 of induced CdA at the hold).
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff_trim,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 500,20000,T
ARGS --sim-press 25000,1000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [mass]
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
# The trim holds pitch up: every row is nose-high, and the hands-off rows are the
# first 20 s (measured 3.4 deg there, rising into the A-press at the end).
CHECK all(d["aoa"] > 2.0 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
CHECK any(d["aoa"] > 8.0 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
# 12 deg of standing deflection, and the bill follows the command: the hands-off
# rows bill 0.3-0.5 m2 of induced, the kicked rows up to 2.6.
CHECK any(abs(d["deg"]) > 10.0 for d in ctrl)
CHECK max(d["induced"] for d in drag) > 1.5
# The tail is one lifting system with its elevators (#556): its span is 4 m, so
# the standing bill is priced at AR 3.1, not at one 1.6 m tab.
CHECK 0.25 < min(d["induced"] for d in drag if d["t"] <= 20.0) < 0.45
FORBID error:
