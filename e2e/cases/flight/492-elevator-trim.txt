NAME elevator-trim
CLAIM The same 569 kg skiff, with 12 deg of elevator trim authored on the parts and nothing else changed, cruises at +4.8..+6.2 deg of angle of attack with the stick released: the download that trims this airframe comes from deflection, and the standing bill is real (0.32-0.47 m2 of induced CdA while the hull is pointing into the flow, 0.85 the instant the rudder is kicked).
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff_trim,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 500,20000,T
ARGS --sim-press 25000,1000,A
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [mass]
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
# The trim holds pitch up: every row is nose-high. Measured 4.8..5.1 deg while the
# phugoid is fast, 6.2 at its bottom (t=14 s at 24.0 m/s, and the slowest row of the
# run is t=15 s: 23.4 m/s at 7402 m), and 4.8..5.0 from t=27 s on, once the throttle
# has damped the exchange. #568 moved this hold 2 deg up and 20 m/s down: the
# pod-on-tank stack carries its tailplane pair 1.65 m behind the wing rather than the
# spindle's 0.85 m, so the same 12 deg of tab buys more download than this hull wants.
# #567 widened the exchange instead of shifting it: the floor of the hold is 0.2 deg
# lower and its top 0.3 deg higher, and the bottom of the exchange is 5.4 m/s slower
# (28.8 -> 23.4 m/s), because the trim download now has 24 m2 of AR-10.7 wing to answer
# it.
CHECK all(d["aoa"] > 4.5 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
# The phugoid is real but bounded: it never decelerates past 23.4 m/s and it never
# trades more than 398 m of height in 50 s. The speed floor is worth pinning next to
# the stall row: 23.1 m/s is what the law says this hull can hold at 7 km (the stall
# arithmetic in res/ships/README.md), and the phugoid's bottom lands 0.3 m/s above it.
# A wing change that cut the lift slope would show up here as the exchange punching
# through the stall, so the floor sits 0.4 m/s under this run's slowest row and above
# the stall speed rather than a comfortable 22.
CHECK min(d["v"] for d in drag) > 23.0
CHECK max(d["alt"] for d in drag) - min(d["alt"] for d in drag) < 450.0
# 12 deg of standing deflection, and the bill follows the command: the rows that point
# into the flow bill 0.32-0.47 m2 of induced, and the single row in the rudder kick
# (t=26 s, Beta -8.4) bills 0.852 -- a steered surface pays induced drag at its own
# mount (#494). #567 took 0.13 m2 off the hold and 0.26 off that kick: inducedCd charges
# a lifting system (cl x A)^2 / (pi x 0.8 x span^2), and this went 12 m -> 16 m of span.
# Pinned on the PITCH axis, because this run also kicks the rudder (Beta swings 8.4 deg)
# and an unpinned "any surface is well off neutral" would pass on that alone.
CHECK all(0.28 < d["induced"] < 0.52 for d in drag if d.get("induced") is not None and d.get("beta") is not None and abs(d["beta"]) < 4.0)
CHECK max(d["induced"] for d in drag if d.get("induced") is not None) > 0.75
CHECK any(c["axis"] == "pitch" for c in ctrl)
CHECK all(11.5 < abs(c["deg"]) < 12.5 for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch")
# The tail is one lifting system with its elevators (#556): its span is 4 m, so
# the standing bill is priced at AR 3.1, not at one 1.6 m tab.
