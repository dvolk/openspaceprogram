NAME elevator-trim
CLAIM The same 552 kg airframe as skiff, with the tailplane's 5 deg of mount incidence REMOVED and 12 deg of elevator TRIM authored on the part instances instead, stays inside 6941-7001 m at 73.9-76.1 m/s while powered with the stick released (since #455, 100 m lower and 3 m/s slower than it used to be), stays at +2.4..+2.6 deg of angle of attack on every row of a 50 s run, holds -12.0/+12.0 deg of elevator deflection with no key pressed, reads the tail at the wing's own angle of attack (within 0.4 deg), and keeps its form-drag centre 0.57-0.63 m behind the CG -- so the download that trims this airframe now comes from deflection rather than from how the surface was bolted on.
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff_trim,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 3000 --drag-log --mass-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R,500,20000,T,25000,1000,A
EXPECT [ctrl]
EXPECT [surface]
EXPECT [mass]
# skiff_trim.json IS skiff.json except for two things: the tailplanes lost their
# "roll": -5/+5 (the incidence that #490 used to trim this airframe, a build choice
# the pilot cannot reach and cannot change in flight) and the elevators gained
# "trim": 12. Same 11 parts, same 552.33 kg, same hull.
CHECK mass[0]["parts"] == 11
CHECK 545.0 < mass[0]["mass"] < 560.0
# TRIM HELPS WITH NOTHING PRESSED. R ramps the throttle at 0.5 s and T latches the
# engine until 20.5 s; no W/S/A/D before the 25 s rudder kick. The airframe holds
# 6941-7001 m while powered at 73.9-76.1 m/s and never left +2.4..+2.6 deg of AoA in
# the whole 50 s -- including through the sideslip and the glide. The untrimmed,
# unincided version of this ship (#492's repro) sits at AoA exactly 0.0 and sinks:
# this law gives a control surface no say from its own angle, so with everything
# symmetric alpha = 0 is the only pitch equilibrium.
# RE-BASELINED by #455 from 7006-7087 m at 75.4-79.1 m/s. The trim itself did not
# move -- AoA is the same 2.4 deg it always was, and the deflection rows below still
# read exactly 12.0 deg -- but 28.6 m2 of planform now pays 1.09 m2 of Profile= on top
# of
# the hull's 0.53 m2 of CdA, so this throttle schedule leaves the airframe 100 m lower and
# 3 m/s slower at the same angle. The airplane part of the claim is intact; the
# performance part is what the itemised bill costs.
CHECK len(drag) >= 40
CHECK all(6920.0 < d["alt"] < 7020.0 for d in drag if 5 <= d["t"] <= 20)
CHECK all(73.0 < d["v"] < 77.0 for d in drag if 5 <= d["t"] <= 20)
CHECK all(2.2 < d["aoa"] < 3.1 for d in drag if d["t"] >= 5)
# WHAT THE TRIM IS DOING, READ OFF THE LOG. Every pitch surface is held at 12.0 deg
# of travel with no key held, and the mirrored pair carries OPPOSITE signs off one
# authored number -- controlDeflectionSign resolves it per instance exactly as it does
# for the stick, which is why the pilot's slider needs no idea how the part is mounted.
# The rudder row only appears during the 25 s kick (deg up to 20.1, its full travel).
CHECK all(abs(c["deg"]) > 11.5 for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch")
CHECK len({c["index"] for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch"}) == 2
CHECK abs(min(c["deg"] for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch") + max(c["deg"] for c in ctrl if c["axis"] == "pitch")) < 0.2
CHECK all(c["t"] is not None for c in ctrl)
# And #485's per-surface rows say where the download comes from now: the tailplane
# reads the WING's angle (0.2 deg apart at worst, because its incidence is gone) and so
# do the elevators, yet the airframe still trims nose-up. The deflection is not an
# angle of attack -- it is a separate term -- and this is the pair of facts that says
# the trim went into the law rather than into the mount.
CHECK len(surface) == 8 * len(drag)
CHECK max(abs(a - b) for a,b in zip([s["a"] for s in surface][2::8],[s["a"] for s in surface][0::8])) < 0.4
CHECK max(abs(a - b) for a,b in zip([s["a"] for s in surface][5::8],[s["a"] for s in surface][0::8])) < 0.4
# FLIGHT QUALITY: upright throughout, a 62 m/s bottom in the throttle-off exchange with
# nothing below 6206 m, the airframe's form-drag centre still 0.58-0.64 m behind the CG,
# and the wings still paying induced drag on their own aspect ratio.
# RE-BASELINED by #494's bill, and this is the interesting one: the run now tops out at
# 7036 m instead of 7097 and bottoms at 6401 instead of 6497. The trimmed tail used to
# make its download for free, so the same throttle schedule buys ~96 m less -- the
# honest price of a tab held at 12 deg for 20 s. The floor sits at 6350, 50 m under the
# measurement, because that margin IS the bill: put the free lift back and the whole
# run drifts up by ~100 m, and the induced pin below fails first anyway.
# RE-BASELINED again by #455: the top went 7036 -> 7001 and the bottom 6401 -> 6206,
# because the tail's download is no longer the only thing the tabs cost. Their planform
# (and the wings', and the fin's) now pays profile CdA at its own mount, which is
# 1.09 m2 of standing form drag at every angle of attack, and this airframe pays for it
# in the same currency #494 billed the downwash in -- the honest price of surfaces that
# turn air is that they also rub it. The floor moves 6350 -> 6150 for the same reason it
# moved before: that margin IS the bill, now a bigger one.
# RE-BASELINED by #503 earlier: the TRIM is 12 deg of elevator, INSIDE the surface's
# 15 deg deflection stall, so this airframe's trimmed state reads exactly as it did
# before -- the two tabs still bill 2 x 0.56 m2 x 6.0 x 0.2094 rad = 1.41 m2 of q*S.
# A player who trims past 15 deg is in the droop, where 5 more degrees of tab buys LESS
# download; that is the honest end of the sizing question #494 asks.
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.85 for d in drag if d["t"] >= 5)
CHECK min(d["v"] for d in drag if d["t"] >= 22) > 55.0
CHECK all(d["alt"] > 6150.0 for d in drag)
# The weathervane, itemised (#455). CpL is the BODY's silhouette lever now -- -0.20 m
# at the trim angle, -0.28 m at the worst row, still aft of the CG but no longer the
# 0.69-0.89 m this case used to quote, because the wings and tail left that union. The
# number that carries the old claim is FormCp: the hull's CdA and every surface's
# planform bill together, 0.58 m behind the CG at rest and 0.64 m on the sideslip row
# (#559 trims that to 0.57 / 0.63: the A stick still throws this airframe 8 deg of
# sideslip, and on those rows the wings used to be charged face-on for air that runs
# along their span).
CHECK all(d["cpl"] < 0.0 for d in drag if d["cpl"] is not None)
CHECK any(d.get("formcp") is not None for d in drag)
CHECK all(-0.72 < d["formcp"] < -0.5 for d in drag if d.get("formcp") is not None)
CHECK all(d["induced"] > 0.0 for d in drag if d["L"] > 1.0)
CHECK max(d["induced"] for d in drag) > 0.5
# THE BILL ITSELF (#494), pinned on the hands-off rows where nothing but the trim is
# moving: every one of them reads 0.45 m2 (0.447 when this was written; #455 leaves the
# induced sum alone -- it is computed off the lift loop, never off the silhouette
# union -- so the 0.003 that moved here is the airframe flying 3 m/s slower, not the
# bill changing), and with the deflected surfaces left out of
# the induced sum they read 0.265. The 0.182 m2 difference is the two tabs' own
# downwash, and it is the same arithmetic the wings use: each surface's own area and its
# own span, cl taken from the force the control law just made. 12 deg of elevator on
# cl_control 6.0 is cl 1.256 (inside the 15 deg stall, so the law is still linear
# there); elevator_r0.8h0.7 spans 1.60 m over 0.56 m2 of control area, so AR 4.57 and
# CDI = 1.257^2 / (pi x 0.8 x 4.57) = 0.137, times drag_cd 1.2 times 0.56 m2 = 0.092 per
# tab, 0.184 for the pair. The measured difference is 0.182, and the 0.002 is NOT the
# bill moving: a held deflection bills a constant of the catalog, because CL is
# recovered as |F|/(q*S) and the q cancels. It is the two LIFT sums -- 0.265 and 0.447
# come from different runs, and the second one's whole envelope sits 96 m lower.
# That is two thirds of what the LIFTING surfaces of this same ship
# pay at the same q (the 0.265 above), for a surface that is 1/19th of the wings' area:
# a short fat tab bills hard, and until #494 it paid nothing at all. Drop the sum, or
# bill it at the ship's span instead of the surface's, and this row band moves.
CHECK all(0.40 < d["induced"] < 0.50 for d in drag if 3.0 <= d["t"] <= 20.0)
