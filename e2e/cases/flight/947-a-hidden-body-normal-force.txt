# #947: the SIDE of a body used to pay for nothing. The axial bill charges the silhouette --
# the union of every part's shadow seen along the flow -- and a hull strip that some OTHER
# part covers from upwind votes for zero there, while the strip itself feels the flow
# arriving perpendicular to that silhouette, at up to cd x its full side area. A parked
# skiff's hull read Ld 0.00 in case 560 because there was genuinely no perpendicular force
# in the model at all -- for a body hidden inside its own silhouette, there never is.
#
# The law: each hull part refuses to swallow its OWN shadow. The fraction of its projected
# area the union does not credit it (1 - visible/own, cached columns from the SAME polygons
# so an exposed part cancels exactly -- tests/test_aerosweep.cpp pins that at real alpha)
# times the mesh box's crossflow strip (4 hz (hy|u.x| + hx|u.y|), exactly 2 r L sin(alpha)
# for a round part -- the sin lives in the geometry, not a second multiply), times the
# part's authored drag_side, answered against that part's OWN local flow (#483) at its
# mount. The force is perpendicular to the flow, so it gets its own log columns --
# SideCdA=/SideCp= -- instead of folding into CpL/FormCp, which are the along-flow bill's
# lever and cannot share a scalar with a line of action at 90 deg to it.
#
# WHY THIS CASE: the audit that re-checked #947's body found the suite had no ascent pin for
# this column at all -- 560 is a parked skiff and 490 a Kerbin cruise, and neither weighs a
# tall stack whose hull hides BEHIND its own pod. mars_explorer_mk1 is the hull the ticket's
# numbers were written against, and the fact worth pinning is the SIGN: the billed strip
# sits aft of the COM on every row, so the force is restoring. Pre-#947 the same climb let
# angle of attack drift past 3.8 deg toward 5.5 while the stack torqued harder the faster
# it flew; now it holds inside a degree and a half through the fat air.
#
# THE RUN'S SHAPE (re-measured 2026-11-27, so the windows below say what they weigh):
# scripted mode prints one [drag] row per log tick -- 24 billed rows, t = 10..240 s -- then
# rho = 0 dashes AoA and the side pair from t = 250 s on (#560's rule) and the hull is
# still above the air when the 4300 ticks stop near t = 481 s. The same ARGS used to print
# 53 billed rows: this stack now climbs out of the air and never falls back through it
# inside the window, so the fall rows the original measurement leaned on -- where staging
# moved the COM and the lever swung aft to -3.4 m -- are not printed at all any more. That
# shape change is not bisected (the band's near end is, below); getting that coverage back
# would be an --ascent/--sim-steps change plus a fresh measurement, not a wider band.
NAME hidden-body-normal-force
CLAIM On an autopilot ascent the hull's hidden side bills real strip (SideCdA up to 42 m2 at the pad pitch-over), its normal force always lands aft of the COM (SideCp < 0), and the hull holds inside 3 deg of AoA in the fat air instead of the pre-#947 drift toward 5.5.
KIND golden
ARGS --startship mars_explorer_mk1,res/ships/mars_explorer_mk1.json,Kerbin,pad --ascent 100e3 --time-accel 10
ARGS --drag-log --sim-steps 4300 --timeout 600 --no-paint
LIMIT 90
EXPECT Autopilot: ascent armed on 'mars_explorer_mk1'
EXPECT [drag]
FORBID error:
CHECK len(drag) >= 20
# THE DASH DISCIPLINE of the new pair: it rides the same lastDragAlphaValid gate as AoA,
# so a row may dash AoA only when it has no air at all. Measured: dashed rows are exactly
# the t >= 250 s ones, every one with rho=0.
CHECK all(d.get("aoa") is not None or d["rho"] == 0.0 for d in drag)
# THE LAW BILLS SOMETHING, at scale. This hull is 16 m2 of axial silhouette; its tube
# strips are far larger, and at the t=10 s pitch-over the stack flies ~6.6 deg broadside
# to its own speed low in the air: SideCdA peaks at 41.7 m2. 20 pins "the strip column
# exists and is not a rounding artifact" without betting on the exact wobble of one frame.
CHECK max([d["sidecda"] for d in drag if d.get("sidecda") is not None]) >= 20.0
# THE COUPLING: crossflow implies a bill. Every row flown more than a degree off the line
# carries at least 1 m2 -- measured >= 9.9. A run that kept this cheap by SKIPPING the
# crossflow while still printing AoA fails here instead of quietly billing nothing.
CHECK all(d["sidecda"] > 1.0 for d in drag if d.get("aoa") is not None and abs(d["aoa"]) > 1.0)
# THE SIGN IS THE CLAIM, on rows where the lever means something. Measured -0.94..-2.20 m
# across the 24 billed rows (SideCdA >= 0.5): -1.3..-1.4 through the pad pitch-over, a dip
# to -0.94 at t = 60 s, then aft steadily to -2.20 by t = 180 s as the stack thins and the
# COM moves off the hull. The band keeps 0.24 m clear at the near end and 0.30 m at the
# aft. A POSITIVE
# SideCp would mean the normal force moved ahead of the COM -- the hull levering itself
# further off its flow instead of weathervaning back, the failure direction #947 was
# predicted to fix. SideCp is deliberately NOT gated on aoa: at aoa~0 the strip shrinks
# toward zero (sin in the geometry) and rows below 0.5 m2 are exactly where a lever
# divided out of noise could wander -- the tail past the air is not here to be wrong.
#
# WHY THIS BAND MOVED, and it was not the aero law. Bisected (77 commits, tmp/bisect947.sh
# over this one case): first bad 2cf8364f "Weigh parts off their own mesh, not the cylinder
# around them (#1171)". Weighing the catalogue off its own mesh took 2,339 kg (2.5%) off
# this hull -- getMass 94568.94 -> 92229.84 kg -- and SideCp is a lever measured FROM the
# COM, so the whole band slid toward zero with it: -1.41..-3.35 at 1e43a18a, -1.12..-2.42
# at 2cf8364f, on the same log. Nothing in drag.h moved. The rest of the creep to -0.94 is
# the run's shape (above), not more mass. And note where the near end sits: both dips,
# t = 60 s (-0.94) and t = 190 s (-1.13), fall within two logged rows of where the climb's
# AoA crosses zero, which is where the upwind partition swaps hands -- so a shift in the
# guidance's pitch program moves this floor again before anything is wrong with the law.
CHECK all(-2.5 < d["sidecp"] < -0.7 for d in drag if d.get("sidecda") is not None and d["sidecda"] >= 0.5)
CHECK sum(1 for d in drag if d.get("sidecda") is not None and d["sidecda"] >= 0.5) >= 10
# THE FIGHT IS GONE in the fat air (rho >= 0.01, after the t=10 s pitch-over the guidance
# itself commands): the hull holds inside 3 deg of its line -- measured -0.6..+2.3, against
# the pre-#947 drift that grew 3.8 -> 5.5. The near-vacuum rows above the air are excluded
# on rho, not on time: at 2e-5 kg/m3 the aerodynamic authority that would carry any
# weathervane argument is gone, and the same rows there read -4 deg on a hull the air can
# barely touch. That is #960's guidance question, not this law's.
CHECK all(abs(d["aoa"]) < 3.0 for d in drag if d.get("aoa") is not None and d["t"] > 20.0 and d["rho"] >= 0.01)
# And the torque the whole stack feels stays inside the envelope the guidance was sized
# for: max |tau| measured 110 kNm on this climb. 300 kNm keeps the pin clear of the
# measured worst while reddening anything that levers the stack around.
CHECK all(d["tau"] < 300000.0 for d in drag if d.get("tau") is not None)
