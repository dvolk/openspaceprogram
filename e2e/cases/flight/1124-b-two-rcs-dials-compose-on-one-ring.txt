NAME two-rcs-dials-compose-on-one-ring
CLAIM The keys' dial and the pointing dial share one ring and compose instead of fighting: half the keys still asks 60 N, and the bells it leaves still steer at half (#1124, #1067, #595).
KIND invariant
# #595's priority, now with a dimmer on each end of it: translation spends the bells
# FIRST, and the attitude rows carry what is left -- which this case halves a second
# time. So both numbers move at once, in the same press window:
#   [rcs]     the keys ask 60 N (50 % of the 120 N the ring can bring to bear along +X,
#             #1119's per-direction ceiling) and each firing bell spends 30 N of its
#             60 N rating;
#   [steerlog] the R/P/Y authority reads 69/0/69 while nothing is pressed -- half of
#             the 138 N m the ring is worth -- and the YAW figure drops to 52 DURING the
#             press (69/0/52), because the two bells spending gas are the pair that
#             levers yaw and they have only half of their leftover left to steer with.
#             roll stays 69. rcs_hw= never moves: the ring is still worth 138 N m.
# Neither dial alone shows this pair moving together, and a dial that reached the
# RATINGS would put rcs_hw= at 69/0/69 and fail the same line from the other side.
ARGS --time-accel 1 --sim-steps 480 --startship rcs_box_pair,res/ships/rcs_box_pair.json,Kerbin,inertial-orbit --steering-log --drag-log --rcs-frac 0.5 --rcs-att-frac 0.5 --sim-press 2000,3000,N
EXPECT RCS: translation limited to 50% of what the ring can do that way
EXPECT RCS: attitude limited to 50% of what the ring leaves after the keys
EXPECT [rcs] cmd=(60 0 -0)N ach=(60 0 -0)N lim=50%
FORBID error:
FORBID rcs_hw=69/0/69
# The bell names ride in a CHECK, not the EXPECT above: a bracketed [-Z] reads as a
# character class to the case linter, which is right that EXPECT is a substring test.
CHECK "box_l[-Z]=30N box_r[-Z]=30N" in out
CHECK len(re.findall(r'\[steerlog\]', out)) >= 8
CHECK re.search(r'\[steerlog\][^\n]*rcs=69/0/52 rcs_hw=138/0/138 rcs_on=1 att_lim=50%', out)
# In that order, and so the 52 cannot be a one-off: the dimmed-but-idle figure comes
# first, the pressed figure after it, and the pressed one recurs for every tick of the
# held key.
CHECK out.index("rcs=69/0/69") < out.index("rcs=69/0/52")
CHECK len(re.findall(r"rcs=69/0/52 ", out)) >= 3
