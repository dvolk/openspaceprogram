# A split-off booster's ROOT part is the decoupler that made it (extractSubtreeAsShip
# re-roots the new ship at the cut). Pressing a stage on such a ship used to print
# "Stage: nothing left to separate", which reads as "this ship is out of stages" rather
# than "this stage cannot separate" (#541). The bed is the one 02 already uses: stage
# once to make the booster, hand control to it with F6, press again.
NAME booster-root-decoupler
CLAIM Pressing a stage on a ship whose root part is the decoupler says which part cannot separate, instead of claiming the ship has run out of stages.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 10 --startship stager,res/ships/stager.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --sim-press 2000,0,SPACE,4000,0,F6,6000,0,SPACE
EXPECT Stage: dropped 1 ship(s)
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2
EXPECT cannot separate -- it is this ship's root part, so its side is the whole ship
# The toast is the half a player actually reads, so the part has to be named there too.
# Game::toast mirrors every toast to stdout with a "[toast] " prefix.
EXPECT [toast] Stage 2 cannot separate
# The old generic line must NOT be what a player reads here: that is the whole point of
# the fix. ("Assertion" is already in run.py's DEFAULT_FORBID, so it needs no line.)
FORBID Stage: nothing left to separate
FORBID error:
