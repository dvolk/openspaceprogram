NAME static-margin
CLAIM The VAB's static margin and the flight log's SM are the same number: a trimmed skiff with 0.39 m of margin flies at +3 deg of angle of attack and stays wings-level through a 6 s glide.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 900 --drag-log
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 4
CHECK all(d.get("sm") is not None for d in drag)
CHECK all(0.30 < d["sm"] < 0.50 for d in drag if d.get("sm") is not None)
CHECK all(2.0 < d["aoa"] < 4.0 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.9 for d in drag)
FORBID error:
