NAME static-margin
CLAIM The VAB's static margin and the flight log's SM are the same number: a hands-off skiff with 0.55 m of margin flies at +2.5..+2.7 deg of angle of attack and stays wings-level through a 15 s glide (7000 m down to 6893 m).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 900 --drag-log
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 4
CHECK all(d.get("sm") is not None for d in drag)
# 0.55 m on every row (test_stability gets +0.552 from the parts alone). #568's
# pod-on-tank hull reads 0.18 m less than the one-piece spindle it replaced
# (+0.73), which is the stack's own COM walking aft -- the full tank and the engine
# hang 1.75-3.25 m behind the pod -- not a change in the lifting rig. #567 then
# grew the wing from 12 x 1.8 to 16 x 1.5 and the slope with it (153.6 -> 168.0
# m2/rad authored, and flight reads 167.82-167.84 only because of the cos(alpha)
# dip), which cost 0.026 m of margin. The mechanism is the weighting, not the
# chord: SM is the slope-weighted mean station, and the wing's slope grew at the
# FORWARD station -- (129.6 x -0.32 + 24 x -1.97) / 153.6 = -0.578 m of neutral
# point became (144 x -0.32 + 24 x -1.97) / 168 = -0.556 m. The 0.08 m band -- 0.03 m
# under this run's +0.55 and 0.05 m over it -- is what separates this from the spindle's
# +0.73 and from a rig that has started to diverge.
CHECK all(0.52 < d["sm"] < 0.60 for d in drag if d.get("sm") is not None)
CHECK all(2.3 < d["aoa"] < 3.0 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.9 for d in drag)
