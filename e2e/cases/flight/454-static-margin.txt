NAME static-margin
CLAIM The VAB's static margin and the flight log's SM are the same number: a hands-off skiff with 0.55 m of margin flies at +2.4..+2.6 deg of angle of attack and stays wings-level through a 15 s glide (8498 m down to 8267 m).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 900 --drag-log --mass-log
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 4
CHECK all(d.get("sm") is not None for d in drag)
# +0.55 m on every row, and the bench agrees with it: buildStability() on
# res/ships/skiff.json with one suit answers CLa 168.000 m2/rad and SM +0.5532 m. The
# air prints 170.17..172.04 for the slope -- the bench's 168.0 dressed by Prandtl-Glauert
# at this run's Mach plus the cos(alpha) dip in drag.h::LiftSlopeSum
# (168.0 / sqrt(1 - 0.219^2) x cos(2.4 deg) = 172.0 against the 172.04 the row prints),
# and nothing more.
# This row reads +0.55 again after a season at +0.31. #1171 weighs a part off its own
# mesh instead of the cylinder around it, which took 121.87 kg out of the POD alone: the
# COM walked 0.24 m aft and a margin defined as "how far behind the COM the lift change
# sits" fell with it (#1203 -- the hull's along-flow bill crossed in front of the COM and
# the airframe started levering alpha instead of weathervaning it). What put it back is
# not a re-scale and not a reshaping: it is an authored outfit allowance on the pod
# (#1204's interim, POD_OUTFIT_KG in utils/art/gen_parts.py), because a crew pod's seat,
# panel, life support and drums are mass that no closed shell mesh encloses. lift_area,
# lift_slope, radius and height are untouched; only mass moved, and the airframe follows
# mechanically -- a stronger margin needs less alpha to hold the same nose, hence the
# +3.6..+4.0 this row carried at 446 kg coming back down to +2.4..+2.6.
# Hence the mass pin below: it is the cause, and a band on SM alone would not say so
# next time.
CHECK any(m["ship"] == "skiff" and abs(m["mass"] - 568.11) < 1.0 for m in mass)
# The 0.08 m band -- 0.04 under this run's +0.55 and 0.04 over -- still separates this
# hull from the SAME BUILD with no crew: the bench answers +0.3714 m for the empty skiff,
# so one suit adds 0.18 m of the margin. Losing a seat reddens this, and so does a rig
# that has started to diverge.
CHECK all(0.51 < d["sm"] < 0.59 for d in drag if d.get("sm") is not None)
CHECK all(2.2 < d["aoa"] < 2.8 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.9 for d in drag)
