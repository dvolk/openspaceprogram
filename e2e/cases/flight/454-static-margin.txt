NAME static-margin
CLAIM The VAB's static margin and the flight log's SM are the same number: a hands-off skiff with 0.59 m of margin flies at +2.6 deg of angle of attack and stays wings-level through a 15 s glide (7000 m down to 6859 m).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 900 --drag-log
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 4
CHECK all(d.get("sm") is not None for d in drag)
# 0.59 m on every row (test_stability gets +0.586 from the parts alone). #568's
# pod-on-tank hull reads 0.14 m less than the one-piece spindle it replaced
# (+0.73), which is the stack's own COM walking aft -- the full tank and the engine
# hang 1.75-3.25 m behind the pod -- not a change in the lifting rig: both hulls
# bill the same 153 m2/rad of lift slope, and flight reads 153.0-153.5 only because
# of the cos(alpha) dip.
CHECK all(0.55 < d["sm"] < 0.65 for d in drag if d.get("sm") is not None)
CHECK all(2.4 < d["aoa"] < 3.2 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.9 for d in drag)
