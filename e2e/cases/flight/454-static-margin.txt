NAME static-margin
CLAIM The VAB's static margin and the flight log's SM are the same number: a hands-off skiff with 0.31 m of margin flies at +3.6..+4.0 deg of angle of attack and stays wings-level through a 15 s glide (8540 m down to 8475 m).
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 900 --drag-log --mass-log
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 4
CHECK all(d.get("sm") is not None for d in drag)
# +0.31 m on every row, and the bench agrees with it: buildStability() on
# res/ships/skiff.json with one suit answers CLa 168.000 m2/rad and SM +0.3122 m. The
# air prints 168.62..169.93 for the slope -- the bench's 168.0 dressed by
# Prandtl-Glauert at this run's Mach plus the cos(alpha) dip in drag.h::LiftSlopeSum
# (168.0 / sqrt(1 - 0.162^2) x cos(3.6 deg) = 170.1 against the 169.93 the row prints),
# and nothing more.
# This row read +0.55 at +2.5 deg until the masses moved, and what moved is the mass,
# not the lifting rig: #1171 weighs a part off its own mesh instead of the cylinder
# around it, so the skiff launches at 446.24 kg where flight/490 used to log 568.65 kg.
# The parts that lost most are AFT (tank and bell), so the COM walked aft and a margin
# defined as "how far behind the COM the lift change sits" fell 0.24 m. The airframe
# follows mechanically -- a weaker margin needs more alpha to hold the same nose, hence
# +2.5 -> +3.6..+4.0. The geometry did not move: res/data/parts.json changed no
# lift_area, lift_slope, radius or height in that window, only mass.
# Hence the mass pin below: it is the cause, and a band on SM alone would not say so
# next time.
CHECK any(m["ship"] == "skiff" and abs(m["mass"] - 446.24) < 1.0 for m in mass)
# The 0.08 m band -- 0.04 under this run's +0.31 and 0.04 over -- still separates this
# hull from the SAME BUILD with no crew: the bench answers -0.0001 m for the empty
# skiff, so one suit is now the entire margin (it used to add 0.18 m to a 0.37 m base).
# Losing a seat reddens this, and so does a rig that has started to diverge.
CHECK all(0.27 < d["sm"] < 0.35 for d in drag if d.get("sm") is not None)
CHECK all(3.4 < d["aoa"] < 4.2 for d in drag if d["aoa"] is not None)
CHECK all(d["up"] > 0.9 for d in drag)
