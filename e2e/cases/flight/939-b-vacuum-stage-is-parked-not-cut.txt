# The other half of #939's claim. A dropped stage on Moho has the same shape as
# the one on Kerbin and the same 5 km cull radius, and it must NOT be cut loose:
# vacuum means canRail() is true, so the proximity pass can park it past
# --prox-fly-off (frozen, forceless, silhouette-free) and it stays a landmark.
# The population the cull is allowed to delete is exactly the one the sim CANNOT
# park -- airborne inside an atmosphere -- and this case is what pins that the
# !canRail() guard is a guard and not a coincidence. flight/939-far-stage-is-cut-
# loose shows the fire; this one shows the non-fire on identical geometry.
#
# Two numbers moved when #1171 made stager 3516.62 kg instead of ~4.1 t. The ask
# is 220 km, because at 100 km (and at 160 km) the boost stage is still not dry
# when the law reaches Done on Moho, so nothing separates and this case pins an
# absence -- flight/928 works that arithmetic out. And --cull-radius came DOWN to
# 1000 m: at the higher orbit the dropped stage is parked soon after it clears
# the 2 km engage radius, so its LIVE proximity rows now reach only ~2.4 km
# rather than the 5.4-9.5 km the old geometry produced. With the radius at 5 km
# the FORBID below would have been true by geometry; at 1 km there are two LIVE
# rows past it, so the silence is the canRail() guard doing its job.
NAME far-stage-in-vacuum-is-parked-not-cut
CLAIM A dropped stage the sim is able to park is never cut loose.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship stager,res/ships/stager.json,Moho,pad --ascent 220e3 --time-accel 10
ARGS --ascent-log --prox-log --orbit-log --orbit-interval 5 --cull-radius 1000
ARGS --sim-steps 6000 --timeout 200 --no-paint
LIMIT 60
# The press happened, so the absence of a cut below is about a dropped stage.
EXPECT autopilot press: staged
EXPECT Stage: dropped 1 ship(s) / 3 part(s); now on stage 1 of 2
FORBID [cull]
FORBID Cut loose
# And the neighbour is still in the world while live. It matters that it is
# listed as LIVE rather than parked: --prox-fly-off is 10 km, so these rows are
# all well inside a release the sim has not granted yet. The non-fire is
# therefore the canRail() guard doing its job, not hysteresis happening to keep
# it close, and not the sim having given up on it.
EXPECT stager Decoupler (2m) dist=
EXPECT LIVE
# The teeth the radius came down to buy: at least one LIVE row with a four-figure
# distance, i.e. beyond --cull-radius. Measured 606.6, 1779.7 and 2379.3 m LIVE;
# after that the hull is on rails and stops being a proximity neighbour at all.
CHECK re.search(r'Decoupler \(2m\) dist=\d{4,}\.\d m rate=[+\-0-9.]+ m/s alt=\d+ m LIVE', out)
CHECK last(orbit)["peri_asl"] > 219e3
