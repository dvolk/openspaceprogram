NAME drag-ascent
CLAIM Drag reduces ascent acceleration and the drag log reports finite forces.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Duna,pad
ARGS --timeout 12 --drag-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 3000,2000,T
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 2
CHECK any(d["F"] > 0 and d["rho"] > 0 for d in drag)
FORBID error:
