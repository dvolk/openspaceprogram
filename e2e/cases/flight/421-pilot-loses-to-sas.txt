NAME pilot-loses-to-sas
CLAIM With --sas-policy keep, the same held pitch key against a latched prograde hold moves the nose by nothing at all: the law keeps its full demand and out-shouts the pilot on the steered axis.
KIND invariant
# #421 / #425 step D-2, the ALTERNATE end -- the pre-policy behaviour, now named so it
# can be chosen and pinned. Same ship, same key script, same log window as
# 421-pilot-owns-the-axis.txt; only --sas-policy differs. There the identical 1.5 s S
# press took the pointing error out to ~0.50 rad; here E reads 0.0000 rad on every row
# from t=1.5 on (0.0034 at worst, in the pre-SAS settling at t=1.1) and |w| never
# leaves the 0.003 rad/s it settled at, because the law's I*w/h ask swamps the pilot's
# 2200 N m of key on that axis.
# The pair is the point: the difference is the policy, not the ship, the keys or the
# window, and neither end is pinned anywhere else in the battery.
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --sas-policy keep
ARGS --att-log --slew-log --orbit-interval 0.3
ARGS --sim-press 1000,0,Z
ARGS --sim-press 3000,1500,S
ARGS --timeout 12
LIMIT 90
EXPECT [attlog]
EXPECT [slew]
FORBID error:
CHECK len(slew) >= 10
# Every row after SAS has settled, not just the two windows: the whole-run bound is
# the one that cannot drift with wall clock (these cases do not use --sim-steps).
CHECK max(s["E"] for s in slew if s["t"] > 1.5) < 0.005
CHECK max(a["wnorm"] for a in att if 3.5 < a["t"] < 6.6) < 0.02
CHECK last(slew)["E"] < 0.005
CHECK all(a["wnorm"] == a["wnorm"] and a["wnorm"] < 1e4 for a in att)
