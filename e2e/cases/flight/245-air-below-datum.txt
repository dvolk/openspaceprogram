# airDensity() used to answer VACUUM at or below a body's sea-level datum, and
# that single answer took drag, dynamic pressure, lift and jet thrust off a hull
# together (#245). The datum is radius + the authored sea_level (terrain.h), so on
# shipped content it is the bare radius and only a hull under the sea surface --
# crossing one of Kerbin's oceans at 50 m, since Kerbin's sea level sits on its
# reference sphere and half its terrain is below it -- could reach it. Now that
# #412 made the datum authored, e2e/fixtures/systems/245-kerbin-sea.json raises
# Kerbin's sea_level to 600 m and puts the space port itself 336 m UNDER the
# datum: the pad's alt_asl goes 264.0 -> -336.2 while its alt_agl stays 8.9, and
# bme flips to "ocean" (terragen.h: has_sea and alt <= 0 reads as Ocean).
# This case parks the skiff there and asks what the air is. Before the fix every
# [drag] row read rho=0.0, |F|=0.00 N, |L|=0.00 N and the jet printed thrust=0.0N
# -- a plane at the densest point of the model's atmosphere, in vacuum.
NAME air-below-datum
CLAIM At or below a body's sea-level datum the air is its sea-level density, flat: a hull under the datum gets rho0, its drag, its lift and its jet thrust.
KIND invariant
ARGS --system e2e/fixtures/systems/245-kerbin-sea.json
ARGS --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --info-log --drag-log --drain-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,3000,R
ARGS --sim-press 500,20000,T
ARGS --timeout 12
LIMIT 60
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 8
CHECK len(drainlog) >= 8
CHECK len(surf) >= 8
# THE WORLD: the space port sits under the datum on this fixture (it parks at
# alt_asl 264.0 on the shipped Kerbin, whose datum is the bare radius), so the
# hull never leaves sub-datum air in this run.
CHECK all(s["alt_asl"] < -300.0 for s in surf)
CHECK all(s["bme"] == "ocean" for s in surf)
# THE LAW: rho == the authored sea_level_density at -336 m. Flat, not the
# exponential continued downward (that would bill 1.98 kg/m3 here and make the
# deepest sea the densest air in the game), and not 0 (what it used to be).
CHECK all(abs(d["rho"] - 1.225) < 1e-3 for d in drag)
# IT PUSHES: the throttle press drives the hull 0.6 -> 18.6 m/s through that air
# and the bill follows (|F| 7.5 -> 3.1 kN at t=5.9s), where the sub-datum vacuum
# answer charged 0.00 N on every row of this run.
CHECK max(d["F"] for d in drag) > 1000.0
CHECK max(d.get("L", 0.0) for d in drag) > 1000.0
# IT BREATHES: jetAirGate reads rho/rho_sea, so the same answer starved the engine.
# The r0.25h1 fan is rated 5625 N at full gate; at -336 m it now runs at full gate.
CHECK max(r["thrust"] for r in drainlog) > 5000.0
# MUTATION: airDensity() back to `alt <= 0.0 -> 0.0` (the #245 law, with this fix's
# vehicle.cpp early-out already gone, so drag.h is the whole question) reddens the
# rho, force, lift and thrust checks together -- tmp/aero/n245_e2e_mut.log.
