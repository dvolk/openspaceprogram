NAME runway-brake-at-10x
CLAIM A gear hull that rolls up under thrust at 10x warp brakes to a stop and stays stopped.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 10 --startship skiffgear,res/ships/skiff_gear.json,Kerbin,pad,site=ksc_runway
ARGS --sim-steps 300 --dbg-log --orbit-interval 0.5
# Brakes are on at spawn: release (O), roll up under thrust, then brake (O) again.
# --sim-steps so the press schedule counts logic ticks, not the wall (#472): 1000 ms
# is 60 ticks whatever the accel, and at warp 10 that is 10 s of sim.
ARGS --sim-press 500,0,O
ARGS --sim-press 1000,700,T
ARGS --sim-press 2000,0,O
EXPECT [dbg]
# Why this case exists at warp 10 when 852 does the same story at warp 1: the substep
# here is 3 x 0.0556 s, the coarsest the shipped loop makes (warp >= 11 rides rails),
# and that is the only regime in which wheel.h's momentum clamp can bind (#770). Every
# other driving case ran at warp 1, where the criterion 2.7*g*h is ~0.04 and the clamp
# is provably dead code -- so the whole warp 8-10 rolling regime had no net under it.
#
# What it measures is that the clamp did not eat the rolling law. Measured here: 40.1
# m/s at t=15 s, 45.0 m/s at t=20 s (alt 2.3-2.8 m, so this is rolling, not flying),
# brake on at 2000 ms = 20 s of sim, 0.47 m/s at t=25 s and 0.01 m/s from t=30 s on.
# With the clamp forced off (in.stopRate = 0) the same run is identical to 0.02 m/s --
# the clamp cannot bind at 45 m/s, where tanh has saturated and the friction circle
# (kGrip*N ~ 1.7 kN) sits two orders under the momentum budget (stopRate*|v| ~ 180 kN).
# It binds at REST, which 859 pins. So this case's job is the negative: keep the moving
# hull's braking law working at the coarsest substep, where an over-damped tire would
# show up as a hull that never stops or stops and craws.
CHECK max(d["v"] for d in dbg) > 30
CHECK last(dbg)["v"] < 0.1
CHECK all(abs(d["v"]) < 0.1 for d in dbg[-4:])
# And it stays where the brakes left it: under 0.5 m of travel across the last four
# log steps (~40 s of sim). Measured 0.2 m.
CHECK (lambda ds: max(sum((b["pos"][j]-ds[-5]["pos"][j])**2 for j in (0,1,2)) for b in ds[-4:]) < 0.5)(dbg)
