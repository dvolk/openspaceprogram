# A bed can hand the hull a flight-path angle (pitch=) or an incidence (aoa=), and the two
# are not the same knob: pitch= moves the nose AND the velocity together, so the hull
# spawns along its flow at any flight-path angle, while aoa= is a hull-only turn about the
# wing span that leaves the path where heading/pitch put it (#1223). Nothing in the spawn
# asks used to buy an angle of attack at all, so every polar sweep started at AoA 0 and
# waited for the hull to swing to its own zero-moment incidence -- about a second, which is
# the row the measurement cannot use.
#
# The readout here is the Spawn line's own AoA figure, computed by drag.h's AeroFrame from
# the attitude that was just placed -- the same law the --drag-log rows use -- so the check
# cannot pass by the spawn and the readout agreeing on a private convention.
NAME a-start-ship-spawns-at-an-angle-of-attack
CLAIM aoa= hands the hull the angle of attack the aero law measures (+ = nose above the flow), while pitch= leaves that angle at zero and moves the flight path instead.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship a4,res/ships/spaceplane.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise,speed=120,aoa=4
ARGS --startship dn6,res/ships/spaceplane.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise,speed=120,aoa=-6
ARGS --startship p5,res/ships/spaceplane.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise,speed=120,pitch=5
ARGS --timeout 2 --drag-log
EXPECT [drag]
FORBID error:
# Asked 4 deg, read back 4 deg: the sign is the row's sign, not the hull axes' (+X is the
# pilot's LEFT, so the turn inside bedHullTurns is negated for the reading a player types).
CHECK re.search(r"AoA = \+4\.0 deg, asked state: v 120\.0 m/s air aoa \+4\.0 deg", out)
# And below the flow. A sign flip in the +X rotation reads +6.0 here.
CHECK re.search(r"AoA = -6\.0 deg, asked state: v 120\.0 m/s air aoa -6\.0 deg", out)
# The distinction the field exists for: 5 deg of path is NOT 5 deg of incidence. The hull
# climbs and its nose stays on the flow, so this line must read zero.
CHECK re.search(r"AoA = \+0\.0 deg, asked state: v 120\.0 m/s air pitch \+5\.0 deg", out)
# The incidence is a state, not an equilibrium: all three hulls are flying the same airspeed
# the bed was handed, and the aero pass has flow enough to measure it from the first row
# (parse_drag omits the key when the row dashes it, so .get() is the check).
CHECK re.search(r"\|v_air\| = 120\.0 m/s", out)
CHECK len([d for d in drag if d.get("aoa") is not None]) > 0
