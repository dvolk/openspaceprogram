# The case the periapsis gate got wrong: a departure burn can leave the
# periapsis under the surface while the ship is high, in vacuum and receding.
# Rails warp must be allowed; the player brings the warp down before the conic
# comes back to the air (case 310 is the automatic stop if they do not).
NAME rails-departure-periapsis
CLAIM Rails warp allows a high receding burn whose periapsis is under the ground.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,ellipse-low --time-accel 10
ARGS --timeout 8 --orbit-log --orbit-interval 1
ARGS --sim-press 1500,50,PERIOD
ARGS --sim-press 2500,50,COMMA
EXPECT @@@ racer parked on rails around Kerbin
EXPECT [toast] Time accel: 100x (rails)
EXPECT @@@ racer left the rails around Kerbin
EXPECT [toast] Time accel: 10x
FORBID Rails warp refused
FORBID frozen on rails
FORBID error:
# The premise: the conic really does meet the ground (periapsis at or below
# the sea-level radius), not a grazing orbit that would pass the old gate.
CHECK all(o["peri"] < 600000 for o in orbit)
