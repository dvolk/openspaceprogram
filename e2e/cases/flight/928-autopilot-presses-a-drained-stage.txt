# The ascent autopilot pushes the stage button. Moho again, and again on purpose:
# the point here is the PRESS, not the air, and on an airless hull a staging
# failure has exactly one cause. stager is two stages whose upper engine cannot
# drink the lower stage's tank (no fuel links in res/ships/stager.json), so the
# stack goes dry unless somebody separates it -- which is the whole difference
# between phase=done and why="the tanks are dry" (#928).
#
# THE ASK IS 220 KM, NOT 100 KM, because the hull got lighter under the case.
# The law presses on stage_due || dry (guidance.cpp:335), so a mission that
# reaches its ask with the boost stage's tanks still standing never asks, and
# #1171 -- parts weighed off their own mesh -- made stager 3516.62 kg. Measured
# on this build: at 100 km the flight finishes at t=562 s with the first stage
# still un-drained and no press at all; the same is true at 160 km (t=737.8 s,
# still no press). At 220 km the boost stage drains mid-climb and the press lands
# at t=891.3s reading "(stage drained + dry)" -- empty stage, nothing to burn --
# with the verdict 18 s later. So the ask moved to keep the premise, not the
# numbers pretty. Kerbin with the same hull and the same 100 km ask still presses
# at t=73.7s (flight/939-far-stage-is-cut-loose): the difference is Moho's well,
# not a law that stopped asking. The hull mass is pinned below so the next
# #1171-shaped retune reddens THAT line instead of losing the press silently.
NAME autopilot-presses-a-drained-stage
CLAIM The ascent autopilot separates a drained stage by itself and still makes the ask.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship stager,res/ships/stager.json,Moho,pad --ascent 220e3 --time-accel 10
ARGS --ascent-log --orbit-log --orbit-interval 1 --mass-log
ARGS --sim-steps 6000 --timeout 200 --no-paint
# 6000 logic ticks is ~1000 s of sim at warp 10 (0.1667 s of sim a tick), and the
# Done verdict hands the clock back to 1x (#612). The verdict lands at ~910 s, so
# ~5400 ticks buy the flight and the last ~600 ticks are worth ~10 s: enough
# margin to see a stall, not enough to run the case long.
LIMIT 60
EXPECT Autopilot: ascent armed on 'stager'
# The press, and the press ACCEPTED: stageVehicle() answers with an outcome and
# tick.cpp prints it, because a separation the player cannot see is a mission
# that quietly stops staging. "now on stage 1 of 2" is the ship's own count, so a
# press that dropped the wrong seam fails here instead of flying a different
# rocket.
EXPECT autopilot press: staged
EXPECT Stage: dropped 1 ship(s) / 3 part(s); now on stage 1 of 2
FORBID autopilot press: refused
FORBID phase=failed
# The mass the ask was sized against (3516.62 kg spawn, 7 parts; the dropped
# stage is 633.03 kg of it). Red here means 220 km is no longer a two-stage hop
# on Moho, so re-derive the ask -- see the header -- rather than widen the bands.
CHECK len(mass) >= 4
CHECK abs(mass[0]["mass"] - 3516.62) < 1.0
# The mission the press exists for: the press row above says the stage was dry,
# so without it the law has no thrust and the phase ends Failed with "the tanks
# are dry". "done" is the staging result, not a coincidence of the profile.
CHECK last(ascent)["phase"] == "done"
CHECK last(ascent)["why"] == "periapsis at the ask, apoapsis inside the band"
CHECK last(orbit)["peri_asl"] > 219e3
CHECK abs(last(orbit)["apo_asl"] - 220e3) < 5e3
CHECK last(ascent)["dv_exp"] > 1050.0
# The lever is closed on the tick the mission finished -- the same pin the 610
# case makes, restated here because a hull that has just split in two is the more
# likely place to leave a burn running.
CHECK last(ascent)["lever"] == 0.0
