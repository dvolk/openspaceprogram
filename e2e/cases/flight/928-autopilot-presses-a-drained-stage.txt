# The ascent autopilot pushes the stage button. Moho again, and again on purpose:
# the point here is the PRESS, not the air, and on an airless hull a staging
# failure has exactly one cause. stager is two stages whose upper engine cannot
# drink the lower stage's tank (no fuel links in res/ships/stager.json), so the
# stack goes dry at ~100 km unless somebody separates it -- which is the whole
# difference between phase=done and why="the tanks are dry" (#928).
NAME autopilot-presses-a-drained-stage
CLAIM The ascent autopilot separates a drained stage by itself and still makes the ask.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship stager,res/ships/stager.json,Moho,pad --ascent 100e3 --time-accel 10
ARGS --ascent-log --orbit-log --orbit-interval 1
ARGS --sim-steps 6000 --timeout 200 --no-paint
# 6000 logic ticks covers ~607 s of sim: the climb buys its time at warp 10 and
# the Done verdict hands the clock back to 1x (#612), so the last ~1300 ticks are
# worth ~20 s. The verdict lands at ~587 s, which leaves ~20 s of sim and ~1300
# ticks of margin -- the same shape as the 610 case, sized off its own flight
# rather than guessed.
LIMIT 60
EXPECT Autopilot: ascent armed on 'stager'
# The press, and the press ACCEPTED: stageVehicle() answers with an outcome and
# tick.cpp prints it, because a separation the player cannot see is a mission
# that quietly stops staging. "now on stage 1 of 2" is the ship's own count, so a
# press that dropped the wrong seam fails here instead of flying a different
# rocket.
EXPECT autopilot press: staged
EXPECT Stage: dropped 1 ship(s) / 3 part(s); now on stage 1 of 2
FORBID autopilot press: refused
FORBID phase=failed
# The mission the press exists for. Without it the law fails dry at ~936 m/s of
# delta-v with periapsis ~218 km underground, so "done" is the staging result and
# not a coincidence of the profile.
CHECK last(ascent)["phase"] == "done"
CHECK last(ascent)["why"] == "periapsis at the ask, apoapsis inside the band"
CHECK last(orbit)["peri_asl"] > 99e3
CHECK abs(last(orbit)["apo_asl"] - 100e3) < 5e3
CHECK last(ascent)["dv_exp"] > 1050.0
# The lever is closed on the tick the mission finished -- the same pin the 610
# case makes, restated here because a hull that has just split in two is the more
# likely place to leave a burn running.
CHECK last(ascent)["lever"] == 0.0
