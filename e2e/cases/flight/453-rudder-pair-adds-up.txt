NAME rudder-pair-adds-up
CLAIM The jet's two rudders are bolted the other way round, so one stick drives them to OPPOSITE printed deflections and they still push the SAME way: a held rudder throws the airframe off the centreline instead of cancelling itself out.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship jet,res/ships/jet.json,Kerbin,flying-slow
ARGS --sim-steps 300 --drag-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,3000,D
EXPECT [drag]
EXPECT [ctrl]
# The pair's fingerprint: a fin and its twin, mounted 180 deg apart, present
# opposite plate normals, so the same ship-axis command prints antisymmetric
# deflections and both still reach the travel stop (0.35 rad = 20.1 deg).
CHECK len(drag) >= 4
CHECK any(a["deg"] < -15.0 and b["deg"] > 15.0 for a in ctrl for b in ctrl if a["t"] == b["t"] and a["axis"] == "yaw" and b["axis"] == "yaw" and a["index"] != b["index"])
# And the pair still turns the airframe: 3 s of full pedal from 176 m/s takes Beta
# past 30 deg inside a second and past 90 deg by the second -- the shipped trainer
# departs under a held pedal at cruise, which is what a full-rudder hold does. The
# pin is the DIRECTION and the SIZE, not a recovery: a pair that cancelled reads ~0.
CHECK max(abs(d["beta"]) for d in drag if d.get("beta") is not None) > 50.0
