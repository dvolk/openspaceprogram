# Every pad ship stands ON its site deck. Ships used to be nudged 20 m
# sideways per (body, scenario) slot, back when spawning one also invented a
# pad under it; with the pads as world content that offset walked each ship
# past the deck edge (space_port.obj's top face is a hexagon out to x = 8.66)
# and left it hanging at deck height over bare terrain, up to 200 m from the
# site for the debug fleet. So both vessels here read the pad's authored
# lat/lon (main_space_port: 0.286473, 270.286477) -- 1e-3 deg is ~10 m on
# Kerbin, and the old slot-1 offset alone was 20 m.
#
# Occupancy itself is deliberately unhandled (#231): two ships on one pad
# share the pose, and since they sit inside each other's 10 m grounded
# proximity radius they wake and shove -- hence the 10 m bound rather than a
# tight one, and --orbit-interval 0.25 so the rows sample the whole settle.
# The debug fleet's startships.json is knowingly a pile for the same reason.
NAME pad-ships-share-the-deck
CLAIM Every pad ship stands on its site deck, not beside or above it.
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 5 --info-log --orbit-interval 0.25 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad --startship tanker,res/ships/tanker.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --sim-press 2000,50,F6
# Two ships, one seat each: the seated kerbals are seats on their capsules, not
# vessels (part.h), so the fleet counts 2 and F6 steps racer -> tanker.
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2: tanker
FORBID error:
CHECK len(surf) > 10
CHECK all(abs(s["lat"] - 0.286473) < 1e-3 and abs(s["lon"] - 270.286477) < 1e-3 for s in surf)
CHECK all(s["sit"] == "landed" for s in surf)
