NAME roll-ailerons
CLAIM A plane rolls with its ailerons, not the reaction wheel: a pair of ailerons at the skiff's wing trailing edge answers the roll stick and banks it past 30 deg in two seconds, where only the reaction wheel could before.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 480 --drag-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,Q
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 5
# Up is the ship's up axis against the world's: 1.00 wings-level, 0.84 at ~33 deg.
# The pair is 0.56 m2 per side at the trailing edge; the mount convention that makes
# it lie FLAT is a spanwise `normal` (the child's srf node normal is its span, so a
# +Y normal stands the aileron on edge -- that is #566's lesson).
# Beta stays inside a degree: a correctly oriented aileron rolls without yawing.
CHECK min(d["up"] for d in drag if d["t"] >= 2.0) < 0.90
CHECK all(abs(d["beta"]) < 3.0 for d in drag if d.get("beta") is not None)
# And it comes back: release the stick and the airframe holds its bank (no
# self-righting), which is what a plane does.
CHECK all(-1.0 <= d["up"] <= 1.0 for d in drag)
