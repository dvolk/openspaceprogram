NAME roll-ailerons
CLAIM A plane rolls with its ailerons, not the reaction wheel: two ailerons on the skiff's wing answer the roll stick, and 2 s of Q banks it past 20 deg without the wheel.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 480 --drag-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,Q
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 5
# Up is the ship's up axis against the world's: 1.00 wings-level, 0.94 at 20 deg.
# The wheel alone cannot do this in 2 s (it is the reason the gap was noticed).
CHECK min(d["up"] for d in drag if d["t"] >= 2.0) < 0.95
# And it comes back: release the stick and the airframe holds its bank (no
# self-righting), which is what a plane does.
CHECK all(-1.0 <= d["up"] <= 1.0 for d in drag)
FORBID error:
