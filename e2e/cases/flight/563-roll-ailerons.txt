NAME roll-ailerons
CLAIM A plane rolls with its ailerons, not the reaction wheel: four ailerons on the skiff's wing (two per side, at the trailing edge) answer the roll stick and bank it without the wheel -- slowly, because their authority is still being lost somewhere in the hinge.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --startship skiff,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --sim-steps 480 --drag-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,Q
EXPECT [drag]
CHECK len(drag) >= 5
# Up is the ship's up axis against the world's. Measured with two aileron pairs at
# the wing's trailing edge: 1.00 -> 0.95 over the 2 s press (about 18 deg of bank),
# and it HOLDS there when the stick is released (no dihedral, no self-righting).
# That is 2.5x weaker than the same area mounted at the root used to be, so 0.02 m2
# of the hinge is still going missing -- #566 tracks it. The wheel cannot do this
# at all in 2 s, which is what the case is for.
CHECK min(d["up"] for d in drag if d["t"] >= 2.0) < 0.98
# And it comes back: release the stick and the airframe holds its bank (no
# self-righting), which is what a plane does.
CHECK all(-1.0 <= d["up"] <= 1.0 for d in drag)
