# The other half of the #876 blend: a FLYING hull must keep its own frame, or the
# fix would have just replaced one bad frame with another (a rocket banking in
# air whose camera ignores the bank). Keys and the alt pin follow
# flight/03-thrust-ascent, so the case cannot pass on a ship that never left the
# pad. gf= is the ground-frame weight, ax1= the turntable's yaw axis against the
# HULL's lateral axis 1 (0 deg = the hull holds the frame, 90 deg = the surface
# frame does):
#   first rows gf=+1.000 and ax1 ~90: the run starts parked, so the turntable is
#     the surface frame and has nothing of the hull in it -- see
#     flight/876-pad-camera-horizon-frame.
#   min gf < 0.05 and, on those rows, ax1 under 10 deg: once she is moving,
#     isGrounded() goes false and the damped weight hands the frame back to the
#     hull within about a second. ax1 rather than pole, because pole only says
#     90 while the hull stays vertical -- this hop's arc drifts it to 98 deg by
#     t=8 for reasons that have nothing to do with the blend.
#   every row after t=5 keeps gf < 0.05, which is the real question. This key
#     sequence HOPS: the apex near t=6.5 sits at 39 m where the hull crawls under
#     kShipGroundSpeed (|v| = 2.0 m/s), so isGrounded() reads true for about a
#     second and the raw reading pulled gf to +0.691 -- the chase camera swung
#     60 deg around the ship and back inside 1.5 s. The latch now needs
#     kCamSettleS of stillness to enter the surface frame, so a hop apex never
#     engages it and gf stays pinned at +0.000 through it.
# Not pinned here: roll. In the hull frame the local vertical is broadside, which
# is the ill-conditioned reading (atan2 of two near-zeros), and what the HUD does
# with that while the hull rolls is #876's open HUD question, not this one.
NAME flying-hull-holds-its-frame
CLAIM An airborne ship's turntable returns to the hull frame and stays there: the ground-frame weight decays to zero, the hull's own axes hold the turntable, and a slow low apex does not swing the frame back.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --dbg-log --cam-log --orbit-interval 0.5
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 3000,2000,T
EXPECT [camlog]
CHECK last(dbg)["alt"] - first(dbg)["alt"] > 10
CHECK first(cam)["gf"] > 0.99
CHECK all(r["ax1"] is not None and r["ax1"] > 89.0 for r in cam if r["gf"] is not None and r["gf"] > 0.99)
CHECK min(r["gf"] for r in cam if r["gf"] is not None) < 0.05
CHECK all(r["ax1"] is not None and abs(r["ax1"]) < 10.0 for r in cam if r["gf"] is not None and r["gf"] < 0.05)
CHECK all(r["gf"] is not None and r["gf"] < 0.05 for r in cam if r["t"] > 5.0)
FORBID error:
