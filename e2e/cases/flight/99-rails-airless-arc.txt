# A suborbital arc is a valid conic: rails warp accepts it even with the
# osculating periapsis under the surface (canRail no longer reads periapsis).
# Moho has no air, so the guard that stops it is the ground: the ship parks
# frozen where the conic meets the terrain and warp drops to 1x.
NAME rails-airless-arc-stop
CLAIM Rails warp accepts a suborbital arc on an airless body and parks it at the ground.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Moho,pad --time-accel 10
ARGS --timeout 10 --dbg-log
ARGS --sim-press 500,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 1500,3000,T
ARGS --sim-press 5000,50,PERIOD
ARGS --sim-press 5500,50,PERIOD
EXPECT @@@ racer parked on rails around Moho
EXPECT Rails: 'racer' hit the ground, warp 1x
FORBID Rails warp refused
FORBID frozen on rails
FORBID error:
# The stop is a ground park: at rest, on the terrain.
CHECK last(dbg)["alt"] < 1 and last(dbg)["v"] < 1
