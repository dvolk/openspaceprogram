# A press must not cost the player their clock. Pre-fix, THIS geometry printed
# "Close approach: time accel limited to 1x" on the tick of the press (the newborn
# stage is inside the 2 km engage radius, so the proximity pass counted the flight as
# engaged) and every sim second after it cost a wall second. The FORBIDs below are the
# claim; they fail against the pre-fix log at t=563.7s.
# Moho rather than Kerbin because it is cheap (1 s of wall) and because the cause is
# the same on both: liveness, not distance, was the trigger. On Kerbin the rule is
# worse than slow -- an airborne stage can NEVER be parked (canRail() is
# isGrounded() || !inAtmosphere()), so the cap does not lift for the rest of the
# flight, however far the booster gets (#937).
#
# The ask is 220 km, not the 100 km this case was written against, and the reason
# is not the warp law: #1171 weighs parts off their own mesh, stager is 3516.62 kg,
# and on Moho a 100 km hop no longer drains the boost stage -- so the autopilot
# never presses and the FORBIDs below would be pinning an absence. At 220 km the
# press lands at t=891.3s and the cap question is asked again, 18 s before the
# verdict. flight/928 works the arithmetic out; this case only needs the press to
# happen, so it does not restate it.
NAME staging-keeps-the-warp
CLAIM Pressing the stage does not put the clock at 1x.
KIND invariant
ARGS --startship stager,res/ships/stager.json,Moho,pad --ascent 220e3 --time-accel 10
ARGS --ascent-log --prox-log --orbit-log --orbit-interval 5 --sim-steps 6000 --timeout 200 --no-paint
LIMIT 60
# The press happened, so the FORBIDs below are about a press and not about a flight
# that never staged at all.
EXPECT autopilot press: staged
EXPECT Stage: dropped 1 ship(s) / 3 part(s); now on stage 1 of 2
FORBID Close approach
FORBID warp CAPPED
# And the flight is still a flight: the cap was never the thing holding it up.
CHECK last(ascent)["phase"] == "done"
CHECK last(orbit)["peri_asl"] > 219e3
