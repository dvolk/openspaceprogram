# #1097: the hull itself can LIFT. A body part's air forces used to be the axial union
# bill (anti-parallel to the flow by construction) plus, since #947, a crossflow normal
# force on only the fraction of its own shadow the union REFUSED. A fully exposed hull
# at angle of attack paid nothing perpendicular -- so a wingless lifting body could not
# fly and the Shuttle's belly trim did not exist. This case is the ticket's claim made
# playable: a 4 m flat plate with an authored body_lift (liftbody_4m in res/data/parts.json
# -- the first part in the catalog to opt in; every pre-existing def stays at 0), no wings,
# no tails, no pilot, spawned prograde into 8.5 km air and left completely alone.
#
# The hull is deliberately ONE part: the force acts at the part's mount, so its arm is
# zero and there is no pitch moment at all -- attitude stays exactly where the bed put it
# and the angle of attack grows only as gravity bends the flight path under it. That
# makes the run readable: AoA is 22 deg at the first logged row and climbs as gravity
# steepens the path beneath the plate, and the whole story is the vertical force balance.
#
# THE RUN'S SHAPE (measured): 800 scripted ticks at time-accel 4 print 13 [drag] rows
# 4 sim-seconds apart, t=4 s to t=52 s. At t=4 the hull flies 21.7 deg AoA with
# SideCdA 0.00 / BodyCdA +1.56: the #947 law acts on this hull NOTHING (every part
# exposed, refused floored to zero) while the #1097 law acts on everything it sees --
# the exposed half of the two laws' split, in the shipped log (the middle and hidden
# halves are pinned in test_aerosweep). |F| settles to ~1.2 kN and holds against the
# 1.26 kN weight (128 kg) for the rest of the fall; Ld stays positive throughout
# (1.29 -> 0.08 as the flow swings under the plate, BodyCdA -- a force meter now, q x
# it is the summed lift's size -- peaking +3.29 at t=20 then decaying to +0.67), and
# the 48 s of flying lose 1.0 km of altitude where a ballistic drop would have lost
# ~11 km. With one part there is no restoring moment, so the plate never trims at a
# fixed AoA -- a hull that cruises level needs tails, and that balance is a bed of
# its own, not this law's job.
NAME wingless-lifting-body
CLAIM A flat plate with an authored body_lift and no lifting surfaces flies: BodyCdA > 0 on every row while SideCdA stays 0.00 (the exposed half of the #947/#1097 split) and the surface-lift column |L| stays 0, Ld > 0, and the sink is bounded far short of ballistic.
KIND golden
ARGS --startship liftbody,res/ships/liftbody.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
ARGS --drag-log --time-accel 4 --sim-steps 800 --timeout 300 --no-paint
LIMIT 90
EXPECT [drag]
FORBID error:
CHECK len(drag) >= 10
# THE BILL EXISTS, at a scale that is not rounding. BodyCdA folds the sin in (q x it
# is the summed body-lift force's SIZE): the plate's silhouette on the row (A=) is
# 4.2..6.6 m2 and body_lift 0.9 x drag_cd 1.2 x sin x cos puts it at +1.56 on the
# first row, peaking +3.29 at t=20, decaying to +0.67 at t=52 as the near-vertical
# fall drives cos -> 0. The 0.5 floor keeps that honest tail in with a quarter to
# spare; 2.5 on the peak is the same margin mid-arc.
CHECK all(d["bodycda"] >= 0.5 for d in drag if d.get("bodycda") is not None)
CHECK max(d["bodycda"] for d in drag if d.get("bodycda") is not None) >= 2.5
# THE PARTITION, ITS EXPOSED HALF: a fully exposed hull is refused NOTHING by the
# axial union, so the crossflow law keeps its hands off while the body-lift law acts
# on everything it sees. Pinned exactly on 0.00: this hull is one part, so "exposed"
# is not a near-miss. (The middle and the hidden half of the split belong to
# test_aerosweep -- a one-part hull's log cannot speak for both laws.)
CHECK all(d["sidecda"] == 0.0 for d in drag if d.get("sidecda") is not None)
# No lifting SURFACE is aboard: |L| stays 0.00 on every row while Ld is positive --
# the perpendicular force this hull flies on is the hull's own, and it shows up only
# in Ld='s split of the air's force, never in a wing's lift sum.
CHECK all(d["L"] == 0.0 for d in drag)
# The force is perpendicular to the flow, so it reads as Ld>0 with no wings aboard
# (the |L| column is SURFACE lift and stays 0 -- Ld= is the air's split of the total
# force, which is exactly the point). Measured 1.29 at t=4, still >= 0.9 at t=8.
CHECK all(d["ld"] > 0.0 for d in drag if d.get("ld") is not None)
CHECK any(d["ld"] >= 0.9 for d in drag if d.get("ld") is not None and d["t"] <= 8.0)
# ONE-PART HULL: the lift lands on the mount, and the mount IS the pivot -- the lever
# reads zero honestly rather than hiding a fabricated arm (cf. #947's review, where a
# noise ratio printed as a plausible +0.52 m arm).
CHECK all(d["bodycp"] == 0.0 for d in drag if d.get("bodycp") is not None)
# THE GLIDE, bounded: 48 s of flying lose 1.02 km (measured); a ballistic fall from the
# same bed would lose ~11.3 km. The 3 km floor is that gap with room for the transient.
CHECK drag[-1]["alt_asl"] - drag[0]["alt_asl"] > -3000.0
