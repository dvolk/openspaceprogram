# #772 authored wind on the last five bodies in the catalogue that have an atmosphere and had
# none: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune, Titan. utils/make_solar_system.py argues each row
# against its fact sheet (and check_fact_wind refuses a row above what that sheet states, with
# FACT_WIND_KNOWN carrying Titan, whose page states no wind at all), and tests/test_scenarios.cpp's
# kWindsAuthored lets a body author rows only if a case like this one pins what they DO.
#
# One run, five hulls, all on the same --startship shape: skiff_trim on the flying-trim bed at
# lat 0 lon 0, which spawns at a tenth of each body's air top and gives an airspeed keyed to
# that body's own air and gravity. The wind rows read at that altitude, at the equator, are:
#   Jupiter  24.5 km  150 m/s east   the authored <30 deg band, held up above it by windAt()
#   Saturn   40.0 km  400 m/s east   the authored <30 deg band (saturnfact.html's own figure)
#   Uranus   25.0 km  225 m/s east   interpolated between the column's 100 at alt 0 and its 250
#                                    at 30 km -- and the #775 generator leaves its equatorial
#                                    belt at the anchors' own values, which is what makes that
#                                    number reachable at lat 0 at all
#   Neptune  20.0 km  264 m/s east   the same, between 120 and 300
#   Titan    20.0 km   30 m/s east   the 20 km row verbatim
# The windless control (this catalogue with those five tables deleted -- tmp, not shipped) prints
# |v| = |v_air| for every one of them: 205.1, 113.4, 72.0, 81.2, 8.3 m/s. That is #688's law
# restated on five more worlds -- a wind is an air velocity, not a force, so the hull's airspeed
# does not move and its ground speed does.
#
# The gap is a VECTOR difference, not a sum. Only Jupiter's track is due east, where the gap is
# the whole 150 m/s. The other three beds throw their hulls tens of degrees off the wind: Saturn
# 27.5 deg (gap 389.9 of a 400 m/s wind), Neptune 26.3 (257.5 of 264), Titan 24.7 (29.4 of 30).
# On Uranus the bed launches the hull heading 224 deg -- 134.5 deg away from an easterly wind,
# so southwest through the air, and the same heading the windless run prints, which is the bed
# and not the wind -- so 72 m/s of airspeed plus 225 m/s of easterly wind comes out as 181.9 m/s
# over the ground. Pinning both numbers per body is what catches a sign flip or a row read at
# the wrong altitude, which neither sum alone can.
NAME the-giants-and-titan-move-their-air
CLAIM A hull spawned on each of the five bodies #772 winds reads its authored wind at the spawn altitude: the airspeed stays the bed's own value while the ground speed takes the wind, at Jupiter by exactly the 150 m/s its sheet states.
KIND golden
ARGS --system res/systems/solar_system.json --timeout 2 --info-log
ARGS --startship j,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Jupiter,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
ARGS --startship s,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Saturn,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
ARGS --startship u,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Uranus,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
ARGS --startship n,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Neptune,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
ARGS --startship t,res/ships/skiff_trim.json,Titan,flying-trim,lat=0,lon=0
FORBID error:
FORBID atmosphere.wind
# Every one of the five carries air: ground speed strictly above airspeed, on all five lines.
# A wind that stopped reaching the aero readout (a row dropped at load, a table past the 64-row
# budget) prints |v| = |v_air| here instead, which is what the windless control does.
CHECK (lambda p: len(p) == 5 and all(float(g) > float(a) + 1.0 for g, a in p))(re.findall(r"around (?:Jupiter|Saturn|Uranus|Neptune|Titan):.*?\|v\| = ([0-9.]+) m/s \|v_air\| = ([0-9.]+)", out))
# Jupiter: the exact case. Its track is east, its band is one row held up above the datum, so the
# gap is the authored figure to within rounding -- 355.1 over the ground, 205.1 through the air.
CHECK (lambda m: abs(float(m.group(1)) - float(m.group(2)) - 150.0) < 0.3)(re.search(r"around Jupiter:.*?\|v\| = ([0-9.]+) m/s \|v_air\| = ([0-9.]+)", out))
# Saturn's 400 m/s band is the fastest wind in the catalogue and the only one that makes #716
# (nothing caps an aero impulse) matter on a shipped body: 15.4 kPa of dynamic pressure at the
# deck on 0.193 kg/m3. Its airspeed pin is the control's 113.4, unmoved.
CHECK re.search(r"around Saturn:.*?\|v\| = 50[0-5]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 11[0-9]\.[0-9]", out)
# The generated columns: the equatorial belt answers with the anchors, so these are the
# interpolated 225 and 264 rather than either row verbatim, and neither body's airspeed budged
# from its control value.
CHECK re.search(r"around Uranus:.*?\|v\| = 18[0-2]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 7[1-3]\.[0-9]", out)
CHECK re.search(r"around Neptune:.*?\|v\| = 33[5-9]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = 8[0-3]\.[0-9]", out)
# Titan: the densest air of this catalogue's MOONS (5.3 kg/m3 at its datum, 1.96 at this 20 km
# spawn), with the calmest table. The hull's own 8.3 m/s is 67 Pa of dynamic pressure; the
# 30 m/s of air passing over the ground is 884 Pa, which is the number a hull parking in it
# would have to survive.
CHECK re.search(r"around Titan:.*?\|v\| = 3[6-9]\.[0-9] m/s \|v_air\| = [78]\.[0-9]", out)
