# two findings run, one couriered home; the one left on the walking crew member
# does not score
NAME courier-two-findings
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 10 --frame-cap 30 --startship labpod,res/ships/labpod.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --experiment 1000
ARGS --pod-experiment 1500
ARGS --eva 2000
ARGS --take 2500
ARGS --store 3000
# Hand control back to the ship with V (Slot::ToggleEva -> lastShip) rather than
# boarding: boarding would carry the suit's still-held study home, and the point
# here is that a finding left on a walking crew member is NOT banked. V is what a
# player reaches for and lands on the ship the walk came from whatever else is in
# the fleet; F6 steps by list position and would need this fleet to stay two
# vessels in a known order. "Recover Vessel" needs a ship active.
ARGS --sim-press 3500,50,V
ARGS --sim-press 4000,0,ESCAPE --recover 5000 --recover-anywhere
EXPECT recorded 'Low orbit observation
EXPECT recorded 'Low orbit materials study
EXPECT moved 'Low orbit materials study
EXPECT moved 'Low orbit observation
EXPECT new: Low orbit observation
FORBID new: Low orbit materials study
EXPECT [recover] t=
EXPECT [spacecenter] entered
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
