# two findings run, one couriered home; the one left on the walking crew member
# does not score
NAME courier-two-findings
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 10 --frame-cap 30 --width 1920 --height 1080
ARGS --startship labpod,res/ships/labpod.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
ARGS --ui-focus 700,labpod/capsule_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 800,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-click 1000,"labpod > Capsule (2m)##0/Run Experiment"
ARGS --ui-focus 1200,labpod/materials_pod_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 1300,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-click 1500,"labpod > Materials Pod (2m)##1/Run Experiment##pod_run"
ARGS --ui-click 2000,"labpod > Capsule (2m)##0/EVA##eva0"
ARGS --ui-click 2500,"labpod > Materials Pod (2m)##1/Take  (to Ash)##pod_take"
ARGS --ui-click 3000,"labpod > Capsule (2m)##0/Store  (into capsule)##store_Ash"
# Both experiments are the buttons a player presses: the capsule's "Run
# Experiment" (its first seat) and the pod's "Run Experiment##pod_run". The
# ##pod_run suffix exists precisely so a bare "Run Experiment" cannot satisfy
# the pod click, and the two paths here prove it: swap them and the case fails.
# --frame-cap 30 makes the frame clock coarse (~333ms), so several scripted
# events fall due in one batch; emit_sim_events pushes the aim before the click,
# which is what keeps an aim-then-click pair correct at any frame rate.
# Hand control back to the ship with V (Slot::ToggleEva -> lastShip) rather than
# boarding: boarding would carry the suit's still-held study home, and the point
# here is that a finding left on a walking crew member is NOT banked. V is what a
# player reaches for and lands on the ship the walk came from whatever else is in
# the fleet; F6 steps by list position and would need this fleet to stay two
# vessels in a known order. "Recover Vessel" needs a ship active.
ARGS --sim-press 3500,50,V
ARGS --recover-anywhere
ARGS --sim-press 4900,0,ESCAPE
ARGS --ui-click 5000,"Game Menu/Recover Vessel"
EXPECT recorded 'Low orbit observation
EXPECT recorded 'Low orbit materials study
EXPECT moved 'Low orbit materials study
EXPECT moved 'Low orbit observation
EXPECT new: Low orbit observation
FORBID new: Low orbit materials study
EXPECT [recover] t=
EXPECT [scene] spacecenter -> spacecenter (enterSpaceCenter)
EXPECT ship=labpod part=0 (capsule)
EXPECT ship=labpod part=1 (materials_pod)
EXPECT EVA: 'Ash' out of 'labpod' part 0
FORBID FAILED
FORBID GL_
