# Jet VTOL: the air-breathing jet (res/ships/jet.json) lifts off from a Duna
# pad at ZERO airspeed -- the static (fan) thrust term gives it ~36 kN at
# rest (jet_fan_thrust 32 kN + the fuel-momentum term; ship ~3.3 t, Duna
# g=2.943 -> TWR ~3.7 at rest). No runway, no wheels: a jet that cannot
# leave the pad at rest has lost its static thrust, and a ship that never
# sheds jet fuel is not running the jet at all.
#
# Duna is the lightest body with an atmosphere: every heavier one (Kerbin
# and below) is below the ship's TWR, and the airless ones give the jet
# no air (zero thrust by design). Kerbin's TWR ~1.1 would lift too, but
# Duna has the widest margin against a regression on either the floor or
# the density falloff.
#
# Timeline (--time-accel 1: 1 wall s == 1 sim s):
#   1.0-3.0 s  R held (throttle ramps to full over ~2 s)
#   3.0-9.0 s  T held (the jet burns; jet fuel at 6.82 kg/s for ~6 s ~= 40 kg)
#   9.0-14 s   coast to the --timeout 14 exit
NAME jet-vtol
ARGS --body Duna --scenario pad --ship res/ships/jet.json --time-accel 1
ARGS --timeout 14 --dbg-log --fuel-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 3000,6000,T
EXPECT [dbg]
CHECK len(dbg) >= 2
# The ship gains real altitude (static thrust ~35.8 kN = 32 kN fan + the
# fuel-momentum term, vs a ~3.3 t ship in 2.94 m/s^2 of gravity: a few
# hundred metres over 6 s of thrust).
CHECK last(dbg)["alt"] - first(dbg)["alt"] > 100
# The jet is an AIR-breathing engine: it burns jet fuel (the onboard fuel)
# while the oxidizer is free air -- so jet fuel must drain during the burn.
EXPECT [fuel]
CHECK len(fuel) >= 2
CHECK first(fuel)["groups"][0]["JF"][0] - last(fuel)["groups"][0]["JF"][0] > 1.0
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
