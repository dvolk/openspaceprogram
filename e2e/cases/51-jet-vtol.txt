# Jet VTOL: the air-breathing jet (res/ships/jet.json) lifts off from a Duna
# pad at ZERO airspeed -- the f0 floor gives it 30% of rated thrust at rest
# (120 kN rated, ship ~3.3 t, Duna g=2.943 -> TWR ~3.7 at rest). No
# runway, no wheels: a jet that cannot leave the pad at rest has lost its
# VTOL floor, and a ship that never sheds H2 is not running the jet at all.
#
# Duna is the lightest body with an atmosphere: every heavier one (Kerbin
# and below) is below the ship's TWR, and the airless ones give the jet
# no air (zero thrust by design). Kerbin's TWR ~1.1 would lift too, but
# Duna has the widest margin against a regression on either the floor or
# the density falloff.
#
# Timeline (--time-accel 1: 1 wall s == 1 sim s):
#   1.0-3.0 s  R held (throttle ramps to full over ~2 s)
#   3.0-9.0 s  T held (the jet burns; H2 at 6.82 kg/s for ~6 s ~= 40 kg)
#   9.0-14 s   coast to the --timeout 14 exit
NAME jet-vtol
ARGS --body Duna --scenario pad --ship res/ships/jet.json --time-accel 1
ARGS --timeout 14 --dbg-log --fuel-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 3000,6000,T
EXPECT [dbg]
CHECK len(dbg) >= 2
# The ship gains real altitude (a VTOL floor of 30% of 120 kN vs a ~3.3 t
# ship in 2.94 m/s^2 of gravity: a few hundred metres over 6 s of thrust).
CHECK last(dbg)["alt"] - first(dbg)["alt"] > 100
# The jet is an AIR-breathing engine: it burns H2 (the onboard fuel) while
# the oxidizer is free air -- so H2 must drain during the burn.
EXPECT [fuel]
CHECK len(fuel) >= 2
CHECK first(fuel)["groups"][0]["H2"][0] - last(fuel)["groups"][0]["H2"][0] > 1.0
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
