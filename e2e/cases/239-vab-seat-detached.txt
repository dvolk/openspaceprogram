# A named capsule is detached out of the build. The plan for it cannot survive
# -- the part is gone from the tree -- but it must not vanish in silence either:
# the next launch falls back to the pad's line, and a player who watched their
# own choice disappear has no way to know that (issue #288).
# This is also the assign -> detach -> launch sequence that used to reach
# vabLaunch's assert when nothing pruned the stale entry in between.
NAME vab-seat-detached
ARGS --vab res/ships/small_docker.json --kerbals e2e/fixtures/three_kerbals.json
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 12 --frame-cap 30
ARGS --vab-assign 3000,1,0,Ace
ARGS --vab-detach 1 --vab-detach-at 5000 --vab-launch 7000
EXPECT [vab] assign hook: 'capsule_1' seat 0 = 'Ace'
EXPECT [vab] detached capsule_1 (6 parts)
EXPECT [vab] dropped the seat plan for 'capsule_1' 'Ace' (it left the build)
EXPECT [toast] 1 seat assignment dropped: no such seat in this build
EXPECT [toast] No seats named: the next launch draws the pad's line
EXPECT [vab] launched 'small docker' (1 parts)
EXPECT [vab] detach hook: build 1 parts, 1 subassemblies
FORBID Assertion
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
LIMIT 90
