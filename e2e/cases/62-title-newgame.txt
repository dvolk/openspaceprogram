# New Game from the title screen: the floor becomes the Space Center hub with
# NO ship (the player then goes to the VAB to build and launch the first
# vessel; vabLaunch -> enterFlight). --new-game is the headless hook for the
# button. Boot is shipless (no --ship): the constructor seeds [Flight], then
# the shipless boot collapses it to Title.
NAME title-newgame
ARGS --new-game 2000 --timeout 8
EXPECT [scene] flight -> title (enterTitle)
EXPECT [scene] title -> spacecenter (enterSpaceCenter)
EXPECT [game] new game: Space Center, no ship
