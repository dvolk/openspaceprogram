#
# 1) A click aimed at a widget BEHIND the card used to land on that widget:
#    the card drew on top of everything but took nothing away, so a confirm
#    could be answered AND the click under it could open a window, toggle a
#    checkbox or pick a part. The scrim drawn under the card (drawQuestionScrim,
#    gameui.cpp) makes imgui hover nothing there, which --ui-click reports as a
#    MISMATCH instead of a clean click -- and the 5400ms dump proves the window
#    that click aimed at never opened. The stray click aims at "Controls"
#    (rect y 599..633) rather than "Settings" (y 561..595) on purpose: the
#    card's own buttons sit at y 565..585, so a click in that band could land
#    on a card button, which would answer the card and pass for the wrong
#    reason.
#
# 2) The game's OWN key handling ran while a card was up, so ESC popped a scene
#    under the live card and the card's callback then ran from a scene the
#    question was never asked in. ESC is now the card's last choice ("Cancel"
#    in every card) and no other key or mouse event is dispatched.
