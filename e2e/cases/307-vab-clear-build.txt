# "Clear build" empties the workspace. It is destructive and the editor has no
# undo, so the card is the guard: Cancel must leave the tanker's seven parts
# alone (the later clear reports 7 removed, which is the proof), and the second
# pass confirms. Once empty, the button is greyed and the click is refused --
# the refusal is the assertion, not a click that went wrong.
#
# A palette part is armed BEFORE the clear. Clearing drops the tool too; the
# viewport click after the clear proves it, because a surviving armed part would
# root itself at the origin and the launch would carry 1 part instead of
# refusing. Save proves the name field was reset: it must fail as "untitled"
# rather than overwrite the cleared tanker.
NAME vab-clear-build
ARGS --vab res/ships/tanker.json --width 1920 --height 1080 --timeout 10
ARGS --ui-click 1500,"Capsule (3m)##capsule_r1.5h3"
ARGS --ui-click 2200,"VAB TopBar/Clear build"
ARGS --ui-click 2800,"Question/Cancel"
ARGS --ui-click 3600,"VAB TopBar/Clear build"
ARGS --ui-click 4200,"Question/Clear"
ARGS --ui-click 5000,"VAB TopBar/Clear build"
ARGS --sim-mouse 5600,0,960,540,LMB
ARGS --ui-click 6200,"VAB TopBar/Save"
ARGS --ui-click 7000,"VAB TopBar/LAUNCH"
CHECK re.search(r"\[vab\] cleared the workspace \(7 parts removed\)", out)
EXPECT [toast] Workspace cleared
EXPECT [vab] camera aimed at the build: 0 part(s)
EXPECT path="VAB TopBar/Clear build" FAILED disabled
EXPECT [toast] Save FAILED: untitled
EXPECT [toast] Nothing to launch
FORBID launched
FORBID FAILED not found
FORBID FAILED ambiguous
FORBID Assertion
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
