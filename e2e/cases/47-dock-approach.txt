# Approach docking: --dock-test approach starts the port faces 2 m apart --
# just OUTSIDE the 1.5 m capture window -- co-moving on the same orbit, with
# the probe's dock target armed. The probe must burn the last stretch: its
# engine thrusts along +Z, toward the station ahead of it, so a short prograde
# burst (T) closes the gap into capture while the relative speed stays under
# the 2 m/s gate, and the targeted ports mate. (The burst is wall-clock timed
# via --sim-press, so it lands on 1 or 2 physics ticks; either closes the gap
# slow enough to dock -- both are asserted only through the [dock] line.)
NAME dock-approach
ARGS --dock-test approach --time-accel 1 --timeout 4
ARGS --sim-press 500,30,T
EXPECT Dock-test (approach)
EXPECT [dock] t=
EXPECT a="dockprobe" b="dockstation"
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
