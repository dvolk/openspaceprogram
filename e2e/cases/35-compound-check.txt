# Ship as ONE rigid body: alongside its per-part bodies, every ship is also
# built as a single btCompoundShape (one child per part hull, at the part's
# authored ship-local pose) re-based into the centre-of-mass / principal-axis
# frame, plus the btRigidBody for it. --compound-check re-derives each part's
# world pose from that body and prints how far it is from the part's own live
# pose; in free fall that is double-precision dust, and the deviation it does
# show on the pad IS the weld wobble the compound exists to remove.
#
# rebuildCompound() also asserts -- silently on success -- that the compound
# reproduces the assembly's centre of mass, its inertia tensor about that COM,
# and every re-based child pose. That runs on EVERY ship build in EVERY case,
# so this one's job is the wiring around it: the flag reaches the tick, a line
# is printed per ship, and the compound survives a staging event (the part
# list shrinks 7 -> 4 and the compound is rebuilt from it, the survivors
# keeping their authored poses in the original ship-local frame).
#
# "does NOT reproduce" is the assert's own message; a tripped assert also
# aborts the process, so the exit-code check would catch it too -- the FORBID
# is here for the diagnostic.
NAME compound-check
ARGS --scenario rot-orbit --ship res/ships/stager.json --time-accel 1 --timeout 15 --compound-check
ARGS --sim-press 3000,0,SPACE
EXPECT Main loop starting
EXPECT [compound] t=
EXPECT ship=stager parts=7
EXPECT Stage: dropped
EXPECT ship=stager parts=4
FORBID does NOT reproduce
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
