# The scene-switch shortcuts (1/2/3/4) are in-game navigation: on the title
# (no game running) they are all denied, and GoFlight is additionally denied
# whenever there is no ship to fly -- entering flight with none crashed the
# HUD's ship readouts (the title is the canonical no-ship case). So pressing
# all four on the title must be a no-op: the stack stays [title], no
# "title ->" transition is logged, and the game runs to the timeout without
# crashing. The digits are decimal SDL keycodes (49..52 = '1'..'4').
NAME scene-switch-title-denied
ARGS --timeout 6
ARGS --sim-press 800,0,49 --sim-press 1200,0,50 --sim-press 1600,0,51 --sim-press 2000,0,52
EXPECT Timeout reached (6.0 s)
FORBID title ->
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID error:
