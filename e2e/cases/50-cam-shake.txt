# Camera shake at high acceleration: on Moho (racer TWR ~2.3, the only
# body where the racer can lift off), ramp throttle (R) then fire (T).
# The felt acceleration (thrust + aero over mass) must be zero before
# ignition (steady cam, amp == 0) and rise above the 1 m/s^2 threshold
# while burning (amp > 0). Moho's ~29 m/s^2 of felt thrust saturates
# the amplitude at its cap -- the case asserts the pipeline (force ->
# accel -> amplitude), not the exact magnitude.
NAME cam-shake
ARGS --body Moho --scenario pad --ship res/ships/racer.json --time-accel 1
ARGS --timeout 12 --shake-log
ARGS --sim-press 1000,2000,R
ARGS --sim-press 3000,2000,T
EXPECT [shakelog]
CHECK len(shake) >= 2
# some samples steady (pre-ignition or post-cutoff, whichever the slow
# host's first frame lands in) and some shaking while the burn is live
CHECK any(d["amp"] == 0 for d in shake)
CHECK any(d["a"] > 0 and d["amp"] > 0 for d in shake)
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
