# A conic stops being a coast at the air. This apoapsis bed has a periapsis
# inside the atmosphere: rails warp accepts it (the ship is high and in
# vacuum), then the descent reaches the air, leaves the rails and drops to 1x
# so drag and the ground are simulated.
NAME rails-atmosphere-drop
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,ellipse-apo --time-accel 10
ARGS --timeout 10
ARGS --sim-press 1500,50,PERIOD
ARGS --sim-press 2000,50,PERIOD
EXPECT @@@ racer parked on rails around Kerbin
EXPECT Rails: 'racer' entered the atmosphere, warp 1x
EXPECT @@@ racer left the rails around Kerbin
FORBID frozen on rails
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
