# A ship sitting on the launch pad has no orbit worth framing. Its rotation-only
# state is an ellipse that grazes the surface -- apoapsis at the pad, periapsis
# deep inside the body -- so the fit lands on Kerbin's 600 km disk: 6002.6
# m/pixel, within 0.05% of the old flat 6000 the map was tuned at. The exact
# figure is the launch site's rotation speed; what must not move is that the
# BODY, not an orbit, sets the zoom.
#
# It used to pin "fit 600264 m" as text, and moved to 600263 -- one metre in 600
# km, exactly the incidental thing the paragraph above says not to care about.
# So the digits became a band and the claim became the arithmetic: the printed
# scale is the printed extent over kMapFitRadiusPx = 100 px (gameui.cpp:5191,
# constants.h:237), and the extent sits just above the body's own radius rather
# than below it.
NAME map-fit-landed-body-floor
CLAIM A landed ship opens framed on the body's disk.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 4 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
FORBID error:
CHECK (mm := re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Kerbin: body 600000 m, fit (\d+) m -> ([\d.]+) m/px", out)) is not None and 600200.0 < float(mm.group(1)) < 600350.0
# (re-searched, not reused: each CHECK gets its own namespace)
CHECK (mm := re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Kerbin: body 600000 m, fit (\d+) m -> ([\d.]+) m/px", out)) is not None and abs(float(mm.group(2)) - float(mm.group(1)) / 100.0) < 0.05
# The fit is the view a focus opens with, not a per-frame camera: one line for
# the whole run, so a latch that re-fires every draw fails here.
CHECK len(re.findall(r"\[mapfit\]", out)) == 1
