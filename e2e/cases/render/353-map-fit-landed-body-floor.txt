# A ship sitting on the launch pad has no orbit worth framing. Its rotation-only
# state is an ellipse that grazes the surface -- apoapsis at the pad, periapsis
# deep inside the body -- so the fit lands on Kerbin's 600 km disk: 6002.6
# m/pixel, within 0.05% of the old flat 6000 the map was tuned at. The exact
# figure is the launch site's rotation speed; what must not move is that the
# BODY, not an orbit, sets the zoom.
NAME map-fit-landed-body-floor
CLAIM A landed ship opens framed on the body's disk.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 4 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
FORBID error:
CHECK re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Kerbin: body 600000 m, fit 600264 m -> 6002\.6 m/px", out)
# The fit is the view a focus opens with, not a per-frame camera: one line for
# the whole run, so a latch that re-fires every draw fails here.
CHECK len(re.findall(r"\[mapfit\]", out)) == 1
