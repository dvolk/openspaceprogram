# The fit follows a focus change: F6 hands the map to a ship at the Mun, which
# then opens framed on its own orbit instead of keeping Kerbin's framing. Both
# numbers are that ship's apoapsis at 100 px, so bodies of different sizes with
# different orbits each get their own view.
NAME map-fit-follows-focus
CLAIM A focus change refits the map to the new ship's orbit.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 6 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,inertial-orbit
ARGS --startship probe,res/ships/racer.json,Mun,inertial-orbit
ARGS --sim-press 2500,50,F6
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2: probe
FORBID error:
CHECK re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Kerbin: body 600000 m, fit 725000 m -> 7250\.0 m/px", out)
CHECK re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Mun: body 200000 m, fit 325000 m -> 3250\.0 m/px", out)
