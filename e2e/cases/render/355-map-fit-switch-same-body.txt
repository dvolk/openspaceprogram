# The fit latches on the FOCUS, which is a body AND a ship. Two ships around
# the same Kerbin have different orbits, so F6 from one to the other must
# refit: a body-only latch would keep the first ship's framing and push the
# second ship's apoapsis off the window.
NAME map-fit-switch-same-body
CLAIM Switching ships around the same body refits the map to the new orbit.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 6 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,inertial-orbit
ARGS --startship probe,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,high-orbit
ARGS --sim-press 2500,50,F6
EXPECT Active ship 2 of 2: probe
FORBID error:
CHECK re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Kerbin: body 600000 m, fit 725000 m -> 7250\.0 m/px", out)
CHECK re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Kerbin: body 600000 m, fit 1100000 m -> 11000\.0 m/px", out)
# Exactly the two fits: the opening one and the switch. A latch that re-fires
# every draw would fit the same ship hundreds of times here.
CHECK len(re.findall(r"\[mapfit\]", out)) == 2
