# A body used to state its air as two typed scalars -- molar_mass and gamma -- which is a
# statement about arithmetic, not about a world. It now authors a gas MIX
# (atmo.h:gasSpecies -> system.cpp -> AtmosphereParams::composition) and the loader
# resolves molar mass, gamma and the oxidizer share from it, the same way the altitude
# table's level pressures are resolved rather than authored (#552 A4). This case pins the
# dossier rows the mix is the source of: one "air gas <name>" row per species, and the
# oxidizer share.
#
# tests/test_scenarios.cpp already pins the resolved numbers of every shipped file and
# tests/test_jet.cpp pins the gate. What only an e2e sees is the player-facing row.
#
# The rows got shorter on 2026_11_05. The mix used to be ONE row -- "N2 78.09%, O2 20.95%,
# Ar 0.934%, CO2 0.033%" -- which overran the dossier column on a four-gas world and ended
# in a "+2 more" tail, and the oxidizer row used to narrate what the share does to a jet
# ("a jet gets 0.00676% of its rated air"). Both were prose in an instrument. The gas rows
# are one per species now, biggest first, and the oxidizer row states the share and stops;
# the jet half still exists, it just lives where it is paid -- drag.h:jetAirGate bills
# thrust and burn every tick (vehicle.cpp), so a thin sky shows up as a weak engine rather
# than as a sentence. Eve and Venus have sea-level air denser than Kerbin's -- 1.7 and 92
# kg/m3 -- and both are a dead engine, because a turbine burns against oxygen and there is
# barely any in a CO2 sky. The mix rows are what lets the game say so at all.
NAME air-composition-dossier
CLAIM A body's dossier names the gases its air is made of, biggest first, and prints the share of that air a turbine can burn.
KIND invariant
ARGS --system res/systems/solar_system.json --timeout 6 --atlas-dump 4000
FORBID error:
# Earth authors dry air (utils/make_solar_system.py's DRY_AIR): N2 78.084, O2 20.946, Ar
# 0.934, CO2 0.033 %, which the loader normalizes by its own 0.99997 total -- hence the
# 78.09 %. Order is part of the claim: biggest gas first, so the top of the block reads
# like the world it belongs to. One regex over the four consecutive rows rather than four
# searches, because four searches go green even if the rows shuffle.
CHECK re.search(r"air gas N2\s+78\.09%\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air gas O2\s+20\.95%\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air gas Ar\s+0\.934%\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air gas CO2\s+0\.033%", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Earth weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 23.1 % of dry air's MASS is O2 -- 20.95 % by MOLE in the rows above it, since a mole of
# O2 weighs more than a mole of N2, which is why the unit is on the value. The verdict that
# used to follow it is gone on purpose, so this pins that the row stops at the number.
CHECK re.search(r"air oxidizer\s+23\.14% by mass\s*\n", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Earth weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Venus: 96.5 % CO2, 3.5 % N2, and no free oxygen at all -- 92 kg/m3 of air at the surface,
# 13 MPa, and a jet that produces nothing. The "none" is the mix's own answer:
# gasSpecies("CO2").oxidizer is false.
CHECK re.search(r"air gas CO2\s+96\.5%\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air gas N2\s+3\.5%", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Venus weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
CHECK re.search(r"air oxidizer\s+none\s*\n", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Venus weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Mars is the interesting one: it HAS oxygen (0.13 % of the molecules, 0.1 % of the mass),
# so its row must not read "none" even though a jet there gets 0.0068 % of a rated intake.
# Before the split that distinction needed the gate printed beside the share; now it needs
# only the share, which is the number the mix actually authors.
CHECK re.search(r"air gas O2\s+0\.1303%", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Mars weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
CHECK re.search(r"air oxidizer\s+0\.09588% by mass\s*\n", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Mars weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Jupiter: an H2/He sky. Hydrogen is the fuel, not the oxidizer, so the densest air in the
# system after Titan buys a jet nothing. Its molar mass is 0.0022 kg/mol -- a factor 13
# under dry air, and every temperature and sound-speed row in this dossier follows it.
CHECK re.search(r"air gas H2\s+89\.8%\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air gas He\s+10\.2%", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Jupiter weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
CHECK re.search(r"air oxidizer\s+none\s*\n", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Jupiter weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Every row above searches a BOUNDED block -- (?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z) stops at the next
# body -- rather than "Earth weight=.*?air oxidizer", because the unbounded form can reach
# into a later body's rows and still go green if Earth's own row disappears. render/552
# learned this the hard way: splitting on "[atlas] " alone covers only the header line.
#
# And the rows belong to bodies that HAVE air: Mercury's dossier says "atmosphere none" and
# owes none of them.
CHECK "air oxidizer" not in re.search(r"\[atlas\] Mercury weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0)
CHECK "air gas " not in re.search(r"\[atlas\] Mercury weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0)
