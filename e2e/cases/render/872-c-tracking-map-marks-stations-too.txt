# Both map windows mark the stations, not just the one you fly with (#872). The Tracking
# Station is where a player looks for something to fly to, and its map is a separate call
# site with its own focus rule (a body, not necessarily a ship's).
#
# Reaching it by scene shortcut and dumping there is what tells the two call sites apart:
# drawn= is the count a map WINDOW stamped while drawing, so deleting the Tracking copy
# leaves the stamp stale (-1) and this case reddens even though the flight-map case stays
# green -- and the other way round for its sibling.
NAME tracking-map-marks-the-stations-too
CLAIM The Tracking Station's map marks each orbital station around its focus body, conic included.
KIND invariant
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 5 --map-dump 3000 --dbg-log --orbit-interval 1.0
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --sim-press 1200,0,51
EXPECT [scene] flight -> tracking (push)
FORBID error:
# Focus is the body, and only the driven stations are marks: the pads the ship is
# sitting on stay the Surface Map's.
CHECK set(m["id"] for m in mapbldg) == {"kerbin_dock", "kerbin_station"}
CHECK not any(m["id"] == "main_space_port" for m in mapbldg)
CHECK all(m["kind"] == "orbit" and m["focus"] == "Kerbin" for m in mapbldg)
CHECK all(m["drawn"] >= 2 for m in mapbldg)
CHECK all(m["pts"] >= 8 and abs(m["conic_r"] - m["r"]) < 1.0 for m in mapbldg)
