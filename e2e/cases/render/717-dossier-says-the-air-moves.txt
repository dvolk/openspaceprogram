# A body can author atmosphere.wind (#688) and until #717 no readout said so: the dossier
# described Venus's air as 9.2 MPa of CO2 and never mentioned that its deck runs at
# 100 m/s. This pins the player-facing rows -- one per authored LEVEL, altitude in the
# label because altitude is the axis the table keys on, speed and compass direction in
# the value.
#
# The direction is the way the air crosses the GROUND (WindLevel is ground-relative),
# which is a different statement from the airspeed the aero law bills -- that pair lives
# on the Surface window and in [surfinfo]'s tas=/hs= split, pinned by
# flight/688-earth-jet-bends-ground-speed and flight/688-mars-wind-shows-on-the-tape.
#
# The rows come from utils/make_solar_system.py, where each one cites the fact sheet it
# stands on (or says plainly that the sheet stops short and the figure is climatology).
# Since #767 the label can also carry a LATITUDE band, and since #772 five shipped bodies use
# it; a table the generator expands (#775) gets a row naming the law and the belt count it
# derived instead of 26 rows the player cannot read.
NAME dossier-says-the-air-moves
CLAIM A body's dossier prints a row per authored wind level, with the altitude, the speed and the compass direction the air moves -- and the latitude band when its table has one -- while a body with no authored wind prints none.
KIND invariant
ARGS --system res/systems/solar_system.json --timeout 6 --atlas-dump 4000
FORBID error:
# Venus: 1 m/s at the datum (venusfact.html's own surface row) and 100 m/s at 65 km
# (the cloud-top superrotation, cited where it is authored). Order is the table's, and
# the table is sorted by altitude at load.
CHECK re.search(r"air wind 0\.0 m\s+1 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 65\.0 km\s+100 m/s east", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Venus weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Earth: 5 m/s at the datum and 40 m/s at 11 km -- a jet core, deliberately NOT the
# sheet's 100 m/s top, which check_fact_wind would allow.
CHECK re.search(r"air wind 0\.0 m\s+5 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 11\.0 km\s+40 m/s east", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Earth weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Mars: one row, the lander-site figure held up to the ceiling by windAt()'s rule.
CHECK re.search(r"air wind 0\.0 m\s+10 m/s east", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Mars weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# #772: a table with LATITUDE bands prints the band beside the altitude, which is the only way
# this readout can tell "150 m/s inside 30 degrees and 40 outside it" -- jupiterfact.html's own
# pair -- from one number for the whole world. Both rows sit at the datum, so the order a reader
# sees is the order the file authored them: equator first, then the two outer halves.
CHECK re.search(r"air wind 0\.0 m, lat -30/\+30\s+150 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 0\.0 m, lat -90/-30\s+40 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 0\.0 m, lat \+30/\+90\s+40 m/s east", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Jupiter weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# A column the GENERATOR expanded says so, and says what it derived: the law, the Rossby number
# and the belt count it asked for. Then it lists the equatorial belt -- the anchors' own values,
# which is what the belt at lat 0 is -- and declines to print the other 24 rows, sending the
# reader to the wind map instead. 26 rows in a dossier would bury the two the file authored.
CHECK re.search(r"air wind \(generated\)\s+rhines: Ro 0\.0487, 6 belts per hemisphere, Rhines scale 13\.6 deg", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Uranus weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
CHECK re.search(r"air wind 0\.0 m, lat -6/\+6\s+100 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 30\.0 km, lat -6/\+6\s+250 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind\s+24 more rows \(one per anchor per belt\) not listed", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Uranus weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# Titan's three levels, on a body no page states a wind for: the rows are published climatology,
# and the FACT_WIND_KNOWN waiver in utils/make_solar_system.py is on record saying so. 1 m/s at
# the datum is worth printing anyway -- it is the calmest deck in the catalogue and the densest
# air (5.3 kg/m3) at the same time.
CHECK re.search(r"air wind 0\.0 m\s+1 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 20\.0 km\s+30 m/s east\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]   air wind 60\.0 km\s+100 m/s east", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Titan weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# And the negative: bodies whose sheet states no wind and whose file authors none print no such
# row -- not "0 m/s calm", which would be a claim the file never made. Pluto is the half of the
# pair that bites since #772: it has AUTHORED air (13 microbar of N2, #700) and no wind rows, so
# this is a world whose sky the dossier can describe and declines to say is moving.
CHECK not re.search(r"air wind", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Moon weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
CHECK not re.search(r"air wind", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Pluto weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
