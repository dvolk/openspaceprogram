# The orbital map opens fitted to the ship's orbit around the focus body, not
# to a flat 6000 m/pixel. That flat value was tuned for 600 km Kerbin (its
# radius at 100 px); at it, 6371 km Earth does not even fit the 480 px map
# window.
#
# [mapfit] prints the body radius, the fitted radius (the apoapsis here) and
# the scale on every fit (world load, focus change, Reset view), so the opening
# scale is observable without a screenshot.
NAME map-fit-earth
CLAIM The orbital map's initial zoom is the ship's apoapsis at 100 px.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 4 --system res/systems/solar_system.json
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Earth,inertial-orbit
FORBID error:
CHECK re.search(r"\[mapfit\] Earth: body 6371000 m, fit 6498500 m -> 64985\.0 m/px", out)
