# The manual ToF slider's band was hardcoded to log10 in [1.8, 7.5] -- 366 days at
# the top -- while the planner searches ToF 60 s .. three target periods and reports
# whatever its min-dv pick happens to be. For Jupiter that is ~14 years, 40x the old
# ceiling. (Jupiter needs a Sun-centred start: the transfer planner only lists
# children of the body the ship orbits, hence heli-earth.)
# imgui clamps a slider only on INTERACTION, so an out-of-band value survives a
# passive draw -- what it cannot do is be adjusted. The grab drew jammed at the end
# of the track, and the first touch of the slider dropped a 14-year plan inside the
# band with no indication. The band now follows the target's own range.
# The last CHECK is the red one: pre-fix, this exact click landed at log10 ToF 6.56
# (3.6e6 s, a 409 km/s burn) because 7.5 was the ceiling the widget could express.
NAME tof-past-the-slider-band
ARGS --system res/systems/solar_system.json --time-accel 1 --timeout 16
ARGS --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Sun,heli-earth --transfer-target Jupiter
ARGS --xfer-log --orbit-interval 1
ARGS --ui-click 2000,"Windows/Target"
# Auto off seeds the slider from what auto just recommended, so the value the
# widget is asked to show is already past the old ceiling.
ARGS --ui-click 6000,"Target/Auto ToF (min dv)"
ARGS --ui-click 9000,"Target/log10(ToF s)"
EXPECT path="Target/log10(ToF s)" window=
CHECK xfer[0]["tof"] > 3.1623e7
CHECK last(xfer)["tof"] > 3.1623e7
CHECK last(xfer)["tof"] != xfer[0]["tof"]
FORBID Assertion
FORBID Segmentation
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
LIMIT 40
