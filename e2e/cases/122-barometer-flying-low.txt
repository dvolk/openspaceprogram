# Barometer in FLYING-LOW: the biome-specific half of the flying rule. A ship
# airborne in the lower atmosphere (the flying-low scenario, ~10km on Kerbin
# -- below the 20%-of-atmo split) records a BIOME-SPECIFIC reading --
# "Flying low barometer of <Biome> on Kerbin" -- unlike flying-high, which is
# "of the planet". First-time flying-low on the home body = 10 x 1.1 x 1.0 =
# 11. Mirrors 123-barometer-flying-high (global) so the two pin the
# barometer's flying biome rule; the landed half is 121-barometer-landed.
NAME barometer-flying-low
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --startship baropod,res/ships/baropod.json,Kerbin,flying-low
ARGS --pod-experiment 1200
ARGS --space-center 2500 --recover 3500 --recover-anywhere
EXPECT [science] t=
EXPECT recorded
EXPECT Flying low barometer of
EXPECT [science] recovered +11
EXPECT [recover] t=
EXPECT [spacecenter] entered
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
