# Science v2: the Landed situation. A ship grounded on the pad (the "pad"
# scenario) runs an observation; because isGrounded() is true it records
# "Landed observation ..." rather than an orbit observation (the #75 fix:
# v1 read a grounded ship as a "low orbit observation"). Recovery scores it
# full: 10 x 1.0 (landed) x 1.0 (home body) = 10.
NAME science-landed
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 8 --startship racer,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --experiment 1500
ARGS --space-center 3000 --recover 4000 --recover-anywhere
EXPECT Landed observation
EXPECT [science] recovered +10
EXPECT [recover] t=
FORBID GL_
FORBID error:
