NAME 830-the-dossier-carries-the-lag-beside-the-swing-it-modifies
CLAIM A shipped world that authored a ground phase lag says so in the same block as its swing -- the knob in radians of phase, its response time in hours, and the measured solar day that converts the two -- while the swing itself stays the law's two ends, so the identity that ties them still closes and it is the READER who is told which of the two numbers the map cannot paint.
KIND invariant
ARGS --timeout 8 --system res/systems/ksp_system.json --atlas-dump 4000
# #830. Before this row, Dres's dossier and Moho's were byte-identical in their thermal block
# except for their kelvin, while Dres's map paints its hottest ground 42 deg east of its star and
# stops 15 K short of its own law's hot end. The fix is an EXTRA row, not a changed one: the two
# ends above are what one anchor and one even share force, and e2e science/724 and 735 hold the
# identity (day/T0)^4 + 3(night/T0)^4 == 4 against them -- making the swing row lag-aware would
# break that for every lagged body, which is why the lag travels beside it instead.
#
# 1. THE ROW, on the most-lagged body in the shipped file (res/systems/ksp_system.json:
#    lag_tau 1.1030374825618534). Radians first because that is the unit the file authors --
#    authored.h's whole argument about someone typing `14` for a 14:00 afternoon peak.
CHECK re.search(r"ground lag\s+1\.103 rad of phase \(a 1\.70 h response; day 09:40:25\)", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 2. THE SWING DID NOT MOVE. 53.6 to 220.0 K is still the law's pair at this body's MEAN-orbit
#    anchor of 156.0 K, and it is still wider than what the world wears: the map at Dres's
#    apoapsis start paints 50.09 to 190.40 K (render/794-b, where temp_ends= prints the same
#    205.63 K law end and temp_ramp= prints the 190.40 K the palette can reach). The dossier's
#    number is the law; the new row is the reason they differ.
CHECK re.search(r"ground swing\s+53\.6 to 220\.0 K", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 3. THE HOURS ARE THE RESPONSE TIME (tau / omega_solar), and they are converted with this body's
#    MEASURED mean solar day -- the same figure its `day length` row prints, never the sidereal
#    rotation. Recomputing one printed number from the other two is the check that catches the
#    wrong day being used: a sidereal Dres day is 34,816 s, so it would not move the tenth here,
#    but on the slow-spinning bodies in this file (Pol: a 10d 12h day, 19.29 h of response) the
#    two differ by minutes.
CHECK (lambda lg, dy: abs(float(lg.group(2)) - float(lg.group(1)) / (2.0 * 3.14159265358979) * (int(dy.group(1)) * 3600 + int(dy.group(2)) * 60 + int(dy.group(3))) / 3600.0) < 0.01)(re.search(r"ground lag\s+([\d.]+) rad of phase \(a ([\d.]+) h response; day (\d+):(\d+):(\d+)\)", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0)), re.search(r"day length\s+(\d+):(\d+):(\d+)", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0)))
# 4. ABSENCE, NOT ZERO. Moho is the body the pricing pass measured at tau 0.006 and refused
#    (utils/gen_systems.py prints it below LAG_AUTHOR_FLOOR), so it keeps the sentinel and the row
#    dashes. A printed 0.000 here would be a claim that this ground peaks at noon.
CHECK re.search(r"ground lag\s+-", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Moho weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
FORBID ground lag\s+0\.000 rad
# 5. WHO HAS ONE: exactly the seven the pass authored, and every ground that prices a swing gets
#    a lag row beside it -- the two columns are one law, so they cannot drift apart by omission.
CHECK len(re.findall(r"ground lag\s+[\d.]+ rad", out)) == 7
CHECK len(re.findall(r"ground lag\s+-", out)) == len(re.findall(r"ground swing\s+[\d.]+ to", out)) - 7
# 6. A banded world gets the banded WORD (it has no ground to lag), so the row cannot read as a
#    sea floor nobody can find. Jool is skipped by the pass anyway -- air is not a radiating skin.
CHECK (lambda jb: re.search(r"level lag\s+-", jb) is not None and "ground lag" not in jb)(re.search(r"\[atlas\] Jool weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 7. THE IDENTITY, over every swing this file printed -- the guard that the new row is an extra
#    row. Same rounding-aware tolerance science/724 derives (the dossier prints tenths, and the
#    fourth powers multiply that half-decimal).
CHECK all(abs((float(d) / float(a)) ** 4 + 3.0 * (float(n) / float(a)) ** 4 - 4.0) < 1.6 / float(a) for (n, d), a in zip(re.findall(r"ground swing\s+([\d.]+) to ([\d.]+) K", out), re.findall(r"ground anchor(?: \(sea level\))?\s+([\d.]+) K", out)))
FORBID error:
