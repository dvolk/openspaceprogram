NAME 830-the-dossier-carries-the-lag-beside-the-swing-it-modifies
CLAIM A world the shipped catalogue gives a ground phase lag says so in the same block as the swing it bends -- the knob in radians of phase, how far past its sun its hottest ground can sit, and the measured solar day that converts the two -- while the swing itself stays the law's two ends, so the identity that ties those ends still closes AND the afternoon the surface map measures lands inside the bound the dossier claims.

ARGS --timeout 14 --time-accel 100 --system res/systems/ksp_system.json --atlas-dump 4000
ARGS --surfmap-log --surfmap-n 128 --surfmap-body Dres --surfmap-map temperature

# #830. Before this row, Dres's dossier and Moho's were byte-identical in their thermal block
# except for their kelvin, while Dres's map paints its hottest ground 42 deg east of its star and
# stops 15 K short of its own law's hot end. The fix is an EXTRA row, not a changed one: the two
# ends above are what one anchor and one even share force, and e2e science/724 and 735 hold the
# identity (day/T0)^4 + 3(night/T0)^4 == 4 against them -- making the swing row lag-aware would
# break that for every lagged body, which is why the lag travels beside it instead.
#
# 1. THE ROW, on the most-lagged body in the shipped file (res/systems/ksp_system.json:
#    lag_tau 1.1030374825618534). Radians first because that is the unit the file authors --
#    authored.h's whole argument about someone typing `14` for a 14:00 afternoon peak -- at four
#    decimals, the same precision --surfmap-log's lag_tau= prints. Then the quantity a player can
#    check by looking at the map: how far past the sun the hottest ground sits.
CHECK re.search(r"ground lag\s+1\.1030 rad of phase \(hottest ground up to 47\.8 deg / 1\.28 h past noon; day 09:40:25\)", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 2. THE SWING DID NOT MOVE. 53.6 to 220.0 K is still the law's pair at this body's MEAN-orbit
#    anchor of 156.0 K. It is wider than what the world wears at its hot end -- the map at Dres's
#    epoch rail paints 50.09 to 190.40 K (render/794-b: temp_ends= prints that rail's 205.63 K
#    law end, temp_ramp= the 190.40 K the palette can reach) -- and NARROWER at its cold end,
#    because 50.09 K belongs to permanently dark polar rows, which the law's cos_inc 0.0 end
#    (53.6 K) never reaches. Neither span is wrong; the new row says why they differ.
CHECK re.search(r"ground swing\s+53\.6 to 220\.0 K", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 3. BESIDE, not merely present. "The row carries the lag beside the swing it modifies" is the
#    claim, and render/736 shows how fragile that adjacency is: adding this row broke two of that
#    case's row chains, which is why 736 now crosses the lag row on purpose. This pins the order
#    for a file players actually load.
CHECK (lambda blk: re.search(r"ground swing\s+[\d.]+ to [\d.]+ K\s*\n\s*\[atlas\]\s+ground lag\s+[\d.]", blk) is not None)(re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 4. THE DEGREES BEHAVE LIKE atan(tau). The harness empties __builtins__ so a case file cannot
#    reach open or exec, which also locks out math -- so this is the three arithmetic
#    consequences of atan, not atan: every phase is under a quarter turn, a bigger knob always
#    means a later afternoon, and the degrees never exceed the radians themselves (atan(x) < x),
#    which is the mistake of printing the authored phase as if it were an offset -- Dres would
#    read 63.2 deg there, 2/3 of its own day, and this bracket reddens it. The exact constant is
#    pinned by CHECK 1 and falsified against the map by CHECK 7.
CHECK all(0.0 <= float(m.group(2)) <= 90.0 and float(m.group(2)) < 57.29577951308232 * float(m.group(1)) + 0.05 for m in re.finditer(r"(?:ground|level) lag\s+([\d.]+) rad of phase \(hottest ground up to ([\d.]+) deg", out))
CHECK (lambda ps: all(a[1] <= b[1] for a in ps for b in ps if a[0] < b[0]))([(float(m.group(1)), float(m.group(2))) for m in re.finditer(r"(?:ground|level) lag\s+([\d.]+) rad of phase \(hottest ground up to ([\d.]+) deg", out)])
# 5. THE HOURS COME FROM THAT BODY'S OWN DAY: the row's degrees over that body's MEASURED mean
#    solar day, not a constant and not the sidereal period. Dres alone could not catch a sidereal
#    bug -- its sidereal period is 2*pi/rot_ang_speed = 34,800.0 s against a 34,825.0 s solar day,
#    a 0.001 h difference no band this wide separates -- so the check runs over EVERY lagged body.
#    Pol is the one that bites: tidally locked to Jool, it spins in 901,903 s but its sun takes
#    909,742 s to come back, so dividing by the first prints 17.82 h where the row prints 17.98.
#    The tolerance is printed rounding, which grows with the day: half a tenth of a degree across
#    360 of it, plus the printed hundredth of an hour.
CHECK all((lambda secs: abs(float(re.search(r"/ ([\d.]+) h past noon", b).group(1)) - float(re.search(r"up to ([\d.]+) deg", b).group(1)) / 360.0 * secs / 3600.0) < 0.006 + 0.06 / 360.0 * secs / 3600.0)((lambda d: int(d.group(1) or 0) * 86400 + int(d.group(2)) * 3600 + int(d.group(3)) * 60 + int(d.group(4)))(re.search(r"day length\s+(?:(\d+)d\s+)?(\d+):(\d+):(\d+)", b))) for b in re.split(r"\n(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=)", out) if "h past noon" in b)
# 6. ... AND THE DAY THE ROW NAMES IS THE DAY ITS OWN BLOCK PRINTS. Two different periods inside
#    one block is the mistake this catches, and it is the reason the row repeats the day at all.
#    Filtered on the converted form, not on "rad of phase": a body with no day to convert against
#    prints `... rad of phase (no day to convert against)`, and slicing that for hours and a day
#    would raise -- which reads as harness breakage rather than as a body the row cannot price.
CHECK all(re.search(r"; day ([^\)]+)\)", b).group(1).strip() == re.search(r"day length\s+(.+?)\s*$", b, re.M).group(1) for b in re.split(r"\n(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=)", out) if "h past noon" in b)
# 7. THE BOUND IS NOT DECORATIVE. The row says UP TO because atan(tau) is exact only for a
#    polar-day row; a day/night row peaks inside it -- render/794-b measures that inside value
#    at +42.6 deg on a 256-column grid. Here the same claim is checked at this case's own 128
#    columns: pin both sides, so the measured afternoon stays under the bound AND within 8 deg
#    of it, or "up to" has quietly become a free number. The bake reports the same tau the
#    dossier read, a row apart.
#    MEASURED at --surfmap-n 128 (this case's own setting): +44.02, +42.53, +43.86 across the
#    three bakes -- a 128-column half-pixel is 1.4 deg, so only one of the three lands on the
#    256-column figure render/794-b quotes. That leaves 2.7 deg of real slack under the 8 deg
#    leg, which is the number to know if this case ever reddens: it breaks by the GRID
#    coarsening, not by the law moving.
#    And the legs below are all() over surfmap, which is TRUE of an empty list -- so the bake
#    count is pinned first. A renamed keybinding would otherwise silently delete this case's
#    only engine-vs-dossier claim, since --atlas-dump alone would keep everything else green.
CHECK len(surfmap) == 3
CHECK all(r["body"] == "Dres" and abs(r["lag_tau"] - 1.1030) < 1e-3 for r in surfmap)
CHECK (lambda bound: all(r["peak_deg"] < bound for r in surfmap) and all(r["peak_deg"] > bound - 8.0 for r in surfmap))(float(re.search(r"\[atlas\] Dres weight=.*?up to ([\d.]+) deg", out, re.S).group(1)))
# 8. ABSENCE, NOT ZERO. Moho is the body the pricing pass measured at tau 0.006 and refused
#    (utils/gen_systems.py prints it below LAG_AUTHOR_FLOOR), so it keeps the sentinel and the row
#    dashes. A printed 0.000 here would be a claim that this ground peaks at noon.
CHECK re.search(r"ground lag\s+-", re.search(r"\[atlas\] Moho weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
#    A CHECK, not a FORBID: FORBID is a literal substring test, so the regex-shaped line this
#    replaced (`ground lag\s+0\.000 rad`) could never have fired.
CHECK len(re.findall(r"ground lag\s+0\.000 rad", out)) == 0
# 9. NO ROW WITHOUT ITS PAIR: every ground (or level) this file prices a swing for carries a lag
#    row beside it, so the two halves of one law cannot drift apart by omission. A correspondence
#    rather than "exactly seven": WHICH bodies author a tau is tests/test_belts.cpp's roster to
#    pin, and a bare seven stays green when one body trades places with another.
CHECK len(re.findall(r"(?:ground|level) lag", out)) == len(re.findall(r"(?:ground|level) swing", out))
# 10. A banded world gets the banded WORD (it has no ground to lag), so the row cannot read as a
#     sea floor nobody can find. And it can only ever dash now: src/system.cpp refuses a
#     surface.lag_tau on a body with surface.bands, because a column is not a radiating skin.
CHECK (lambda jb: re.search(r"level lag\s+-", jb) is not None and "ground lag" not in jb)(re.search(r"\[atlas\] Jool weight=.*?(?=\[atlas\] \w+ weight=|\Z)", out, re.S).group(0))
# 11. THE IDENTITY, over every swing this file printed -- the guard that the new row is an extra
#     row. Same rounding-aware tolerance science/724 derives (the dossier prints tenths, and the
#     fourth powers multiply that half-decimal).
CHECK all(abs((float(d) / float(a)) ** 4 + 3.0 * (float(n) / float(a)) ** 4 - 4.0) < 1.6 / float(a) for (n, d), a in zip(re.findall(r"ground swing\s+([\d.]+) to ([\d.]+) K", out), re.findall(r"ground anchor(?: \(sea level\))?\s+([\d.]+) K", out)))
