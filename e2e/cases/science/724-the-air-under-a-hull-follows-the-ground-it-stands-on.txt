NAME 724-the-air-under-a-hull-follows-the-ground-it-stands-on
CLAIM Where a world authors an air-follow knob, the air at a hull is the profile's own temperature moved toward the ground's by exactly the knob times the weight the column itself supplies; where a world authors none, the air keeps the profile even beside a ground hundreds of kelvin away.
KIND invariant
ARGS --system res/systems/ksp_system.json --timeout 5 --solar-log --startship v,res/ships/skiff.json,Kerbin,flying-cruise
# #724 rung D. Kerbin is one of the two worlds utils/gen_systems.py prices a coupling for
# (KSP_AIR_FOLLOW; the value 0.350 is INVENTED -- no source states how far an air follows its
# ground -- and the generator prints that word rather than dressing it in an analogy). Four of
# this row's columns are one law, not four readings:
#
#   air_T = prof_T + follow * w * (underfoot_T - prof_T)
#
# air_T, prof_T and w are all MEASURED at the hull -- src/tick.cpp prints the aero pass's own
# lastAirT, lastAirProfileT and lastAirP, the same numbers its Mach number divides by -- so the
# residual below is print rounding and nothing else. It is NOT symmetric rounding on one number:
# the closure multiplies a ~49 K span (underfoot - prof) by follow (3 dp) and w (4 dp), so the
# 0.005 K each column's last digit hides arrives at the product several times over. Measured
# worst row this run: +0.0101 K, and the tolerance is 0.02 for that reason -- the case that
# pinned 0.01 went red on the rounding it had always contained, not on a change in the model.
# A recomputed prof_T would have been a second authority at a later instant than the flight
# model used, which is how #687 gets broken quietly.
#
# A cruise bed at ~8.5 km is where this is worth pinning: w = 0.33 there, so a coupling that
# ignored the column is measurably wrong. On a pad w = 0.97 and the two are nearly identical --
# which is why the bed in ARGS is not a pad.
#
# 1. THE COUPLING IS LIVE AND POINTS THE RIGHT WAY. The ground at this local time is warmer
#    than the profile, and the air sits between the two -- not at either end.
CHECK all(d["air_T"] is not None and d["prof_T"] is not None and d["w"] is not None for d in solar)
CHECK all(d["prof_T"] < d["air_T"] < d["underfoot_T"] for d in solar if d["underfoot_T"] > d["prof_T"])
CHECK all(d["air_T"] - d["prof_T"] > 1.0 for d in solar)
# 2. THE IDENTITY, closed on the row's own columns -- including `follow`, which the log prints
#    rather than this file retyping. A hardcoded 0.350 here would be a third spelling of a
#    number ground_model.py and ksp_system.json already state (#636). The band is 0.02 K: the
#    rounding envelope of the row's own four printed columns is about 0.010 K (measured worst
#    +0.0101), and the coupling this pins is 5.7 K here, so a closure that dropped w, or used the
#    anchor instead of the underfoot reading, misses by hundreds of times the band.
CHECK all(abs(d["air_T"] - (d["prof_T"] + d["follow"] * d["w"] * (d["underfoot_T"] - d["prof_T"]))) < 0.02 for d in solar)
# 3. ...AND THE KNOB IS THE FILE'S, not the model's opinion: 0.350 is what gen_systems writes.
#    Moving it in the generator reddens THIS line and asks for a reason.
CHECK all(d["follow"] is not None and abs(d["follow"] - 0.350) < 1e-9 for d in solar)
# 4. THE COLUMN DECIDES THE WEIGHT. 8.5 km up, a third of the sea-level air is overhead, so a
#    third of the coupling applies. Ignoring w -- coupling at full strength anywhere in the air
#    -- reddens check 2 while printing an unchanged w; a coupling that stopped at the datum
#    would answer 0.35 at a pad and reddens here.
CHECK all(0.20 < d["w"] < 0.45 for d in solar)
CHECK all(d["air_T"] - d["prof_T"] < d["follow"] * (d["underfoot_T"] - d["prof_T"]) + 0.01 for d in solar)
# 5. THE GROUND UNDER THE HULL STILL ANSWERS TO ITS OWN KNOB, unchanged by the air under it.
#    Its anchor is an AUTHORED reading (Kerbin states 288.15 K), never this coupling's output:
#    an air warmed by a ground whose anchor is that air's own datum would define its
#    temperature in terms of itself -- the loop #724's review flagged, and the reason rung D
#    reads the anchor off authored numbers only.
CHECK all(abs(d["ground_T"] - 288.15) < 0.01 for d in solar)
CHECK all(abs((d["underfoot_T"] / 288.15) ** 4 - (0.9323 + (1.0 - 0.9323) * 4.0 * max(0.0, d["flat"] / d["flux"]))) < 0.02 for d in solar if d["flux"])
FORBID air_T=-
FORBID prof_T=-
FORBID follow=-
