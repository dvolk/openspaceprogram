KIND invariant
# #667: the other half of the air. Same stick as the barometer, same worth, the
# family table saying "temperature" instead of "pressure" -- which is what decides
# the row its window shows and the unit its finding prints, so the part JSON and the
# family JSON are the whole feature and no code knows the part's name.
# The number is cross-checked against the PROFILE --solar-log prints (prof_T=, "the air
# temperature before any ground coupling" -- vehicle.cpp). Kerbin wears a layer table (#686),
# so the read is at THIS hull's altitude and is not a constant of the world -- which is why the
# pin is an agreement to 0.11 K rather than a literal temperature.
#
# WHY IT MOVED OFF --drag-log's T= column (#783). At this 8.5 km spawn the pod reads 233.9 K,
# prof_T says 233.95, and the [drag] row says T=239.8 a=310.6 -- 5.8 K up, while its own
# p=33774 and rho=0.50278 imply 234.0 K. The drag row's T/a are the RUNG D coupled air (follow
# 0.35, pressure weight 0.333 at this height), the pod and prof_T are the profile, and the row
# therefore describes two airs at once: the force comes from the profile's rho and the Mach is
# divided by the coupled a. #783 is open on which side moves; this case stays on the profile
# because that is the seam Vehicle::airReadingAt -- the gauge and the finding -- actually reads.
# Whichever way the model resolves it, the pod and the drag log currently disagree by 5.8 K over
# the same hull at the same instant, and that split is what #783 owes the player.
