# A fleet of two: the take/store dance must pair the instrument with a courier
# standing beside it, not the first of each in vehicle order. Here a second ship
# sits AHEAD of the flown one in the vehicle list, so the first capsule in list
# order belongs to a ship nobody is standing beside. The old hook order ("first
# container, then demand reach") reported "no kerbal in reach" and blamed the
# courier for the container's bad choice.
# Driven through the buttons now, the pairing is visible in their labels: the pod
# offers "Take  (to Vela)" and the capsule offers "Store  (into capsule)##store_Vela",
# both under labpod's own windows. The racer on the pad has no window open, and every
# path here is qualified by window, so a bare label cannot quietly satisfy the other
# ship.
NAME courier-pairs-across-the-fleet
CLAIM A courier pairs with the instrument beside it, not the fleet's first.
KIND golden
ARGS --time-accel 1 --timeout 9 --frame-cap 30 --width 1920 --height 1080
ARGS --startship extra,res/ships/racer.json,Kerbin,pad
ARGS --startship labpod,res/ships/labpod.json,Kerbin,rot-orbit
# Fly the second ship: the pod and the crew that do the dance are on labpod.
ARGS --sim-press 1000,50,F6
# F6 frames labpod from the default orbit angle, where the pod sits behind the
# capsule: the aim refuses honestly, so the case orbits first -- exactly what a
# player does -- and then the pod is clickable.
ARGS --sim-mouse 1500,1000,960,300,RMB
ARGS --sim-mouse 2500,0,960,620,0
ARGS --ui-focus 3000,labpod/materials_pod_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 3100,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-click 3400,"labpod > Materials Pod (2m)##1/Run Experiment##pod_run"
ARGS --ui-focus 3700,labpod/capsule_1
ARGS --sim-mouse 3800,0,CUR,CUR,RMB
ARGS --ui-click 4100,"labpod > Capsule (2m)##0/EVA##eva0"
ARGS --ui-click 4600,"labpod > Materials Pod (2m)##1/Take  (to Vela)##pod_take"
ARGS --ui-click 5100,"labpod > Capsule (2m)##0/Store  (into capsule)##store_Vela"
EXPECT EVA: 'Vela' out of 'labpod' part 0
EXPECT moved 'Low orbit materials study on Kerbin' Materials Pod (2m) -> Staff
EXPECT moved 'Low orbit materials study on Kerbin' Staff -> Capsule (2m)
# Exactly two transfers: the take and the store. A third would mean a pairing
# it should not have made.
CHECK out.count(" moved '") == 2
EXPECT ship=labpod part=1 (materials_pod)
EXPECT ship=labpod part=0 (capsule)
# The Take label names the courier, so the pairing is asserted by name. Which
# kerbal that is follows the roster's row order (mintCrewPool pushes suits in
# res/data/kerbals.json order, takeCrew pops the front, and --startship order
# decides who fills which capsule): 'extra' takes Ash, labpod takes Vela. If
# that shifts, the click path stops matching and FORBID FAILED catches it.
FORBID FAILED
FORBID ship=extra
FORBID error:
