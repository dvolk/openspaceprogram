NAME 829-b-world-nobody-gave-a-lag-still-answers-this-instant
CLAIM A world with no phase lag authored answers its ground from this instant's cosine to the last printed digit, in the flight log and in the air under a hull alike, so making the lagged readers agree is a branch and not a change to every world.
KIND invariant
ARGS --system res/systems/lag_playground.json --timeout 5 --solar-log --startship v,res/ships/skiff.json,Balmy,flying-cruise --time-accel 10
# #829's control. Balmy is Sultry's twin: same 240 K anchor, same 0.05 even share, same 0.350
# air_follow, same flat 210 K profile, same 600 km radius, same mass, same spin, same orbital rate.
# What it does NOT carry is `lag_tau` -- the SENTINEL rather than a 0.0 someone wrote (authored.h)
# -- and it rides the rail flat where Sultry's is inclined, which is what makes Sultry's closure a
# frame pin and this one a plain control. Absence has to keep answering the instantaneous law,
# because that is what every world in the shipped corpus does today and what every flight golden in
# this repo was measured against.
#
# Both readers now branch on lagTauActive(): an authored lag goes through groundtemp.h's
# groundForcingLagged(), anything else keeps the `SkyAtZenith::cos_inc` it already measured, bit
# for bit. This case is the half of the pair that says the branch did not leak.
#
# The window is an afternoon -- the sun sets through 57.1 -> 56.6 deg -- the mirror of Sultry's
# rising one, and it puts the ground at the top of its swing rather than near a terminator.
#
# 1. THE LOG'S AIR COLUMNS CLOSE ON THE LOG'S GROUND COLUMN, as they do for every body with a
#    follow knob (worst residual here 0.0106 K; band 0.02 like science/724's). The band is print
#    rounding on air_T and prof_T at %.2f -- follow (0.350) and w print exactly for these values.
CHECK len(solar) >= 4
CHECK all(abs(d["air_T"] - (d["prof_T"] + d["follow"] * d["w"] * (d["underfoot_T"] - d["prof_T"]))) < 0.02 for d in solar)
# 2. THE GROUND IS THE LAW AT THIS INSTANT, not a row solved over a rotation: 240 * (f + (1-f)*4
#    * cos_inc)^0.25 with cos_inc taken from this row's own flat/flux. Measured |difference| 0.00
#    K on all five rows -- the two printed columns agree to their last digit.
CHECK all(abs(d["underfoot_T"] - 240.0 * (0.05 + 0.95 * 4.0 * max(0.0, d["flat"] / d["flux"])) ** 0.25) < 0.02 for d in solar)
# 3. THE SUN IS SETTING ACROSS THE WINDOW, so this is an afternoon reading and not a dawn one.
CHECK all(b["elev"] < a["elev"] for a, b in zip(solar, solar[1:]))
CHECK all(d["elev"] > 50.0 for d in solar)
# 4. THE COUPLING RUNS AT THE FULL STRENGTH THE LIGHT PRICES: 12.60..13.62 K here. Sultry's same
#    instrument reads 7.7..8.4 K, because its ground has not caught up with its morning sun. The
#    pair of numbers is the point -- a world that stopped answering its light would have to say so
#    somewhere, and this is where it says it does not.
CHECK all(12.0 < d["air_T"] - d["prof_T"] < 14.0 for d in solar)
# 5. THE OTHER HALF OF #837 IS THIS DASH. Sultry's rows print lag=1.0000 and Balmy's print
#    lag=-, so the log answers "is this ground lagged?" itself rather than leaving a reader to
#    infer it from why the numbers came out different. A 0.0000 here would be a CLAIM -- this
#    world's ground peaks at noon -- that its file never made (#727, and authored.h's sentinel).
CHECK all(d["lag"] is None for d in solar)
FORBID lag=0.0000
FORBID air_T=-
FORBID underfoot_T=-
FORBID follow=-
FORBID prof_T=-
FORBID error:
