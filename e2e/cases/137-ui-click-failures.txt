# --ui-click must fail loudly, not click something else. Two of the ways a
# path is wrong: no such widget, and a label that several widgets share (the
# Controls window's rebind buttons all render as "rebind"). Pins that the
# failure is reported rather than resolved to an arbitrary match -- the whole
# point of naming widgets instead of aiming at pixels.
NAME ui-click-failures
ARGS --timeout 6 --ui-click 1500,"Title Menu/Controls" --ui-click 2500,"Controls/rebind" --ui-click 2500,"Title Menu/No Such Button"
EXPECT path="Controls/rebind" FAILED ambiguous
EXPECT path="Title Menu/No Such Button" FAILED not found
FORBID FAILED MISMATCH
FORBID error:
