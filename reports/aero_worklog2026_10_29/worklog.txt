WHAT STAGE 1 REVEALED (read this before choosing the next block)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The balloon was not only imaginary drag; applied at the area-weighted CP it was
pitch damping the airframes leaned on. With the honest silhouette a held stick
puts the JET 75 deg off the flow and it is still oscillating when the case ends
(AoA 0, 0, 0, 72, 4, 51, 28, -73, -2, 76, 25, -62, -60, 24 -- two runs agreeing to
under a degree). It does NOT loop over: the wings' own planform drag, 35 m2 when
the ship turns, damps that much. The GLIDER, which has no pitch surface at all,
does go past vertical and stay (|AoA| 169-170 deg, |Beta| to 165).
So #454 (static margin / pitch damping) is still the most VISIBLE gap -- nothing
decides where these airframes want to point, and the damping they have is blunt
planform drag that costs 35 m2 to get and is not aimed at the flow. (My first
claim, written on #454 before the mount-offset fix, said both airframes tumble
end over end; that was the bug talking -- 10 m2 of broadside instead of 35 -- and
the ticket now carries the corrected numbers.)
Measured run-to-run spread of the post-input flight (0.28 g vs 0.96 g of
weight carried) is #472, not physics: the sim steps against wall-clock frames
and tick.cpp discards the leftover, so any long under-damped flight is
unreproducible. That is what forced the two 458 goldens to keep shape pins
(stable to 0.01 m2) and loosen or drop the peak-lift bands.

WHY THIS ORDER (three things the tickets do not say out loud)
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. #462 and #463 are the SAME number. Both complain that two 0.4 m^2 plates add
   12.2 m^2 of drag silhouette. #463's fix (union of the per-part 2D projections
   instead of convex-hulling the merged vertices) removes the wingtip blob for
   everything; #462's "keep lifting parts out of the hull" is then largely
   redundant. The per-part projections are ALREADY computed every substep at
   vehicle.cpp:2259 for the centre-of-pressure / Cd blend and thrown away for the
   force, so the union reuses work already in hand.
2. #457 is a prerequisite, not a sibling. The wing's radius is 2.0 because for a
   plate the radius is half the SPAN while the plate is 0.1 m thick. Any span /
   aspect ratio for #462's induced drag minted from `radius` inherits that
   cylinder-shaped lie, and #453's wing-mounted surfaces float off. gen_parts.py
   :298 mesh_dims already computes extents and planform and never emits them --
   one field serves #457 (attach), #462 (AR) and #463 (silhouette). Commented on
   #457 on 2026_10_28.
3. #399/#403/#425 are cited as prerequisites by #452 and #454, backwards. There
   is no trim state to tune an attitude allocator against until the airframe has
   static margin (#454) and a real L/D (#462/#463).
