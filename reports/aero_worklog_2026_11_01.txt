THE LAW AS IT STANDS
 - Lift: per part, in the part's own mount frame (#453), feeling v + w x r and its
   own q at that point (#483; drag.h::localFlow is the ONE composition -- do not
   re-inline it). Slope 6.0/rad, liftCurve zero past 2*stallAngle, droop from
   stallAngle (20.1 deg on the shipped wing).
 - Induced drag: per part, charged on that part's own AR from its extents box
   (#462). A split pair pays its own AR, not the system's (see #462 follow-up).
 - Hull drag: the UNION projected silhouette (#463, 31x31 5 deg cache over +-75)
   times the area-weighted mean part cd, applied at the silhouette cp and feeling
   the flow THERE via localFlow (#486). --drag-cd (1.2) scales the hull's air AND
   both induced terms (the lifting part's #462, the deflected surface's #494 -- so
   it is partly a steering-authority knob, noted on #502); it deliberately does not
   scale a deployed canopy.
 - Control surfaces: DEFLECTION ONLY, and since #494 they PAY for it. No alpha term
   and no surface pitching moment; a surface with zero commanded deflection
   contributes nothing (vehicle.cpp's sv == 0 early-out). Consequence, measured:
   with everything symmetric, ALPHA = 0 IS THE ONLY PITCH EQUILIBRIUM. A stable
   airframe needs an aft download, which means tail incidence: shipdef "roll" on a
   surface mount IS incidence (a plate's chord is its Z axis; span +X, normal +Y),
   and the twin at angle 180 needs the OPPOSITE sign. Per-instance TRIM exists
   (#492): shipdef "trim" / save "trim" / the part window's slider, joined with
   the stick inside the part's own travel (drag.h::controlCommandFrac) and signed
   per instance by controlDeflectionSign, so a mirrored pair takes the SAME
   authored number and still torques the same way. Two law terms now:
   controlForce droops at the part's stall_angle (peak authority at ~15 deg, zero
   by 2x), and the deflection bills inducedCd on its OWN area and span.
 - Application points: lift and control at each part's own mount; hull drag at the
   silhouette cp. Since #512 the cp's ARM is each part's own projected-silhouette
   centroid (the component along the flow stays with the mount, because a projection
   cannot say where along its own normal the area sits), but the WEIGHTING is still the
   parts' summed areas while the force is charged on their union -- that is #514.
 - A non-spinning body is bit-identical to the pre-#483 law, which is what made the
   last several passes testable by row-diff.

VERIFICATION -- what can actually bite a change
 - make test: test_drag links drag.h and nothing else (cheap unit pins);
   test_shipload covers the def model and the shape fingerprint; test_save the save
   round-trip; test_cli flag spellings that error messages quote. NO test reaches
   applyAeroForce -- the 458 goldens plus flight/453-rudder-pair, flight/490-skiff,
   flight/485-per-surface-alpha and flight/elevator-trim are the only vehicle-level
   aero pins. A law change needs an e2e case, always.
 - e2e budget: 1-5 cases per ticket, never repeating the same ones; direct ./osp runs
   are free. Build 'bear -- make'; run the game with SDL_VIDEODRIVER=offscreen.
 - Scripted flights: --sim-press needs BOTH R (throttle up) and T (thrust latch)
   before a jet makes any force -- releaseControl() starts the throttle shut.
 - Reaching a PART WINDOW from a case (ui/492-part-window-trim is the template): orbit
   first with an RMB drag (--sim-mouse TIME,DUR,X,Y,BTN; the delta from the previous
   cursor position is the look, 200 px ~= 1 rad, so 960,540 -> 700,760 is yaw +1.3 /
   pitch +0.8), then --ui-focus MS,ship/partId, then --sim-mouse MS,0,CUR,CUR,RMB to
   pick at the cursor, then --ui-click MS,"<ship> > <Def label>##<partIdx>/<Label>".
   --ui-list MS dumps the live window+label+rect registry to write those paths from.
   A def's label is not its id: elevator_r0.8h0.7 is "Elevator (1.6m)".
 - [drag] log: |F| is the RESULTANT of lift+drag, not drag alone (at AoA 0 it equals
   q*drag_cd*cd*A; reconstruct drag along the flow as q*drag_cd*(cd*A + Induced)).
   There is no ship-wide q: q is per surface (#483), so one row's numbers do not
   multiply into a force. CpL is dashed when the pass had no usable flow.
 - [surface] row (#485): printed only for the ship being piloted, only while a
   readout is armed (the F4 overlay, --drag-log, or an open part window on a STEERING
   part -- #492), one part per lifting/steering
   part that had a usable frame -- a part with none is OMITTED, never quoted at 0, so
   a short row means surfaces went bad rather than went neutral. `a` is signed in
   SHIP axes (up for a wing/tailplane, right for a fin/rudder), not the mount's own;
   `q` is the pressure of the flow at that part, so it differs across a pair while
   the body rotates. run.py's `surface` list inherits `t` from the [drag] row above
   it and keeps the print order, which is the shipdef part order.
 - OSP_SHIP_DUMP=1: one-shot per-part dump (which vehicle/attitude is a lottery --
   the first Vehicle to reach the drag pass). SUM projA is the parts' areas ADDED; the
   force pays unionA. Multiplying SUM by the mean cd overstates a hull's CdA by up to
   2.4x on overlapping ships.
